## 1. The mandate

Carry the programme in [`../ENGINE-AND-PLATFORM-BRIEF.md`](../ENGINE-AND-PLATFORM-BRIEF.md)
(version 2, with the version 1 rules it keeps) to 100% delivery, in reviewable
checkpoints. At each checkpoint, report the figure that
`node scripts/programme-ledger.mjs --summary` computes from
[`acceptance-ledger.json`](acceptance-ledger.json).

- A unit is accepted only when its gate is met, with committed evidence and
  the release state the gate asks for. For an engine capability, that means
  the site vendors the released engine and production serves it.
- Blocked units stay in the denominator.
- Never reach 100% by weakening a test, dropping scope, hiding a failure or
  treating a plan as delivery. A change of scope needs a recorded decision in
  a `DECISIONS-*.md` file.

Keep downloads, calls, citations and search reach separate. They are outcomes
(§9), not delivery.

- Do not force-push shared history or overwrite unrelated changes.

**Licensing:**
- No Swiss Ephemeris code, data or output in engine source, coefficient

  analytics or identifiers.
- Share tokens are described truthfully.
- Test fixtures are synthetic, or a cited published example about a person

  conversation.

**Claims:**
- Every accuracy, time or privacy sentence on a public page has a record in
  `docs/claims/ledger.json`.
- Never claim scientifically validated astrology, a universal engine, or
  "more accurate than Swiss".
- No token, market or crypto language outside the wing.

**Research:**

- Budgets are whole-request.
- A sample is not a proof of completeness.

## 8. How the work gets through CI

- **The site's CI.** "Build & Check" takes about 40 minutes. The other jobs
  are the SQL, drive and drift jobs, "Build with production's feature flags",
  and "Swiss output removal and independent references". The Lighthouse gate
  retakes samples a runner stalled (F-51), up to three times, without
  changing the budgets.
- **Phase 1 captures.** Their receipt hashes `src/lib`, `src/components` and
  more. After any change there, or after merging `main`:
  1. rebuild;
  2. run `node tests/phase1-acceptance-drive.mjs`;
  3. run `scripts/phase1-acceptance-evidence.test.mjs`.

  Retake them last.
- **The scope allowance.** After merging `main`, re-pin `baseCommit` in
  `.github/phase1-scope-allowance.json` to it.
- **Independent references.** `docs/engine-validation/independent-references/`
  records the engine version. A new engine version means rebuilding them with
  `tools/build.py` (pyerfa) and updating the five SHA-256 pins in the tests.
  Site Check fails on any changed byte.
- **Engine-derived data.** Rebuild with the scripts the site's `CLAUDE.md`
  names: sky, ingresses, eclipses, transits, daily editions and events. CI's
  drift gates fail on a mismatch.
- **Budgets.** `scripts/report-bundles.mjs` holds the route budgets and the
  engine import allowlist. Do not raise a budget to pass without a recorded
  decision, as DECISIONS-2026-09-30.md §2 did.
- **Merges.** Merge commits only. A site PR is merged with the title
  "<PR title> (#N)" and the title as the body. An engine PR merges as "Merge
  pull request #N: <title>". The engine's `main` ruleset requires its twelve
  checks.
- **Engine release candidates.** Take the pattern from rc.15 and rc.16:
  1. the feature branches' own commits are cherry-picked onto `main`;
  2. the source commit;
  3. the carrier commit, with the packed archive under `artifacts/` and its
     SHA-256;
  4. the gate records;
  5. the archive-binding check;
  6. the birth-data history check;
  7. a PR.
- **Site adoption of an engine release.** Follow #603's pattern:
  1. vendor the archive;
  2. record parity in one process;
  3. give a cause for every difference;
  4. bring the MCP adapter to the new version, in two commits (build, then
     the `artifactCommit` pin);
  5. update the evidence, findings, claims and checks.

## 10. Checkpoint format

> **Overall delivery: N%**: accepted units over the fixed weighted total;
> blocked B%.
> **Completed:** what now works, with the repository and the PR, commit or
> deployment.
> **Verified:** the gates actually run and their evidence, with the important
> limits.
> **Audit/fixes:** findings closed, and material findings still open.
> **Next:** the next bounded task and its gate.
> **Owner action:** only a step that genuinely needs the owner, with its
> material prepared.
