# Life Chapters meanings and the Ask the Sky question filter: drafts (ZOD-151)

Drafts for two held ideas, ZOD-60 (Life Chapters) and ZOD-61 (Ask the Sky).
Nothing here is shipped. This folder holds drafts and their review only:
no page, no template, no filter code, no engine change, no fix for ZOD-62.

History:

- 12 to 13 November 2026: first version, by the builder session.
- 13 November 2026: `BREAKER.md`, the second session's review. No blocker, 15 should-fix items.
- 13 November 2026: **second version**, the one fix round Dias approved. `01`, `02`, `03`, `SOURCES.md` and this file were revised. `BREAKER.md` is unchanged and describes the first version.

## What the fix round changed

Dias's calls of 13 November 2026:

1. **Gentle love questions are allowed. Any love question about someone under 18 is refused.** `02`, section 3.1, and the minors step. What counts as gentle is the builder's definition and is listed for Dias.
2. **Immigration, legal and medical questions stay refused**, with a new rule ("judge the event behind the wording") and softened examples for the patterns that got past a reader: a permanent move abroad, a job question with an incident in it, a selection that turns on recovery. `02`, sections 2, 4.1 and 4.4.
3. **A job question that mentions another candidate or a salary, and a joint application with a partner, are allowed.** `02`, sections 2 and 3.
4. **The Valens IV.7 misreading is corrected (S1).** The passage is about the sign the chapter's ruling planet is in, not the chapter's own sign. The twelve "Under strain" lines are withdrawn and replaced by four lines keyed to the ruler's sign. `01`, Part 2b; `SOURCES.md`, V13.

## The source problem, in short

The Valens page linked on ZOD-60 could not be read: it returns a bot check
and no book text. The check was not worked around. The same translation was
read from the translator's own PDF, in the Wayback Machine's copy of
11 December 2025, and Dias approved that on 12 November 2026. No new source
was opened in the fix round.
