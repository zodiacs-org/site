Drafts for two held ideas, ZOD-60 (Life Chapters) and ZOD-61 (Ask the Sky).
Nothing here is shipped. This folder holds drafts and their review only:
no page, no template, no filter code, no engine change, no fix for ZOD-62.

## What the ZOD-220 round changed (fourth version)

Dias's calls of 13 November 2026, 18:09 Asia/Bangkok, and what was done
with each. All of it is in `02`, section 4.6, with the wording in section 6
and the open points in section 8.

| Call | What was done |
|---|---|
| 1. Applying to university is not an adult signal by itself. | An application, an offer or an admissions interview shows nothing either way. It does not answer a school sign or "my teacher". Being at university still shows an adult. |
| 2. Once a younger age has been given in a conversation, keep refusing for the rest of it, even if an adult age comes later. | A stated age under 18, or a school year no 18-year-old is in, holds for the conversation. Nothing lifts it: a later adult age, a birthday, a new question, a wrapper. 4.6 says what a conversation is (one chat thread, or one visit to the page) and what is remembered (a state per conversation, no age, nothing kept after it ends). |
| 3. A bare "college" should not refuse. "College" plus any other teen signal should. | "College" on its own shows nothing either way, in love questions too. Beside a sign it changes nothing, and it never shows an adult. |
| 4. AP classes are a high-school signal. | AP classes and AP exams are listed as signs. "Will I get into AP Chemistry this fall?" is refused. |

|---|---|

Added: AP, the PSAT, SAT and ACT, Nat 5s, predicted grades, results day, GCSE options, sets, a timetable, prefect, head boy and head girl, a student council, a report card, the honor roll, a yearbook, a school night, a youth team, words for being too young. |

| G7, the tie rule and parents | A parent is a sign only when the parent decides. A guarantor on a tenancy, a lift, a loan or an opinion is not a sign. A stated adult age or an adult's life answers a parent sign. |
