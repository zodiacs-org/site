# A straight answer about the sky.
Zodiacs.org is a free astrology reference built on one idea: the astronomy can be exact even when the interpretation is a tradition. Every chart here is computed from the real positions of the planets and kept on your own device.
## Company
Zodiacs.org is the official website of Zodiacs LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company formed on August 11, 2026. Zodiacs LLC owns and operates this website and develops Zodiacs: Birth Chart. Company, legal, press, and support inquiries may be sent to admin@zodiacs.org <mailto:admin@zodiacs.org>.
## Why it exists
Most astrology sites are either beautiful and vague or accurate and unreadable. The calculators that get the math right tend to look like tax software; the ones that feel good tend to hand-wave the numbers. We wanted a third option, a place where you can get your full birth chart in about twenty seconds, see the exact degrees behind every claim, and read what they mean in ordinary words.
The reading comes first. Exact degrees, dated sky facts, and sources stay close by for anyone who wants to check them, without turning every page into a technical report.
## Accuracy without the homework
When a date, degree, or birth time matters, it is calculated against the actual sky and checked before it appears. ## Your data stays yours
The engine runs in your browser, so chart calculation sends no birth fields to a chart API. Charts you save live on your device; if you sign in to a free account, they sync to it with their birth details. If you explicitly attach a chart to Guide, which is offered only in the encrypted sync preview and not on the public site, its placements-only summary, not the saved name or raw birth fields, is sent with your question to Anthropic for that conversation; its positions still give your birth date and time. There are no ads and no cross-site tracking. The full detail is on the privacy page </privacy/>.
## Four experiences under one roof
Most of the site is the astrology reference: guides, calculators, horoscopes, and the birthday pages. Alongside it lives Astrofolio </astrofolio/>, a collection view of the Twelve. The Zodiacs Registry </registry/> keeps their canonical records and addresses, while the Terminal </terminal/> presents a denser record of dated public activity. These sections stay separate from the free astrology surfaces by design.
Charts you save start on this device and sync only if you choose a Zodiacs.org account. Saved charts live in your profile and are never required to use the free tools.
The Registry’s operator and economic-interest attestations, the twelve dated origin receipts, and the establishment provenance are published on the disclosure page. Read the Registry disclosure </disclosure/>.
## How Zodiacs.org is maintained
Zodiacs.org is a Zodiacs LLC publication maintained by automated systems, without a traditional human editorial staff. Articles use the Zodiacs.org organizational byline; there is no invented editor, biography, or set of credentials behind it. The calculators remain free because the core math runs on your device.Open the publication detailsAutomation, checks, and public records
AI agents can propose writing, design, and code, but free-form model prose cannot publish itself. Daily editions begin with structured sky facts and versioned local templates. A separate astronomy audit recalculates the sky, while policy checks reject unsupported facts, unsafe certainty, and incomplete editions before publication.
The current daily edition exposes its facts, generation mode, policy version, hashes, and verification result in a public publication record </data/daily-publication.json>. The rulebook is machine-readable </data/editorial-constitution.json>, the daily method is documented </methodology/#daily-publication>, and the correction log </corrections/> records material changes.
## Get in touch
Corrections, questions, press, or a request about your data: write to admin@zodiacs.org <mailto:admin@zodiacs.org>. If you have spotted an astrological or astronomical error on a page, tell us which page; accuracy reports are the ones we answer first.
For a correction or removal request concerning a page in the People directory, write to people@zodiacs.org <mailto:people@zodiacs.org>.
