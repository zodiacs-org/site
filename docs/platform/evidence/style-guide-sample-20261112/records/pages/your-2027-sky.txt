## The 2027 sky everyone shares
Five eclipses, the retrogrades, and where the slow planets spend the year. These dates are the same for everyone; what they touch depends on the chart.
### Where the slow planets spend 2027
Jupiter: Leo until July 26, then Virgo.
Saturn: Aries all year.
Uranus: Gemini all year.
Neptune: Aries all year.
Pluto: Aquarius all year.
