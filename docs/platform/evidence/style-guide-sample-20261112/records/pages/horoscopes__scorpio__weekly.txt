## The choices and turning points shaping Scorpio’s week.
### The week beginsMoon in Scorpio, Nov 9
Follow the consequential thread without turning every silence into evidence. The week opens with your identity and how you present yourself, moves through your money, possessions, and priorities by Thursday, and closes on home, family, and your private life. Give identity and first moves the first concrete move instead of asking one choice to settle all three areas at once.
### Early weekMoon in Scorpio, Nov 9
Early in the week, turn toward your identity and how you present yourself: make your own position visible before reacting to the room, then decide what you want to begin, and what you are willing to be known for beginning. Keep the identity and first moves adjustment small enough to see what it changes before adding another.
### MidweekMoon in Sagittarius, Nov 12
By midweek, attention shifts to your money, possessions, and priorities: check the budget of time, attention, and material resources, then put a number beside the cost, the available capacity, and the value you expect in return.
### A turning pointMoon in Scorpio, Nov 9
At the exact contact, compare the move you intended with the one you are actually making. The New Moon in Scorpio brings your identity and how you present yourself into focus.
### The week closesMoon in Aquarius, Nov 15
The week closes with home, family, and your private life: protect the conditions that make home and private life workable, then before agreeing to another plan, check what you need at home and make time for it. For Scorpio, the final review is to ask what is known before assigning meaning to what is concealed.
