- **On page 13 Nov**: LIVE means the quoted wording is on the 13 Nov capture.
  GONE means it is not; the row is kept for the record, is not counted, and
  says what replaced it.
- **Verdict** (the ZOD-100 rule): **PASS**, the source opened supports the
  wording. **FAIL**, the source does not support it, a figure or date is
  wrong, a needed qualifier is missing, a public record contradicts it, or a
  figure shown as current is stale; every FAIL has a suggested fix.
  **NOT VERIFIED**, the cited source could not be opened or the check was not
  rerun; not a pass. **OPINION**, argument, forecast, hypothetical or labelled
  illustration; listed, not scored.

  `sources.md` and were opened on 13 Nov 2026 in the rerun (there is no R18
  or R19); R22 to R28 were opened on 13 Nov 2026 in the fix round.

  was fetched again and the quoted sentence or figure is still there.
