# Engine rc.14 site adoption evidence

This 2026-09-29 record prepares the site and its local MCP adapter for review
on the local branch `rc14-adoption`. It does not record a push, merge,
deployment or npm publication. Production serves rc.10
([its record](../site-engine-rc10/README.md)).

The site installs the exact
[zodiacs-engine-0.1.1-rc.14.tgz](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zodiacs-org/engine/b221534e75842c9d7e589c456ed57957844cd06b/artifacts/zodiacs-engine-0.1.1-rc.14.tgz),
SHA-256 `adc9805e22cd2468fa3340a864d9c53b36ff91e8f1592fdb35d8da8b69f4476e`,
87,415 bytes, 30 files. Its source is `zodiacs-org/engine` commit
`03db4bb602377896283775920519b26d1f19a890`, carried by
`b221534e75842c9d7e589c456ed57957844cd06b` and merged into engine `main` by
`8deda244dc87ccf5f68abba1f3173f142da40a2a` (engine PR #10). The archive and
its `.sha256` receipt were written from that repository's git objects at
`8deda244`, byte for byte (blob ids `6e5b44ae…` and `b77d92ee…`), and the
digest checked; the anonymous download above gave the same bytes.
`package-lock.json` was regenerated by `npm install` with npm 10.9.7 on Node
22.22.2, the major version CI uses: only the engine's entry changed (version,
`resolved`, the sha512 integrity of these bytes, the licence and the engines
range).

The branch starts from site `main` at `15949ec06390a3d4d0741ae64e6712e4dce4600d`,
the daily edition of 2026-09-29 on top of `145d36e3` (#597): the build's
freshness gate requires the day's edition. Every earlier engine and MCP

## What is adopted

- The engine's calculations for the site are rc.10's. rc.11 to rc.14 add
  configurable aspect policies and declinations with parallels (rc.11),
  secondary progressions (rc.12), exact configured-aspect and declination
  decisions (rc.13), and in rc.14 the Sun's out-of-bounds convention,
  `boundMarginArcsec`, `EPHEMERIS_SPAN` refusal outside Terrestrial Time
  0001-04-30 to 3998-09-03, `engines` `^20.19.0 || >=22.7.0` and the licence
  expression `MIT AND CC-BY-4.0`.
- The site takes its secondary progressions from the package.
  `src/lib/engine/progressions.ts` no longer holds a formula: it calls the
  package's `progressedInstant` and `progressedBodies` and keeps the site's
  body shape, without the package's `sign` and `degree` fields. ChartLens
  still loads it with `await import(...)`, so nothing about it is in
  `/birth-chart/`'s initial bundle. Nothing else in the site used the old
  code; its tests now run against the adapter unchanged.
- The site uses none of rc.11's aspect policies or declinations. Its forms
  still offer whole sign and Placidus.
- The MCP adapter is `0.1.0-rc.14`, bundling rc.14. Its three tools, their
  arguments and their answers are rc.10's apart from the engine version they
  report; it exposes none of the new APIs, and still not `chartPoints`.
- The receipt conventions remain rc.8's. Imported receipts remain claims;
  changing the engine version does not authenticate or reproduce old records.

## What changed that a reader can see, and why

The site's chart calculations give the same numbers as on rc.10 (next
section). The crossing solver behind its returns, void-of-course and event
searches is byte for byte rc.10's, and the build's gates that audit every
committed transit month and replay 30 days of daily editions against the
engine pass with the committed data unchanged. What changed:

- **The engine version** in every chart, receipt, scene and MCP answer, in the
  daily publication manifest and in the worked example on
  `/developers/engine/`: it names rc.14. In the scene snapshot and the
  example's output it is the only change. The manifest's generator digest
  changes as well, because it covers `package-lock.json`; the edition it
  describes is unchanged.
- **What the engine costs on `/developers/engine/`.** Bundled with esbuild
  0.28.1, `import { natalChart }` grew from 63.9 to 68.4 KB minified and from
  27.5 to 29.5 KB gzipped (the local-time and GeoNames rows by about the same
  amounts). About 3.5 KB of the minified growth is rc.11's aspect-policy
  code: the root entry creates `DEFAULT_ASPECT_POLICY` by calling
  `createAspectPolicy()` when it loads, and a bundler keeps a top-level call
  it cannot prove pure, so everything that importing from the root entry
  brings in now carries the policy validator. The rest, about 1.1 KB, is
  rc.13 and rc.14's checks in the shared chart chunk (the ephemeris span, the
  error wrapper). Imports from `@zodiacs/engine/internal`, which is what the
  site's own engine chunk uses, grew by 0.4 KB gzipped in the same
  measurement, and the site's engine chunk measures 26.7 KB of its 27 KB
  budget (26.1 KB on rc.10). The engine could mark that call pure or create
  the policy lazily; that is for the engine's next release, not this
  adoption.
- **The developer pages and `llms*.txt`** give the licence as
  `MIT AND CC-BY-4.0`, the Node range as `^20.19.0 || >=22.7.0`, and the
  refusal outside `EPHEMERIS_SPAN`: `/developers/engine/` had said the engine
  does not bound its input date at all, which rc.14 makes false. They list the
  new functions and the site's use of progressions. `/developers/mcp/` offers
  the `0.1.0-rc.14` archive, and the install block on `/developers/engine/`
  stops outside a project (F-04, below).
- **`/developers/conformance/`** shows the results the engine repository
  regenerated for rc.14 at `8deda244` (every verdict unchanged from rc.12's),
  and now says they are for the version the site's charts use.
- **The Phase 1 captures** were retaken, because their pinned template digest
  covers `package.json` and `package-lock.json`. The daily pages show the
  2026-09-29 edition where the committed captures showed 2026-09-28's, and
  Chromium 149.0.7827.55 took them where 153.0.8010.0 took the last ones. None
  of these pages shows the engine version.
- **An invalid date** passed to the engine or to the site's progressions now
  throws a `RangeError`; before, the engine threw a string and the old
  progression mapping returned an invalid date. No site path passes one.

## Newly executed checks

**The site's outputs on rc.10 and on rc.14** (`site-outputs.json`, by
`tools/compare-site-outputs.mjs`). The base commit's own `src/lib/engine/`
(read from git objects) was bundled against the rc.10 archive, this branch's
against rc.14, and both were called in one process with the same arguments
in the same order. The corpus is the one the rc.10 evidence used: every tenth
day from 1800-01-01 to 2199-12-31 at 12:00 UTC (14,610 instants), through
`computeBodies`, `bodyLongitude` and `longitudeSpeed` for all twelve bodies,
through `computeChart` in both of the site's house systems at the three
locations of the frozen node/polar corpus and three ordinary ones, and with no
birth time with and without a place; plus that frozen corpus itself. 572,767
calls; 33,069,705 values compared exactly (`Object.is`, dates by
milliseconds). 204,546 differ, every one of them a chart's `engineVersion`,
and 5 more are the invalid-date cases above. No position, speed, angle, cusp,
aspect, flag or ΔT differs.

**Progressions, old code against the package.** In the same run, the base
commit's `progressions.ts` on rc.10 against this branch's adapter on rc.14:
158 births drawn from the corpus (with varying times of day, plus a 1907
birth, a leap day, the epoch and the corpus ends), each with nine targets:
the birth itself, 1 ms, a day, a year, 29.5 and 90 years after it, half a
day and a year before it, and 2026-09-29. In all 1,422 cases the progressed
instant and all twelve rows are identical. Another 121 mappings across the
whole JavaScript Date range agree to the millisecond.
The engine's own check of its rc.12 adapter against the site at `f2bd0dd`
matched 1,020 of 1,020 rows; this one runs the site's adopted code.

**Node 22 and Node 24.** `node22-parity.json` and `node24-parity.json` come
from the unchanged `scripts/platform-engine-report.mjs` on Node 22.22.2 and
24.21.0. Both pass its frozen node and polar corpus with both house systems,
and every residual equals the other runtime's and rc.10's. This is not a
broad error bound.

**The packed archive in a clean consumer.** `public-candidate-consumer.log`
records `scripts/verify-packed-consumer.mjs` from the exact source commit,
run separately on Node 22.22.2 and 24.21.0 in a directory with nothing above
it: TypeScript 5.9.3 declarations and all 25 behaviour groups pass on each.
The engine's own evidence adds Node 20.19.0 and 22.7.0.

**Swiss refresh.** `swiss-refresh.json`: the unchanged every-tenth-day tools,
on the pinned binding and data files, 321,420 Swiss calculations, every one
answered from the `.se1` files. The engine's 14,610 input rows are byte for
byte rc.10's (the rc.10 dump regenerated here matches its recorded digest),
and all 1,914 reported statistical values equal rc.10's. Only statistics and
digests are committed; no Swiss code, data or per-instant value.

**MCP adapter `0.1.0-rc.14`.** The bundle was rebuilt by `npm run mcp:build`
and packed by `npm run mcp:pack`; `mcp:build:check` and `mcp:pack:check` pass.
The protocol drive passes 87 of 87, the synthetic benchmark 18 scenarios and

## Publication

The engine `evidenceCommit` in `src/data/platform-engine-candidate.json` and
the MCP `artifactCommit` in `public/examples/mcp-server.json` are the all-zero
placeholder, as they were in the rc.10 adoption before its carrier was
pushed: no local commit is public provenance. After the branch is pushed, a
second commit pins both to the pushed commit that carries this evidence and
the rc.14 MCP archive (`node scripts/pack-mcp-server.mjs --artifact-commit
<sha>`), as `e29d0dbc` did for rc.10. Until then `/developers/mcp/` would
offer a download that does not resolve, so the branch must not be deployed
before that commit.

## What this does not establish

- No merge, push, deployment or npm release.
- The site-output comparison is the site against itself on two archives of
  the same ephemeris family, not a measurement against a reference.
- No new measurement of the rc.11 to rc.14 APIs the site does not use; their
  evidence is the engine's (`docs/evidence/rc11-20260928/` to
  `rc14-20260928/` in its repository).
- The declination accuracy target the engine records as unmet stays unmet;
  nothing here uses declinations.
