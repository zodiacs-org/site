# The reviews of the F-88 corrections

The same two review passes as for F-86's wording ([reviews.md](reviews.md)),
read-only, each given the corrections sheet, real template output (with
receipts and dated forms) and the edited files. Their verdicts are kept as
delivered, apart from headings; the "reverted on disk" notes in them describe
the corrections being moved to their own branch while the reviews ran, not a
loss. What shipped is the "after" column with every CHANGE below applied.

## Spanish

| item | verdict | replacement | reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| ES-C1: articles | ACCEPT | | "El Sol entra…", "La Luna entra…", "con el Sol", "con la Luna" are correct, and the receipts rightly keep the bare names. |
| ES-C1: opposition | CHANGE | "El Sol en oposición a Neptuno…", "Marte en oposición al Sol…", "Venus en oposición a la Luna…" | "En oposición a" is the standard phrase in Spanish. "Con" isn't wrong, but it reads less naturally. Keep "con" for conjunción, cuadratura, trígono and sextil, and note the contraction "al Sol". The Portuguese template already does this. |
