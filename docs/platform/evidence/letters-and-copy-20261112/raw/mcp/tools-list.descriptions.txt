## find_birthplace (Find a birthplace)
Resolves a public town or city name only, so the chart tools can use its coordinates and time zone. Give the place name, and a country when several places share it. It does not take a street address. Results come from the GeoNames list of places of 15,000 people or more, bundled with Zodiacs, so the name is not sent anywhere else, and nothing is stored. Each place includes a region, a country, coordinates rounded to two decimals, an IANA time zone, and the UTC offset for an optional date (historical daylight saving included). Pass that latitude, longitude and time zone to get_natal_chart, get_personal_transits, get_compatibility or get_occasion_sky. Never guess a birth time from a place.
