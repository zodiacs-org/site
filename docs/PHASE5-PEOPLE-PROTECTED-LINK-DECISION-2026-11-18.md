## Controls that stay in force

The same controls as the 16 August 2026 decision (`PHASE5-PEOPLE-PROTECTED-LINK-DECISION-2026-08-16.md`) apply to every name here:

- Each person stays a protected living record: not indexable, `noindex, nofollow` on the page and its assets, outside the sitemap, the People directory, Chart Twins and the famous-chart AI tool.
- Each link carries `rel="nofollow"` and appears only on the sign page listed above.
- Charts are date-only from Wikidata, Wikipedia and Commons evidence. No birth time, rising sign or houses, and no Moon sign where the Moon changed signs that day.
- Portraits of living people are not shown.
- Copy is neutral and uses no pronouns for the subject.
- Every subject is an adult.

## Scope

This decision authorizes nothing else. It does not lift protection for any living person, add living people to any index, search surface or AI tool, or authorize links from any other page.
