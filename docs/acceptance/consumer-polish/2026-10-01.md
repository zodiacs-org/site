## Motion review

| Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Film automatically resumes | User Pause/Resume choice survives visibility changes and reload within the session | Keep deliberate control of ambient motion |
| Sculpture hover takes 900ms and moves 6px | 260ms ease-out and 3px; matching masked sheen | Respond promptly without changing layout |
| Shine and film can run outside the reading area | Shine requires active selection, viewport visibility and visible tab; film preserves existing viewport gates | Keep movement local to visible artwork |
| No direct pause affordance | 44px text action, hidden in reduced motion and after the Astrofolio hero | Keyboard and touch access without covering collection actions |

Verdict: restrained motion, transform/opacity only for the added collectible effects, no spring or layout animation. Source: `src/terminal/split-styles.css` (collectible-shine and fine-pointer hover), `src/app.jsx` (useCampaignFilm, CampaignHero, CampaignLook), and localized homepage hero playback handlers.
