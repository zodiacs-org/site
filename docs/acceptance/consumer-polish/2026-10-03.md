# Visual consistency sweep — 3 October 2026

The owner requested fixes for the Profile empty-state CTA, footer wordmark alignment and changing Astrofolio purchase-pill dimensions, plus a sweep for similar visual inconsistencies. This patch keeps the existing art direction, content and purchase-button design.

| Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Living Chart's empty-state paragraph touches Open Today | A 20px gap separates the paragraph and action | Make the next step distinct and comfortable to tap |
| Footer sign ring aligns with the text baseline | The ring and visible Garamond wordmark align optically | Keep the brand balanced across shared footer surfaces |
| Footer CSS is immutable for a year under an unchanged URL | All canonical footer references carry a new version | Returning visitors receive the alignment repair on reload |
| Astrofolio purchase pill sizes itself around the selected name and status | Stable grid columns, reserved status height and consistent outer dimensions | Sign switching and market loading do not move the purchase action |
| The picker caret wraps into a clipped line beside long names | Every name retains its visible picker caret | Keep the sign switch discoverable, including Sagittarius at 320px |
| The mobile Guide launcher sits nearly against the purchase pill | A clear gap separates the launcher from the stable pill | Keep both controls individually accessible |
| Gemini's article grid exceeds its column on a 320px phone | Explicit zero-minimum grid tracks constrain the article | Keep article headings and prose within the reading column |
| Russian tool heading clips on narrow phones | Long words may wrap and hyphenate | Preserve complete translated labels |
| Long translated saved-chart actions enlarge the Profile grid | Grid tracks can shrink; actions and long button labels wrap | Keep saved-chart actions and sign-in fields onscreen |

## Coverage

The audit samples 83 existing routes at 320px, 768px and 1440px, with four vertical positions per route. It includes all twelve English sign guides and Registry profiles; homepages and Profile in all released languages; horoscope periods, calculators, People, calendars, Learn, games, Astrofolio, Fomo, SDK and the technical wing. Three unsupported localized Today probes return the expected 404 and are excluded from the valid-route count. This is coverage of shared templates and representative routes, not a claim to manually inspect every generated page.

The dedicated regression exercises all twelve signs in loading, unavailable and quoted market states at 320, 360, 390, 430, 768 and 1440px in Chromium and at 390px in WebKit: 252 purchase-bar measurements. It also switches signs through the actual mobile picker, checks the Guide gap, the Profile CTA and footer alignment, and narrow translated controls in both engines. Market values and the saved Profile used by this driver are synthetic test fixtures only; no sample price is added to the public site. No email, purchase or account mutation is performed.

Results are recorded after the final build and browser checks. The shared footer remains a single canonical stylesheet, generated Registry HTML is regenerated through its builders, and the protected-scope gate retains an exact, base-pinned owner-authorized allowance. Existing motion, live-market provenance and reduced-motion behavior are preserved.
