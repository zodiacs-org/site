02 · Measurement

Market and transparency

Registry identity does not depend on price. When market or attention data appears, its source, capture time, and methodology sit beside it so readers can tell the public record from outside context.

Third-party figures may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. They never change whether an address appears in the Registry.

03 · Independent services

Access and third parties

Zodiacs.org provides a read-only reference. Wallets, explorers, marketplaces, bridges, and market-data services reached through external links operate independently and have their own prompts, terms, and risks.

Acquisition links open independently operated third-party venues. Check the destination, network, official mint, amount, and every wallet prompt before approving an action. A blockchain transaction cannot be reversed.

No purchase is required to explore the Registry or use Registry Collection. Never share a seed phrase or private key.

05 · Receipts

Safety and evidence

“Official” means that an asset address appears in this Registry. It is not government, regulator, wallet, or exchange approval, and it does not prove identity, control, legal ownership, safety, value, liquidity, or future performance.

Registry checks are read-only. They do not request signatures, approvals, payments, swaps, network changes, or transactions, and they do not prove identity, control, legal ownership, safety, value, or future performance.

Digital assets are speculative, volatile, and may be illiquid. Their value can fall to zero. Operator and economic-interest statements are published as dated operator attestations, not independently verified.

Dated disclosures and the public archive preserve origin claims, operator relationships, limitations, and changes to the record.
