What does “token account” mean?

Solana reports token accounts, not verified people. One person can use several accounts, and one account can represent a liquidity pool, bridge, exchange, or custodian. The chain does not reveal a real-world identity.

Percentages compare account balances with the recorded native Solana supply. The official Base version is bridged from Solana and is not counted a second time.

The story of AriesA short history

Recorded in the MUL.APIN star catalogues as LÚ.ḪUN.GÁ, the Hired Man of the spring fields.

Identified with Chrysomallos, the golden-fleeced ram of the Argonaut cycle, in the Hellenistic star-myths of Eratosthenes and Aratus.
