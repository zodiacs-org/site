How Terminal works

The division of labour is fixed, and it is the whole point.

The venue executes

Orders route to Jupiter, which builds the transaction, has your wallet approve and sign it, submits it, and charges the exact venue fee shown in the quote, capped here at 0.10%. Zodiacs.org holds no keys or funds, is never a counterparty, and receives nothing from any trade.

Indexes describe

Prices, charts, and the trade tape are read from public market indexes — Dex Screener and GeckoTerminal, without a wallet address or site-added visitor identifier in the request. The providers still receive ordinary web-request metadata. Independent third-party data, not a valuation or recommendation.

The registry verifies

Every mint traded here is read live from the public registry — the one answer to what a sign’s official record is. No verified mint, no trade.

Before anything is signed

Terminal operates no market: it presents a trade that an independent venue builds, executes, and charges for. Jupiter is an independent third-party. Its exact venue fee is reported with each trade-panel quote and may not exceed 0.10%. No referral, platform fee, or other compensation is configured for zodiacs.org.

Zodiac assets are thinly traded — pool depth is measured in thousands of dollars, not millions — and can lose all market value; anyone acquiring one could lose all money used to acquire a Zodiac. A trade is an onchain transaction that cannot be reversed. Price impact and the amount to be received are shown from the venue’s own quote before anything is signed. Verify the official mint, network, amount, and destination before approving anything in your wallet.

A wallet address is sent to the venue only when you choose to trade, never to show a price. Details: Privacy · Terms · Disclosure.
