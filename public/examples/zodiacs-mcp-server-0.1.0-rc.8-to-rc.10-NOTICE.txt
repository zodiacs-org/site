Each archive's package.json gives its licence as MIT, and none carries a
notice. MIT covers the adapter's own code. But the server.mjs in each archive
bundles @zodiacs/engine (0.1.1-rc.8, 0.1.1-rc.9 and 0.1.1-rc.10), and the
engine's ΔT module, inlined there, contains 32 values of Table S15, rounded to
0.01 s, and constants of equations (4.1) and (5.1) from:

  Stephenson, F. R., Morrison, L. V. & Hohenkerk, C. Y. 2016, Measurement of
  the Earth's rotation: 720 BC to AD 2015. Proc. R. Soc. A 472: 20160404.
  doi:10.1098/rspa.2016.0404
  Published under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0):
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  The values are used unmodified except for rounding; the engine rebuilds the
  authors' spline from them.

Those values are under CC BY 4.0, not MIT. If you redistribute one of these
archives or its server.mjs, keep this notice with it.

The engine's ΔT values from 1941 come from the United States Naval Observatory
(historic_deltat.data and tai-utc.dat, US Government works) and from the
International Earth Rotation and Reference Systems Service (IERS EOP 20 C04
and Bulletin A, finals2000A.all), which asks that its products be cited:
IERS, https://www.iers.org/.

Cross, under the MIT License; its permission notice is kept in server.mjs.
