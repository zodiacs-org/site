    • Readonlyarithmetic: "exact-binary64;reported-orb-rounded-half-even"

      Every input is the exact value of its double; separation, orb, motion, eligibility, matching and ordering are decided exactly, without a tolerance. The reported orb is the exact orb rounded to nearest, ties to even.
