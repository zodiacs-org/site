    Interface MinorBodyTable

    A minor body's table: how it is laid out (intervals uniform intervals of intervalDays, series of degree, coefficients in multiples of unitKm, int32 below k32, int16 below k8Start, int8 beyond), the span it covers, its fit's largest error at the vectors it was fitted to, km, and its bytes' SHA-256, as pack-data.ts gives it.
