Your preview account

This test environment has separate accounts from the main Zodiacs website.

Use a unique password of at least 12 characters when creating an account. Creating an account sends a confirmation email. Preview email delivery is limited; contact support if you cannot sign in.

Connect your application

The connected application receives your preview account ID and an access token that includes your email address.

  • Read computed public sky events.
  • Create, refresh and cancel watches you request.
  • Deliver those events to your connected ChatGPT account.

No birth details are needed for Sky Watch. Watches expire within seven days unless you refresh them. Disconnecting stops further deliveries.

Connected applications

    What is stored

    Your preview account, selected event filters, time zone, expiry and an encrypted delivery address. Public sky calculations include their method and engine version. Astrology interpretation is separate from the computed sky.

    This is a test service. Delivery timing and ChatGPT compatibility are still being verified. It is not a time-critical alert service.
