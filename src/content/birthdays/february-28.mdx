In three years out of four, this date closes February; leap
years append one more. The astrology underneath is steadier than
the calendar: the sign question has a single answer,
[Pisces](/pisces/), whatever the year. The finer question is the
interesting one. February 28 lies near the border between the
sign's Jupiter-ruled first decan and its Moon-ruled second, and
while most birth years keep it on Jupiter's side, the top of the
range crosses. So year and hour, not the date alone, pick the
ruler that tones your Sun.

## Two currents share the date

Jupiter's current runs outward: horizon-hungry, hopeful, built
for meaning. The Moon's runs homeward: protective, tidal, tuned
to the people within arm's reach. Most February 28 births
describe the confluence, a
[Pisces Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/) that dreams at
ocean scale and still counts heads before leaving the beach.
Whichever ruler your minute lands on, the other stays audible.

## Harbor-keeping, horizon-watching

In love this date reads need early and gives without ceremony;
what it wants back is a home that works like a harbor (safe,
warm, always open) and a partner who notices the tending while it's
happening, not at the retrospective. At work the intuition is
the résumé: nursing, hospitality, design research, editing,
teaching, trades where sensing the unstated is the core skill.
Environments that punish softness make this chart seasick.

## The smoothed-over sting

The friction: small hurts get smoothed over in real time
(optimism explains them, empathy excuses them) and the sting
files itself away unprocessed. A season later the archive floods
all at once, to everyone's surprise including yours. The work is
reporting the minor injury the week it happens, while it's still
minor and still easy to forgive.

If your birthday sits this close to a decan line, the settling
fact is the Sun's true degree, and that belongs to the clock
rather than the calendar. The
[birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) computes it, along with
the Moon, rising sign, and the rest of the chart no date alone
can see.
