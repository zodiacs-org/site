Rooms run warmer with a November 28 in them. This is
[Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) deep in its Jupiter decan, and the
planet's old signature (largeness, generosity, the bigger frame)
expresses here as morale: a built-in conviction that more is
possible than the current mood admits, transmitted to everyone
standing nearby whether they asked or not.

## Built-in morale

The optimism of a
[Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) born today
is neither naivety nor denial; it's a wider sample. This date has
watched enough situations turn around to expect the turnaround on
schedule, so setbacks get treated as middle chapters. The effect on
groups is observable: plans grow a size, people volunteer, the
meeting ends earlier and lighter than it began. None of it is
technique; the belief is structural.

## Warmth with an itinerary

In love this birthday gives by default (steadily, without keeping
count), and it courts with plans: the trip, the scheme, the standing
Sunday thing. The partner it keeps is the one who says yes often and
means it, and who can also decline a plan without dimming the
planner. At work the gift is ignition: starting things, gathering
believers around them, raising a team's sense of what's possible.
Point this date at beginnings and let someone else run
maintenance. Finishing bores it exactly as much as starting
delights it.

## The cost of yes

The friction is the calendar. Yes gets said in the mood of the
moment, and the cost comes due in a week that never had room for
it. Overcommitment here isn't a scheduling flaw, it's generosity
outrunning physics. The growth is subtraction before addition: one
thing declined, out loud, per week, and the slow discovery that
nothing catches fire from the declining.
