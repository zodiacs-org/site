Nearly every year on the table gives November 23 to
[Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) outright, nearly. In a scatter of
mid-century years the Sun's crossing out of [Scorpio](/scorpio/)
landed on the 23rd itself, which put early births in those years on
the Scorpion's side of the line. If you were born then, check the
table above before settling the question; everyone else reads on as
early Sagittarius with a Scorpio border at their back.

## The Archer, with a footnote

The first days of Sagittarius are the sign at bright voltage: the
[Sagittarius Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) candor,
humor, and horizon-hunger, not yet moderated by anything. The
footnote is the border. This close to Scorpio, the fire tends to
carry unusual depth. The joke lands, then the observation inside it
keeps unfolding for a day. Where mid-sign Sagittarius skims
brilliantly, a November 23 skims and then circles back, wanting to
know what was actually under the thing it just laughed at. That
return trip is the [Scorpio Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-scorpio/)
neighborhood showing through the fence.

## Fast warmth, deep keel

In love this date leads with openness (plans, honesty, laughter on
the first evening) while keeping a keel of seriousness that
surprises people who took the lightness at face value. It falls fast
but not shallow. At work the blend makes a natural communicator of
hard material: teaching, writing, advocacy, field science, anywhere
curiosity does the traveling and rigor does the packing.

## Motion as avoidance

The friction: when something genuinely hurts, the Archer reflex is
distance, the trip, the busy season, the philosophy that reframes
the wound as growth before it has even closed. The Scorpio border
knows better and quietly keeps a copy of the original feeling. The
growth is sitting still with that original, one honest hour at a
time, before converting it into wisdom for other people.

For most birth years the table above answers the sign question
without drama; for the handful of mid-century years that split, the
[birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) reads the Sun's exact
position from your hour and ends the debate.
