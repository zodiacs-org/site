The May 7 reputation, among people who know one, is usually some
version of: quiet, immovable, and then, out of nowhere, the
funniest line of the night. That's the second decan of
[Taurus](/taurus/) working as designed. Mercury runs this middle
stretch of the sign in the traditional system, and on Venus ground
the planet of language turns into timing.

## Deadpan on bedrock

Fixed earth doesn't perform, which is exactly why the humor lands:
no wind-up, no mugging, just an observation set down at the right
moment like a level shelf. The wit is a symptom of the deeper trait.
May 7 people watch carefully and remember everything, and the
[Taurus Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/) beneath it holds
opinions the way masonry holds heat. Slow to warm, slower to cool.
The material never runs out, because the watching never stops.

## Steady company, better banter

Romantically this date offers a rare combination: reliability that
isn't boring. The love is expressed in attendance (being there, on
time, for years) plus the driest running commentary in the
building. What it struggles to express is need; a May 7 Taurus would
rather be the rock than admit to erosion. Careers thrive where
composure and observation both pay: negotiation, teaching,
client work that rewards the unflappable, crafts with an audience.
The trick colleagues never quite learn is that the silence was the
first draft of the joke.

## The punchline as a fence

The friction: humor makes an excellent wall. Deflecting with a joke
feels like grace, and after a decade it functions like distance;
partners can know every one of this birthday's bits and none of its
fears. The growth edge is letting a serious sentence stand unrescued
now and then. The people who love you can take it. That's mostly
what they're for.

A voice this settled still needs its rooms measured. Which halls carry it (work, home, the small circle) is acoustics for the birth hour, and [the chart](/birth-chart/) runs the sound check.
