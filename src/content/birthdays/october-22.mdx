Most tables call October 22 plain Libra and move on. They're nearly
right (in almost every year since 1940 the whole day belongs to
[Libra](/libra/)), but not entirely: a handful of years put the Sun's
crossing into [Scorpio](/scorpio/) late on this very date, and a
birth after the crossing lands in the new sign. The table above
carries the year-by-year answer; for the few split years, the hour
decides.

## Nearly always the Scales

The likely story is late-degree Libra, the sign complete, socially
masterful, precise. But this date lives close enough to the border
that many October 22s recognize the neighbor's furniture: a privacy
under the poise, an intensity of attachment the
[Libra Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-libra/) page doesn't quite
explain. If your year is a split one and your birth ran late, read
the [Scorpio Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-scorpio/) page as the
primary text instead.

## The blend at close range

Whichever side of the line, the mix behaves consistently: this
birthday partners like an air sign (talk, fairness, form) and
attaches like a water one, quietly and completely. Work rewards the
combination. Negotiation with staying power, counsel that keeps
confidences, any role where charm opens the door and discretion
holds the room once inside it.

## Poise over a live wire

The friction: the surface stays agreeable while the lower floors file
everything, and the two layers can go years without exchanging notes.
The growth is letting them meet, saying the deep thing in the polite
room, at moderate volume, before it has to arrive some louder way.

Whichever answer you're hoping for, don't guess. Your year is in the
table above, and if it's a split one, the
[birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) computes the Sun's exact
degree for your birth minute and closes the question.
