Nine days into Taurus season, Venus has finished furnishing the
place. April 29 is the last date to sit entirely inside the sign's
first decan, Venus ruling both sign and stretch, and it receives
the system's finest instrument: discernment. Not appetite, which
came earlier in the decan, but judgment about appetite, the
difference between wanting things and knowing which ones to want.

## The connoisseur's build

The April 29 [Taurus Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/) knows
the difference: between the almost-right fabric and the right one,
the pleasant evening and the perfect one, the person who means it
and the person performing it. This is [Taurus](/taurus/) refined
into a precision tool, fewer things wanted, each chosen exactly.
Around an April 29 you notice your own standards rising; theirs
are quietly persuasive. Gift-giving becomes an unfair contest; so
does grocery shopping.

## Choosing well, in both senses

In romance, this date moves slowly and chooses rarely; being
selected by an April 29 is a credential. Partners get the full
Venus treatment, beauty attended to, comfort engineered, quality
time that is actually both. At work, the discernment is the
career: curation, editing, sourcing, tasting, review of any kind,
any role where someone must say "this one, not that one" and be
right. April 29 is the person whose recommendations you stop
double-checking; the skill scales from wardrobes to hires to
houses without changing shape.

## When standards become walls

The friction: taste this sure can quietly turn into a fortress.
People, plans, and places that arrive unpolished get declined at
the door, and some of the best things arrive unpolished. The
growth edge is remembering that everything April 29 now loves was
once new to it, and someone let it in on potential. Extend the
same visa occasionally.

refinement's arena (craft, home, art, people) is set by houses
