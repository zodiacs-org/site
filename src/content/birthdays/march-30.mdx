The line between Aries' first and second decans runs straight through
March 30. Tradition gives the opening stretch to Mars and the next to
the Sun, and which side of that line your birthday actually occupies
depends on the year and hour you were born. This date straddles it.

## Spark or steady flame

On the Mars side, the [Aries Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-aries/) is
pure ignition: fast, blunt, first, gone. On the Sun's side the fire
changes character, steadier, warmer, more aware of its audience,
burning to sustain rather than to start. March 30 people usually
report the mixture: a sprinter's reflexes attached to a performer's
follow-through, impulse that somehow remembers to take a bow. Which
trait dominates can literally come down to your birth minute, which
is a satisfying thing to be able to check.

## Pursuit in two styles

In love the Mars strain chases and the Sun strain courts. One moves
in a straight line at speed, the other stages the whole production,
lighting included. A March 30 typically does both: the blunt opening
question, then, unexpectedly, the anniversary remembered with
theater. At work the blend suits arenas that need both launch and
reign, founding a thing and then actually fronting it, opening the
restaurant and hosting its floor, starting the practice and keeping
its patients for decades.

## Pride and impatience, twin risks

Each decan ships its hazard. Mars-side, it's impatience, the fuse,
the leap, the bruise. Sun-side, it's pride, the need to be seen
winning, the difficulty of apologizing while lit. On a hinge date
both are worth watching, since either can take the front on a given
day. The common cure is cheaper than it sounds: one breath between
impulse and act, one between slight and reply.
