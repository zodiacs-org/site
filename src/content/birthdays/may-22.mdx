The ambiguity is behind you: whatever the year, by May 22 the Sun
sits settled inside [Gemini](/gemini/), parked in the sign's opening
degrees. First-days birthdays get their sign the way spring gets its
first hot day, suddenly, totally, and a little loudly. This is
Gemini before anything files down the edges.

## The sign at first light

Opening-decan Gemini runs on Mercury twice over (the sign's ruler
and, in the traditional count, this decan's too), so the classic
traits arrive undiluted: the talking-to-think, the six simultaneous
interests, the stranger befriended by baggage claim. What the early
degrees add is urgency. A May 22 doesn't drift toward the new; it
lunges, with the enthusiasm of a sign that just got the keys. The
[Gemini Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-gemini/) runs on input the way
engines run on air, and here the intake is wide open.

## First to text back

Love with this birthday starts as banter and stays as banter. The
relationship is the conversation, and silence is the only real
infidelity. Partners get someone endlessly interested, quick to
laugh, and constitutionally incapable of the sulk that lasts a week.
What wears the bond is repetition without novelty. At work, early
Gemini shines at starts: pitches, launches, first drafts, new beats.
Pair this date with a finisher and the output doubles.

## The starter's tax

The friction is the second week. Novelty carries a May 22 through
any beginning; the middle, where things turn repetitive and real, is
where projects and even friendships quietly get replaced by fresher
ones. The growth edge is choosing a small number of things that get
finished on principle. Depth isn't the enemy of range. It's what
makes the range add up to something.

The date hands you the Sun's sign and nothing more. Whether Gemini
fronts your whole personality or hums in the background depends on
the Moon and rising sign, which come from your exact hour and place.
The [birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) figures them
precisely.
