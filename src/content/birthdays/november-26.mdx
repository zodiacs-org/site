Distance is a nutrient for people born November 26. Not escape,
range: the unvisited country, the unread shelf, the field nobody at
the current table can pronounce. The Sun here stands mid-first-decan
in [Sagittarius](/sagittarius/), Jupiter ruling its own ground, and
appetite for scale is this birthday's baseline setting rather than a
phase anyone should wait out.

## Fed by horizons

A [Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) this
undiluted measures aliveness in new ground gained. Some of it is
literal (this date collects places the way others collect
furniture), but the deeper habit is mental: a new framework every
season, tried on, walked around in, kept or returned. The optimism
people notice is downstream of all that intake; when you've seen
this many ways to live, dead ends stop looking permanent, because
there is always another route on file.

## Travel companions wanted

The relationship brief is honest and specific: a fellow traveler.
Not necessarily a co-passenger on every flight, a partner with a
horizon of their own, so that neither becomes the other's anchor or
warden. Given that, this date is generous, funny, and far more loyal
than its restless reputation suggests. Work needs the same oxygen:
field roles, international anything, research, teaching that
travels. The dangerous job is the comfortable one, pleasant,
static, and slowly airless.

## Routine is not a cage

The friction is the diagnosis reflex: mistaking ordinary routine for
confinement and prescribing departure. Some depths only open to
people who stay: the craft after year five, the friendship after
the tenth winter, the marriage after the boring stretch turns out to
be load-bearing. The growth is redrawing the map: five years into
one craft is also somewhere, and no tourist ever gets there.
