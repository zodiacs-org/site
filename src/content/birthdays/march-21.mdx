Tradition calls March 21 the first day of [Aries](/aries/), and
tradition is usually right, but only usually. In roughly seven years
out of ten since 1940, the Sun has already crossed the equinox point
before this date begins, and the whole day is Aries. In the other
three, the crossing happens during March 21 itself: a dawn birth in
those years is still [Pisces](/pisces/), an evening one Aries. The
year table above says which kind of March 21 was yours; no year here
ends still in Pisces.

## Two signs claim the day

Even confirmed-Aries March 21s tend to carry a watery afterglow,
born with the Sun barely a step into the new sign, the old one still
visible behind it. The
[Aries Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-aries/) supplies the essentials:
directness, appetite for beginnings, honesty at speed. The
[Pisces Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/), where the table
grants it, softens the edges: intuition ahead of action, a gentler
read on people. Either way, this birthday usually presents as fire
with unusually good hearing.

## Courtship and career on the boundary

In love the boundary blend moves first and feels fast: attraction
acted on quickly, but with more listening than Aries alone tends to
budget. Partners report someone direct about wants yet oddly gentle
in conflict, the spear carried, rarely thrown. At work this date
starts things that need sensitivity to survive: new teams, first
hires, launches where trust is the product. It outperforms in rooms
where pure aggression fails and pure patience stalls.

## The shared blind spot

Both signs at this boundary struggle with middles. Pisces drifts past
them; Aries sprints past them. So the March 21 pattern, whichever
column of the table holds your year, is brilliant openings and vague
second acts: relationships, projects, regimens, all superbly begun.
The growth is unglamorous: pick one begun thing per season and carry
it, visibly, to the boring finish.
