Fixed fire is the kind that holds its temperature, and July 29 runs
on it: the Sun settled into its own sign and its own decan, with
steadiness, not spectacle, as the headline trait. This is the Leo
people plan around.

## Warmth on schedule

A July 29 [Leo](/leo/) is generous on a random Tuesday the way other
people are generous on birthdays. The mood you get in week one is the
mood in year ten; the enthusiasm survives the middle of projects, the
sixth month of the renovation, the parts nobody claps for. A
[Sun in Leo](/learn/placements/sun-in-leo/) here demonstrates that
the sign's warmth was never really about occasions. It's a standing
condition.

## Reliability as romance

In love, the grand gesture exists, but the real offer is the pattern:
shows up, stays warm, remembers, repeats. Partners stop bracing for
the drop-off because there isn't one, and after certain histories
that steadiness reads as the most romantic material available. At
work, July 29 carries long efforts, the standard-keeper, the one
institutions trust with things that must not flicker. Give it a flame
and a decade and both will be accounted for.

## Stubborn in the sunlight

Steadiness has a failure mode: position becomes identity. July 29 can
keep warming a plan that died two winters ago, because changing
course feels like self-betrayal. The growth is a distinction.
Loyalty to the goal sometimes requires disloyalty to the plan, and
updating the route is not dimming the flame.
