A question mark would make a fair crest for this date. February 7
sits at the far end of the Mercury-ruled middle of
[Aquarius](/aquarius/), the last date fully inside that decan, and
it concentrates the stretch's signature move: asking the thing
everyone else had silently agreed not to ask.

## The question underneath everything

An [Aquarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-aquarius/) distrusts
inherited answers on principle, and Mercury's decan gives the
distrust a voice. February 7's questions aren't performance; they're
quality control. Why is the meeting weekly? Who does this rule
serve now? What would change your mind? Rules that survive the
audit get followed to the letter (this is the paradox people miss)
while the ones that fail get routed around without drama. The result
is a reputation for being difficult held almost exclusively by
people whose answers were thin.

## Love that starts as a debate

First dates that run four hours on one coffee: that's the romantic
signature. This birthday falls for minds first and settles for
nothing less than a partner who will say "you're wrong, here's why"
and stay for dessert. Once committed, it is strikingly steady,
questioning the world, not the relationship. At work, that wiring
makes the reviewer you want on the contract, the analyst who finds
the soft assumption, the interviewer nobody bluffs. Point the
skepticism at problems and it reads as brilliance; at colleagues,
as friction.

## Skepticism's exempt collection

The blind spot: the questioner's own beliefs, grandfathered in
decades ago and never re-examined. Fixed air archives its
conclusions, and the auditor rarely audits the archive. The growth
is symmetry. Audit your own certainties as ruthlessly as you audit
the world's, and retire the ones that fail.

Questions this good deserve their proper workshop. Which rooms of a life host the tinkering is set by your birth hour. The [chart](/birth-chart/) shows the floor plan.
