Midway through Cancer's third decan, July 15 tends to produce the
sign's elders-in-training: people who seem, from remarkably early, to
be rehearsing the role of the wise one. The Jupiter tone of these
degrees adds horizon to the sign's depth.

## Depth plus distance

A [Sun in Cancer](/learn/placements/sun-in-cancer/) feels everything;
this cut also interprets it. July 15 is the friend who can tell you
what your mood is actually about, the parent who treats a tantrum as
information, the colleague who reads the meeting's undertow and names
it without drama. The feelings aren't smaller than in early
[Cancer](/cancer/). They arrive with context attached.

## The mentor pattern

In love this birthday steadies people. Partners get patience,
proportion, and the rare experience of being understood slightly
before they understand themselves, plus, occasionally, the sense of
dating a very kind referee. The relationship works when the wisdom
travels both directions. At work the pattern is mentorship in any
costume: the senior engineer, the floor supervisor, the person new
hires get quietly routed to. Institutions lean on this date for
judgment, and the judgment usually holds.

## Wisdom as a hiding place

Perspective has a shadow use: narration instead of participation.
July 15 can describe its own feelings fluently and have them rarely,
advising others as a way of staying safely off the field. The growth
is taking a turn as the student: be advised, be surprised, be the one
at dinner who doesn't already know.
