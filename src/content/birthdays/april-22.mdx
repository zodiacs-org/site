Born on April 22, you get [Taurus](/taurus/) without dilution. The
date sits in the sign's first decan, which the traditional system
also gives to Venus, ruler of the sign, ruler of the decan, no
other planet's fingerprints on the file. Whatever the Bull means,
this stretch means it twice.

## The senses in charge

A double-Venus [Taurus Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/) runs
its life through the body's instruments. April 22 registers rooms
as textures and temperatures, judges days by meals, and trusts a
gut verdict over a spreadsheet with a clear conscience. The gut
has the better record. Taste is the organizing talent: for objects,
for food, for people. This date simply knows what's good, early and
without argument, and builds its world from the shortlist. Ask for
a restaurant, a mattress, or an honest opinion of your haircut.
The answers arrive with the same calm authority.

## Affection you can hold

Love, for April 22, is administered physically and materially:
cooked, carried, fixed, kept warm. Grand speeches are suspect;
groceries are a sonnet. It pairs best with partners who read that
dialect and answer in kind, and it wilts with those who need
constant verbal proof. Work follows the senses too: food, design,
horticulture, massage, materials, hospitality, fields with
texture. Whatever the role, the tell is the workspace: an April 22
desk is the comfortable one with the good lamp and the real mug.

## Pleasure's closed loop

The friction is that excellent taste, once satisfied, stops
exploring. The proven restaurant, the settled route, the known
pleasures on rotation: comfort becomes a walled garden, and
novelty waits outside with the growth. The edge is one deliberate
discomfort per season: the cuisine not yet trusted, the city not
yet rated, the acquaintance not yet vetted. Venus rewards it; the
shortlist gets richer.
