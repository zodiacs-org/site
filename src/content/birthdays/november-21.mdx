Most years, November 21 belongs to [Scorpio](/scorpio/) from
midnight to midnight, and then some years it doesn't, which is why
this page carries a table instead of a slogan. In a run of recent
years the Sun's crossing into [Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) lands on
the 21st itself, so a birth late enough in the day comes out on the
Archer's side of the line. The table above holds your year; if it
marks a split, the birth hour is the deciding fact.

## The last degree and the first

Either way, this date lives against a boundary, and boundary
birthdays tend to carry both textures. End-stage Scorpio is the
[Scorpio Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-scorpio/) at its most
concentrated: private, penetrating, finished with pretense. Day-one
Sagittarius is the
[Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) at its most
unfiltered: candid, restless, already halfway elsewhere. The common
thread is truth (one excavates it, the other announces it), and a
November 21 usually does some of each: the investigator who, at long
last, publishes the findings.

## Loving from depth, speaking from range

In love this blend bonds like water and talks like fire: attachments
form deep and private, then get discussed with a frankness pure
Scorpio would never authorize. Partners get real intimacy plus
honest weather reports, a rarer combination than it sounds. At work
the mix suits truth-facing trades: investigative anything, research
that ends in publication, teaching hard material, and it carries a
low tolerance for jobs that run on polite fictions.

## Where holding meets leaving

The friction is internal: Scorpio grips, Sagittarius bolts, and both
reflexes can fire on the same bad day. Hold harder, or go now. The
result can be relationships kept past their truth or left before
their repair. The growth is the third option both signs respect:
stay, and say the true thing out loud, while it can still change
something.

Don't take the cusp as a shrug. Your year is in the table above, and

degree for your birth minute, one date, one answer.
