July 7 lands in the middle of [Cancer](/cancer/), no cusp drama, no
year-to-year ambiguity, just the Sun parked deep in the sign of tides
and memory. Mid-sign birthdays tend to get the sign's character at full
strength, and this one comes with a twist worth knowing: it falls in
Cancer's second decan, the stretch tradition hands to Mars.

## The Mars-flavored Cancer

A [Cancer Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-cancer/) runs on care,
loyalty, and a long memory. The second decan adds spine. Where the
first days of the sign protect by holding close, the July 7 stretch
protects by stepping forward. This is the Cancer that writes the
strongly worded email, negotiates the family estate, coaches the team
it loves. The warmth is the same; the delivery has more edge. People
born this week are often surprised to be called intense, because from
the inside it just feels like taking care of things that matter.

## In love and at work

In relationships the pattern is devotion with initiative. This
birthday doesn't wait to be chosen; it decides, then builds. Partners
get fierce loyalty and a person who notices what they need before they
say it, along with occasional campaigns about how the household ought
to run. At work the same wiring makes a natural protector-manager:
someone who remembers every colleague's situation and will fight the
org chart on their behalf. The best roles have both people to look
after and something real to push against.

## The growth edge

The friction point is that hurt and anger arrive holding hands. When a
July 7 Cancer feels dismissed, the crab shell and the Mars temper go
up together, and a small slight can draw a response sized for a siege.
The work is separating the two: feel the sting, then choose the fight.
Not every tide needs a seawall.

days, so two July 7 birthdays a year apart can run on very different
