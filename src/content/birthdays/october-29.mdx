Fixed signs anchor the deep middle of their seasons, and none anchors
like Scorpio. October 29 gets that grip in its Mars-decan form: one
aim, chosen slowly, kept through the long unglamorous stretch that
defeats most enthusiasm. What [Scorpio](/scorpio/) keeps, it keeps.

## One aim, held

Attention with tenure. Interests here aren't phases. The craft
picked up at nineteen is still compounding at fifty, the question
that hooked this date in school still gets worked on weekends. A
[Scorpio Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-scorpio/) brings its entire
attention or none of it; this decan extends the warranty, so the
entire attention stays put for decades. Mastery is less a goal than
a side effect of never leaving.

## Slow to choose, hard to shake

The choosing takes seasons; the staying takes decades. Partners get
someone who doesn't renegotiate the relationship every rough month.
The commitment was made with rough months already priced in. Work
suits the long arcs: surgery, craft trades, deep research,
institution-building, anything where year eight matters more than
week one and most competitors have already left.

## Grip past the point

The friction is holding what's finished: the job outgrown, the
position defended after the war ended, the promise kept to a person
who no longer exists. The growth is noticing that an ending can be
the project succeeding. No sign restarts better than this one, but
you have to set the old thing down to find that out.
