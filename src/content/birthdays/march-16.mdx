Late [Pisces](/pisces/) gets described as gentle, and March 16
mostly is, up
until something it believes in gets stepped on. Then the other
inheritance shows: these are Mars-ruled degrees by the traditional
count, and the fish, it turns out, has been holding convictions the
whole time.

## Conviction under the current

The [Pisces Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/) is famously
flexible about plans, schedules, dinner, nearly everything. March 16
is flexible about all of it except the short list: a handful of
beliefs about loyalty, fairness, and how people should be treated
that sit below the waterline, non-negotiable and mostly invisible.
Friends discover the list by accident, usually by watching this mild
person go suddenly immovable in a meeting. The surprise is the point
of the story they tell afterward.

## A devoted partner, a driven colleague

In love, the March 16 keel shows as constancy: moods may drift, plans
may dissolve, but the commitment itself doesn't wobble. Partners
learn the difference between what's soft here, almost everything,
and what isn't: the vows, spoken or not. At work this date does its
best in mission-shaped places (nonprofits, schools, newsrooms, teams
with a cause in the charter) and goes quietly gray in roles that ask
it to believe nothing. A paycheck alone has never been enough to
move it; the work has to mean something, or the effort quietly
withholds itself.

## The fuse nobody sees coming

The friction is disclosure. Because the convictions live underwater,
people trample them unknowingly, and March 16 logs each step
silently until the dam gives all at once, startling everyone,
including itself. The fix isn't fewer convictions; it's earlier
signage. Post the list. Let people know where the load-bearing walls
are before they lean.
