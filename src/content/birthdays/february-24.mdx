Change breaks some people and merely re-forms this one. February
24 sits midway through the Jupiter-ruled opening decan of
[Pisces](/pisces/), and it shows the sign's adaptability at full
function: new city, new audience, new diagnosis. Where others are
still grieving the old arrangement, this date has already learned
the new banks and found the current.

## Water that learns the banks

A [Pisces Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/) doesn't resist
the river; it studies it. What reads as luck from outside is
actually rapid, mostly unconscious recalibration, sensing the
new rules of a room before anyone explains them, adjusting
without announcing it. Jupiter's tone adds appetite: change isn't
just survivable here, it's interesting. February 24 is the friend
who moved abroad with two suitcases and was somehow local by
June.

## Easy to live with, hard to pin

In love the flexibility is a gift with a clause. This date
accommodates beautifully (schedules, quirks, in-laws) and makes
a famously easy partner; pinning it to a five-year plan is the
struggle, since every plan feels like a wall pretending the river
stopped. It pairs best with someone decisive enough to propose a
direction and relaxed enough to revise it. At work, flux is the
habitat: startups, turnarounds, freelancing, live production,
anywhere the plan changes hourly and the calm person wins.
Rigid process kills the gift.

## The dissolving default

The friction: adapt long enough to enough containers and the
original shape gets hard to locate. Preferences go quiet, then
missing; one day the accommodating partner can't answer "what do
you actually want?" and the silence frightens both of you. The
work is keeping two or three non-negotiables on file and saying
them early, before the shape-shifting starts.

The date fixes a fluid Sun; the fixed points come from the clock.
Rising sign, Moon, the houses that give the current its banks:
hand the [birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) your birth
time and see the structure under the water.
