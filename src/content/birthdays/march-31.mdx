March ends inside [Aries](/aries/) but on an ambiguous degree: the
31st straddles the seam between the sign's first decan and its
second, and only your birth year and hour say whether the Sun stood
in Mars' opening stretch or the Sun-ruled run that follows. The difference is real.
It's the difference between fire that starts things and fire that
holds a room.

## Where raw drive meets warmth

The pure first-decan profile is velocity: candid, kinetic, allergic
to queues. What the second decan adds, wherever a birth time lands
there, is sustain: loyalty with a longer half-life, generosity with
some ceremony to it, an instinct for occasion. Most March 31s
recognize both: the
[Aries Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-aries/) impatience wrapped
around an unexpectedly warm center, the person who interrupts you at
dinner and then gives the best toast of the year.

## Devotion with a spotlight

In love this date runs hot and visible. Affection isn't just felt but
staged, the public birthday tribute, the defense of a partner
delivered in front of witnesses. It bonds fast, Aries style, then
keeps the bond with a warmth the sign isn't always credited with. At
work the double profile likes the front of the house: leading
visibly, presenting, teaching, any role where drive also has to carry
a crowd. Pure back-office speed wastes half the toolkit.

## The applause problem

The friction, strongest on the Sun-leaning side: effort starts
tracking its audience. A March 31 can catch itself doing generous
things at half strength when nobody's watching, or keeping count of
thanks not received. The Mars side has the usual haste to manage; the
deeper work is doing one substantial thing a month that stays
anonymous on purpose. It recalibrates what the effort was for.
