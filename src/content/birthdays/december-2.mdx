Some birthdays sit on a line most horoscopes never mention.
December 2 is [Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) without question, but it
falls almost exactly where the sign's first decan hands off to its
second, which means birth year and hour decide whether your Sun sits
in the Jupiter-ruled opening or the Mars-ruled middle. Two people with
this birthday, born a few years apart, can run on noticeably different
fuel.

## Two tones of the same fire

On the Jupiter side, the
[Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) is at its
most expansive: philosophical, generous, funnier than it needs to be,
interested in everything at once. On the Mars side the same fire picks
up a direction and a push, quicker to act, more competitive, less
patient with talk that goes nowhere. Most December 2 people recognize
both settings in themselves: the browser with forty tabs open, and the
sudden decisive lunge that closes all but one.

## Company and career on either side

The Jupiter cut loves like a co-adventurer and works best where the
learning never stops: teaching, travel, anything with a frontier
still on it. The Mars cut loves like a teammate (loyal, direct, a
little competitive) and wants work with clear stakes and visible
progress. Either way, the constants hold: honesty over comfort,
freedom over supervision, and a partner who packs a bag instead of
holding the leash.

## The overreach pattern

Both settings share one bill: too much, agreed to too fast. Jupiter
overbooks out of enthusiasm; Mars overcommits out of confidence. The
growth work for this date is the pause between want and yes, one
honest look at the actual week before the word leaves your mouth. The
adventures that survive that pause are the ones worth packing for.

Which side of the decan line you fall on is not a matter of opinion;
it is a degree. The [birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) computes
your Sun's exact position from your birth date and time and settles
it, along with everything else the date alone cannot tell you.
