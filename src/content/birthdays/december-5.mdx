Some Sagittarians choose their goals by how hard the goals push back; December 5 is their birthday. Mid-sign, Mars decan:
the [Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) love of distance fused with a genuine
taste for difficulty. Easy wins bore this date. The interesting
mountain is the one the guidebook warns about.

## The appetite for difficulty

Where other birthdays ask whether a thing will work, a December 5
[Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) asks whether
it is worth the fight, and lights up when the answer is yes. The
pattern shows early: the hardest teacher becomes the favorite, the
steep route gets attempted first, the dare outcompetes the invitation.
It is not recklessness so much as calibration. This birthday only
trusts the victories it had to stretch for, and its optimism runs deep
enough to make stretching feel safe.

## Partners in the attempt

Relationships work best when they are a shared attempt at something:
a build, a move abroad, a family raised deliberately. The partner who
says "you cannot be serious" and starts packing anyway is the one who
stays. What wears the bond down is comfort pursued for its own sake;
this date tends to confuse settled with finished, and needs a partner
who can tell the two apart out loud. In a career, aim it at
turnarounds, frontier problems, and first attempts of any kind. It
will out-effort the specialists on appetite alone.

## When everything becomes a contest

The friction: not every room is an arena. The habit of measuring
creeps into love, conversation, even rest, and the people closest to
a December 5 sometimes want company rather than a rival or a coach.
The growth edge is letting some things stay unmeasured: a walk that
is not training, a talk that nobody wins.
