Halfway through the calendar, and halfway between two versions of the
Crab: July 2 falls so close to the border of Cancer's first and
second decans that the birth year, occasionally the birth hour,
decides which one claims you. Most years put this date just past the
line, into the stretch old astrology assigns to Mars; a good number
leave it in the Moon's opening third. The sign itself is never in
question, only the flavor.

## Which side of the line

The lunar cut of [Cancer](/cancer/) is the pure caretaker: receptive,
tidal, tuned to whatever the room isn't saying. The Mars cut keeps
the tenderness and adds drive. It acts on what it feels. People born
July 2 tend to read as gentle right up until something they love is
threatened, then turn abruptly decisive, and that composite is the
border itself. A [Cancer Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-cancer/)
always runs on protectiveness; here the open question is whether it
guards by holding still or by moving first.

## At home and on the job

In love: warm, constant, slow to open, very hard to dislodge.
Whichever decan claims the year, partners get someone who tracks
their moods like coastal weather and stocks the shelves accordingly.
The Moon tone soothes; the Mars tone solves; the best relationships
let this person do both without being told to relax. At work, July 2
thrives where there are people to look after and problems to chase:
teaching, health care, team leads, a small business run like a
household.

## When guarding turns to grip

The shadow is proprietary care. Under stress this birthday starts
managing its people, vetting their choices, pre-living their risks,
narrating the dangers. The kindness underneath is real; the effect
can read as control. The practice is trust: care that leaves the
other person's hands free.
