Summer ends on this birthday, at least by the meteorologist's
reckoning, and the temperament agrees with the season's turn: take
stock, keep what proved itself, spend nothing twice. August 31 closes
the month deep in [Virgo's](/virgo/) opening decan, where Mercury rules twice over, with the stocktaking instinct at full strength.

## The stocktaking temperament

An August 31 measures the year in outcomes, not intentions. The
[Virgo Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-virgo/) here has an accountant's
honesty about effort (what worked is kept, what didn't is retired
without a eulogy), plus Mercury's speed in telling the difference. It
makes for a person of unusual practical courage: not the loud kind,
the kind that cancels the failing plan in week three instead of year
three. Sentiment gets its place; it just doesn't get the books. Ask for an
honest read on the plan and you'll receive one, dated, initialed,
and kinder than you feared.

## Tending people, tending projects

In relationships this date is a cultivator, attentive to conditions,
patient with seasons, unimpressed by grand beginnings that skip the
watering schedule. Its love deepens on evidence and is nearly
impossible to fake out. Partners get durability and honest weather
reports; they should bring the spontaneity themselves. At work, trust
an August 31 with anything that must genuinely conclude: audits,
launches, hard calls about sunk costs. It ends things well, which is
rarer than starting them.

## When done beats flawless

The friction is the standard applied to the self at year-end scale:
harvests judged against imagined yields, a good season written off for
the one failed row. This birthday can be a generous assessor of
everything except its own ledger. The growth edge is counting what
actually came in, publicly, occasionally, with something resembling
celebration.
