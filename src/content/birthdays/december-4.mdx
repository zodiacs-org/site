By December 4 the Sun is settled well inside the stretch of [Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) that tradition gives Mars, and the signature shows up first in speech. Every Sagittarius tells the truth; this stretch of the sign
tells it with velocity. The observation, the verdict, and the joke
arrive in a single delivery, often before anyone asked.

## Where the bluntness sharpens

The [Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) treats
honesty as a form of respect, and Mars adds urgency to the principle.
December 4 people are the ones who name the problem in the meeting
where everyone else is nodding, who ask the question the whole dinner
table has been avoiding. It makes them invaluable and occasionally
combustible. The saving grace is a total absence of malice: the arrow
is aimed at the issue, never at the person, and they are genuinely
puzzled when feelings catch a graze.

## Sparring partners, shared causes

Love, for this birthday, needs a partner who hears directness as
intimacy, someone who would rather have the real sentence than a
soft one, and who can serve it straight back. Playful argument is
often the courtship. Work rewards the same wiring wherever straight
talk is the job: steering a team through a hard patch, giving the
honest assessment, negotiating without theater. Cultures that run on
hints read this date as a bull in the china shop; cultures in real
trouble call it a relief.

## Aim before you loose

The growth edge is timing. A true sentence delivered at the wrong
moment does the work of a false one. It gets defended against
instead of heard. December 4's lesson is not to soften the truth but
to pick the hour it can land. Held for a day and aimed with care, the
same arrow persuades instead of stinging.
