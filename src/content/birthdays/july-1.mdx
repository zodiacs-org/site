The year tips over on July 1 (six months behind, six ahead) and the
date carries a matching wrinkle in the zodiac: it sits almost exactly
where the first decan of [Cancer](/cancer/) hands off to the second.
In most years the Sun is still finishing the Moon's own opening third
of the sign; in some it has already crossed into the Mars-toned
middle. Which reading is yours depends on the year, and close to the
line, on the hour.

## The hinge between Moon and Mars

A [Cancer Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-cancer/) organizes life
around care; the decans set the delivery. The Moon's cut protects by
atmosphere. It feeds people, keeps the peace, absorbs trouble before
anyone else notices trouble arrived. The Mars cut confronts: same
warmth, more push, the version of the Crab that phones the landlord
and wins. July 1 people often describe having both settings and a
felt switch between them, gentle by default, abruptly forceful when
something they love is on the line.

## How the two tones love and work

Either way, this is a devoted partner and the colleague who notices
things. Moon-toned, the bond runs on refuge: kept rituals, a calm
kitchen, the sense that someone is always minding the door.
Mars-toned, devotion turns active, less sitting with your problem,
more solving it by Thursday. At work the split reads as maintainer
versus defender: one keeps the team functioning, the other takes on
whatever threatens it. Both do their best where people, not
abstractions, are the point.

## The friction worth naming

The hinge shares one weak spot: indirectness under stress. On the
Moon side it looks like going quiet and hoping to be asked; on the
Mars side, like snapping about the dishes because the real grievance
went unsaid. The repair is the same for both. Say the need before it
ferments.

The hinge between decans is a measurement away from settled. [Compute the degree](/birth-chart/) for your birth minute and the question closes, while the Moon's sign, the larger half of any Cancer story, comes along in the same pass.
