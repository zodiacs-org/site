No date in October gets asked its sign more often, and none deserves
a table more. In most years since 1940, the Sun's move from
[Libra](/libra/) into [Scorpio](/scorpio/) happens during October 23
itself (births before the crossing come out Libra, births after it
Scorpio), while a minority of years hand the whole day to Libra and a
few give all of it to Scorpio. The table above has your year; the
hour does the rest.

## One day, two owners

End-stage Libra and day-one Scorpio share a preoccupation, other
people, and disagree about method. The last degrees of Libra read a
room to balance it; the first degrees of Scorpio read it to learn
what's underneath. A birth on either side of the line tends to
inherit both moves: the [Libra Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-libra/)
grace running on the [Scorpio Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-scorpio/)
depth of attention. People describe the result as disarming,
friendly on approach, oddly hard to fool.

## Loving like one, guarding like the other

This birthday courts openly and attaches privately. It wants the good
conversation and the unbreakable confidence, ideally from the same
person, and it gives both back once convinced. At work it's the
trusted negotiator, sent in when the deal needs charm and the files
need guarding, equally at home making the introduction and keeping
the secret that follows from it.

## Charm hiding the sting

The friction: the polish holds while something underneath keeps
score, and the eventual reckoning arrives polished too, which makes
it sharper. The growth is complaining earlier and rougher. Drafts are
allowed; resentment with good manners is still resentment.

This is the one October birthday where the sign is genuinely a
computed answer that changes with the year. Check the table above; if
your year splits the day, the [birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/)
reads the Sun's degree for your exact minute, and the question closes
for good.
