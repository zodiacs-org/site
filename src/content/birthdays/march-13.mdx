Advocacy comes naturally to the Mars-run stretch of late
[Pisces](/pisces/), and March 13 may be its clearest case: the empath
who takes sides. Where much of the sign feels everything and stays
neutral, this birthday feels everything and then files a complaint on
your behalf.

## An advocate's temperament

The [Pisces Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/) here scans a room
like any fish, but what it does with the scan is Mars all through:
the intern being talked over gets the floor handed back, the friend
with the bad landlord gets company at the tenancy hearing, the group
chat's absent member gets defended in absentia. March 13 people often
say they can't watch unfairness without their pulse changing. It
isn't performance; witnesses are optional. The strong sense of how
people ought to be treated is simply wired to the motor.

## Loyalty in love, advocacy at work

A partner of this date acquires a permanent second: someone who will
state their case to anyone, including to the partner's own family,
calmly and without being asked twice. The shadow side is picking
fights the partner didn't want fought. Work-wise, the profile is
unsubtle (union rep, public defender's office, social work, ombuds
roles, journalism with teeth), but it thrives anywhere there's a
power gradient and someone standing at the bottom of it.

## Fights borrowed from other people

The friction is inventory: a March 13 can carry so many other
people's battles that its own case never gets to court. The raise
unasked, the boundary undefended, the grievance unspoken: all
postponed while someone else gets championed. The rule that helps:
for every fight taken up for another, one taken up for yourself.
Advocates deserve representation too.
