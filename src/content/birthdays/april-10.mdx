One degree of the sky separates two readings of this birthday.
April 10 stands at the opening of Aries' third decan, the stretch
tradition assigns to Jupiter, but in a share of years the Sun
hasn't crossed yet at birth hour, leaving the date in the Sun-ruled
second. Year and hour decide; the honest description of April 10 is
a Jupiter-leaning fire with a solar aftertaste.

## Where the fire gets bigger

Third-decan [Aries](/aries/) trades some of mid-sign's self-focus
for scale. The [Aries Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-aries/) here
wants projects with acreage: the business that becomes three, the
trip that becomes a year abroad, the argument that turns out to be
a philosophy. Meanwhile the lingering second-decan tone keeps the
performance instinct alive. April 10 does its expanding in public,
narrating as it goes. The combination reads as infectious ambition:
people leave conversations with this date having accidentally
agreed to something large. The follow-up message arrives before the
doubt does.

## Bolder maps, warmer rooms

In love, April 10 is the proposer of things (moves, ventures, next
chapters), and it thrives with partners who counter-propose rather
than merely consent. Enthusiasm is both its dialect of affection
and the response it listens for. At work it suits frontier roles:
expansion, founding, field work, anything where the map still has
blank patches. Maintenance posts make it visibly droop; it needs a
next thing on the calendar the way other people need weekends.

## Promises sized too generously

The friction is arithmetic. Jupiter-leaning Aries commits at the
size of its excitement, and excitement is always larger than the
week. Deadlines slip not from laziness but from optimism
compounding daily. The growth edge is halving: promise half,
deliver whole, enjoy the novel sensation of being early. The
ambition loses nothing; the reputation gains everything.
