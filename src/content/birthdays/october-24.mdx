By October 24 the handover is mostly complete: in the large majority
of years since 1940 the whole day belongs to [Scorpio](/scorpio/),
and in the remainder the Sun leaves [Libra](/libra/) partway through
the day, so an early birth in those years still lands in the Scales.
Two things follow: the odds favor Scorpio, and the table above beats
the odds, because it has your actual year.

## Depth with diplomatic manners

The probable read is first-decan Scorpio: attention that goes all
the way down, loyalty that outlasts fashion, a preference for the
true version over the pleasant one. With Libra a single day behind,
the delivery often stays diplomatic: the
[Scorpio Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-scorpio/) depth wearing the
[Libra Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-libra/) manners. If your year
splits the day and you arrived before the crossing, run the emphasis
the other way.

## Committed, and considered

In love this date is all-in once decided, but the deciding is
courteous, interest shown plainly, questions asked kindly, nobody
made to feel processed. Work suits depth with a client face:
counsel, therapy, deal work, research that has to be explained to a
room before it counts. The rare mix of gravity and tact gets noticed
early and promoted quietly.

## The velvet interrogation

The friction: questions that are really tests, asked charmingly, with
the results recorded. Partners eventually notice the pattern and mind
it. The growth is swapping the quiz for the disclosure. Say what you
fear instead of testing for it. It yields the same information and
costs no trust.

If your birthday is October 24, start with the table above. Most
years it answers Scorpio outright. If yours is a split year, the
[birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) settles it from your birth
minute, and adds the Moon and rising sign while it's at it.
