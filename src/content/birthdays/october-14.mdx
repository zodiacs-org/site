Libra changes register around the 14th: the Sun settles into the
sign's final decan, the third that the traditional system gives to
Mercury, and the Scales get a voice. Everything the sign feels about
balance, this stretch can say, which makes October 14 the opening
date of [Libra's](/libra/) most articulate run.

## The sign finds its voice

Fairness with vocabulary. This date can name the exact asymmetry in a
situation (not "something's off" but which thing, held by whom,
since when) and do it without drawing blood. Friends borrow its
phrasing for their difficult emails. A
[Libra Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-libra/) works rooms toward
level; the Mercury decan does it out loud, gracefully, in complete
sentences that people find themselves repeating later as their own.

## Courtship by conversation

Talk is the engine of love here. This birthday falls for minds
mid-sentence and stays for the ongoing exchange; silence with a
partner has to be earned comfort, never avoidance wearing a calm
face. Work runs on the same fuel: negotiation, teaching, writing,
any field where the explanation is half the product. Put an October
14 where nothing needs saying and watch the lights dim.

## Talking around the choice

Articulacy can become evasion: every option gets a beautiful
paragraph, none gets a signature. The weighing is sincere, but at
some point the sentences have to stop and one thing has to be picked.
The growth practice is ending meetings, including the internal ones,
with a decision read back in plain words, dated, and left alone.

An eye for proportion still needs commissions. Where the taste gets exercised (spaces, sentences, relationships, ledgers) is allocated by your birth hour, and [the calculator](/birth-chart/) itemizes the portfolio of rooms.
