On December 12 the Sun stands at the border between the second and
third decans of [Sagittarius](/sagittarius/), close enough that
birth year and hour, not the calendar, decide which side yours falls
on. One side belongs to Mars, the other to the Sun itself. Same sign,
same fire; noticeably different burn.

## The fighter and the beacon

The Mars cut of the
[Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) is the
campaigner: direct, driven, happiest with resistance to push against.
The Sun cut is the beacon: warmer, more visible, the version of the
sign people gather around rather than follow into battle. December 12
people usually report a blend (a competitor's reflexes with a host's
instincts), and which one leads can genuinely come down to the year
and hour of birth. Both cuts share the sign's spine: honesty first,
horizon always.

## Two ways to pair up and show up

On the Mars side, love is partnership in motion (shared missions,
cheerful argument, loyalty proven in difficulty), and work wants real
stakes and a worthy opponent. On the Sun side, love is more
ceremonial: this cut gives well, remembers occasions, wants the bond
witnessed; work leans toward the front of the room, teaching,
presenting, leading on warmth. In practice a December 12 borrows from
both playbooks and confuses anyone who expected only one.

## Heat management

Either way, the common friction is heat. The Mars version argues past
the win; the Sun version performs past the point of listening. Both
can leave the quietest person in their life feeling outshone or
outgunned without ever meaning harm. The growth edge is shared too:
noticing your own wattage, and turning it down on purpose in rooms
that need light more than fire.

The decan question has an exact answer. Your Sun occupies one
degree, computable from your birth date and time.
The [birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) settles it, then keeps
going: Moon, rising, all of it.
