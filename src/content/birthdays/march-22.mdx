March 22 is the first date in March that belongs to a single sign
with no year table and no asterisk: [Aries](/aries/), newly arrived,
the Sun in its opening degrees. Both the sign and its first decan
answer to Mars in the traditional system, the same planet twice,
and the doubling shows. Nothing about this birthday is diluted.

## The sign at its newest

The [Aries Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-aries/) runs on ignition
everywhere, but in the opening degrees the ignition is the whole
dashboard. March 22 people start (conversations, ventures, mornings)
with a spontaneity other signs assume must be courage and Aries
experiences as the absence of any reason to wait. The wheel has just
turned over, and people born in its first hours seem to inherit the
setting: everything interesting is ahead, none of it has happened
yet, let's go.

## First moves in love and work

Romance with a March 22 skips the slow burn. Interest is declared
early and plainly, pursuit is energetic, and games are not so much
refused as never learned. What keeps this Aries interested is a
partner with momentum of their own, self-contained, busy, slightly
ahead. At work the value is obvious: hand this person the blank page,
the cold start, the empty territory. First weeks, first drafts, and
first customers are the specialty; handovers should follow soon
after, for reasons the next section covers.

## Finishing, the old enemy

The friction is the second half of everything. Early-decan Aries
supplies the launch and then goes looking for the next launch,
leaving a wake of seventy-percent-done, the app at beta, the garage
half cleared. Two workable fixes: team up deliberately with
finishers, or redefine the finish line as its own contest. March 22
will complete anything it has decided is a race.

The date makes the Sun's sign unambiguous; the rest of the chart
still runs on the clock. What feels, what greets, and where it all happens move by the hour, and the [birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) fixes each one from your exact birth time and town.
