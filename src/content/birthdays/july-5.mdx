There is nothing borderline about July 5: the Sun stands mid-Cancer,
second decan, Mars-toned, the sign running at full depth. Dates this
far into a sign don't borrow from neighbors or hedge between decans.
What they offer instead is concentration, and this one concentrates
on endurance. If other birthdays sprint, this one packs water.

## Stamina of the heart

Plenty of placements love intensely; fewer love long. A July 5
[Cancer](/cancer/) treats devotion as a distance event: the
friendship maintained since second grade, the parent driven to every
appointment for a decade, the team supported through three rebuilds.
The Mars tone matters here: this isn't passive patience but
maintained effort, care with training miles behind it. This grade of [Sun in Cancer](/learn/placements/sun-in-cancer/) doesn't burn out of love; it conditions for it.

## The long-haul partner

In love, July 5 chooses slowly and then considers the matter settled.
The decision, once made, becomes load-bearing. Partners get
consistency that outlasts moods, theirs included, plus somebody who
still notices things in year twelve. At work this is the person
institutions quietly depend on: the ten-year employee who actually
knows why the system is shaped that way, the client relationship
nobody else could have kept. The best roles reward staying: long
projects, deep crafts, places where trust accrues. Fast-churning
workplaces frustrate this date less by their pace than by their
amnesia.

## Holding on past the point

The same grip that keeps treasures keeps expired things: jobs
outgrown, friendships running on memory alone, plans that quietly
turned into weight. The honest question is whether a thing is held
because it's alive or because release feels like betrayal, and
asking it yearly costs less than never asking at all. Grief for a
released thing passes; the drag of a dead one doesn't.
