After a month of deep water, fire. By November 24 the Sun is in
[Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) with no asterisks, first decan, which
the traditional system gives to Jupiter, the sign's own ruler.
Doubled rulership means nothing diluted: this is Sagittarius
straight from the source, and birthdays this early in a sign get its
qualities fresh, enthusiasm before management, honesty before
diplomacy.

## First days of fire

A [Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) at the
start of the sign runs on undiluted forward motion. Born today, the
signature is immediacy: the opinion now, the plan now, the packed
bag by the door as a standing arrangement. There's a brightness to
early-sign people that others find either infectious or alarming
(usually infectious), the sense that the story is just getting
started and you're invited.

## Room to move, someone to move with

In love this date offers momentum: the relationship that goes
somewhere, literally and otherwise. What it needs back is space
without suspicion, a partner who reads the restlessness as
metabolism rather than flight risk. Given that, the loyalty is real
and more durable than the sign's reputation predicts. At work,
motion is the requirement: travel, variety, steep learning curves,
doors that open onto further doors. A fixed desk under fluorescent
sameness will be survived, briefly, and then escaped.

## New as a reflex

The friction is that *new* answers every question. New job for the
stale week, new city for the hard conversation, new person for the
repairable relationship. The good part of many things starts only
after the novelty ends, and a November 24 can spend years arriving
just in time to leave. The growth is one deliberate stay, letting
something get boring on purpose and finding out what lives under the
boredom.

The date starts the Sagittarius chapter of your chart; the plot is
local. Hour and birthplace assign the Moon, the Ascendant, and the
houses where the fire actually burns. The
