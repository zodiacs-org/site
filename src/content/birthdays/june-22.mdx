The turn is usually complete by this date: in most years since 1940,
June 22 belongs to [Cancer](/cancer/) from its first minute, and in
no year on record here does the whole day stay [Gemini](/gemini/).
But roughly one year in eight catches the Sun still finishing Gemini
when the date begins. The solstice crossing lands partway through
the day, and births before it come out twins. The table above names
those years; this page describes the blend the boundary leaves
behind.

## Mostly Crab, with a quicksilver thread

Even in solidly Cancer years, a birth date this near the border
tends to keep a Gemini accent. The
[Cancer Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-cancer/) leads: care first,
rooms read on entry, people kept for decades. Through it runs the
[Gemini Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-gemini/) thread: the need
to talk it out, the joke at the tender moment, curiosity that
wanders even while the heart stays home. The result is a Crab who
narrates: protective and articulate about it, which the sign's
quieter cuts rarely are.

## Closeness with a commentary track

In love, June 22 offers a rare package, deep loyalty that can
actually discuss itself. Feelings get named here more readily than
Cancer's reputation suggests, though usually with a laugh attached
as insurance. At work, the blend suits jobs where care needs
language: teaching, health communication, editing memoirs, managing
teams through change. Give it people to keep and words to keep them
with, and it runs for decades.

## The deflection habit

The friction: the commentary can become the moat. When something
cuts close, June 22 talks around the wound, articulate about
everything except the sore point itself, and the shell closes
behind the sentences. Listeners leave informed and unadmitted. The
growth is dropping the insurance laugh once a season and letting a
tender line stand plain.

For the divided years no page can call it, but the sky can be computed. The [Sun's degree at your birth minute](/birth-chart/) settles the sign, and the chart around it explains what the settlement means.
