Loyalty gets loud on this date. July 28 runs early [Leo](/leo/) at
full temperature (Sun-ruled decan, no moderating influence), and its
signature is courage sourced from affection: bravery that shows up
because somebody needed defending, not because a fight looked fun.

## Courage with a warm engine

Where other bold placements seek the contest, a July 28
[Sun in Leo](/learn/placements/sun-in-leo/) seeks the cause, and the
cause is nearly always a person. The shy friend gets introduced
around the party like visiting royalty. The absent colleague's work
gets credited to its actual author in the meeting that mattered. The
heat is real, but it points outward, at whatever stands between this
date's people and their due.

## Defense counsel for your people

In relationships, partners get championed, introduced with their
full title, bragged about in absentia, backed through career changes
that alarm both families. The pride in its people is enormous and
public. At work this date takes blame upward and passes credit
downward, which makes it the manager people follow between companies
and the first voice in the room when a policy lands unfairly.

## Pride's blind corner

The weak spot is reverse gear. Once July 28 has taken a public
position, especially in someone's defense, backing down feels like
abandoning them, and the argument can outlive everyone's memory of
its subject. The growth is separating the person from the position:
you can update the second without deserting the first, and conceding
in private costs less than it feels like it will.

Advocates prepare from complete files. The date contributes a single affidavit; your birth hour supplies the discovery, and [the chart](/birth-chart/) binds the full record for review.
