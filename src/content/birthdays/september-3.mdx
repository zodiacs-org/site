By the third of September the Sun has settled into Virgo's middle
decan, the ten degrees old astrology hands to Saturn, and the sign
changes register slightly. The quicksilver quality of early
[Virgo](/virgo/) picks up ballast here: same precision, longer
horizon. If the sign's first decan is the editor, this stretch is the
project manager of the edit.

## Where patience joins precision

A [Virgo Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-virgo/) notices everything; a
Saturn-decan Virgo Sun also waits. September 3 people tend to be
planners in the deep sense, not the color-coded-calendar cliché but
the habit of thinking in sequences: what has to be true before the
next thing can happen, and what before that. They are hard to rush and
hard to rattle. Friends learn that the slow, considered answer, when
it finally arrives, is usually the one the room ends up using.

## Slow to choose, built to stay

Romance runs on the same clock. This birthday vets quietly and
thoroughly, then commits with a completeness that surprises anyone who
mistook the caution for coolness. Anniversaries are remembered;
promises are kept to the letter, because to this date a promise is a
specification. Careers flourish where endurance is the skill: long
research arcs, medicine, building trades, anything with a
certification most people quit halfway through. Shortcuts offend
something in this Sun. Friends planning anything real learn to loop
this birthday in early; the plan comes back tighter, with the failure
points already braced.

## Rest that never gets scheduled

The catch is that the finish line recedes. There is always a next
prerequisite, and rest gets filed after it, which means, in practice,
never. September 3 people can spend a decade preparing for a life
they'd already earned. The corrective is treating rest as one more
thing to be specified: on the calendar, non-negotiable, done to
standard.

a sign or two that week, the rising sign turned with the hour, and the
houses depend on where you were born. The
