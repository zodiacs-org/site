An archer's craft is mostly waiting, reading the wind, holding
still, releasing once. November 29 gets more of that patience than
[Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) is usually credited with: late in the
first decan, the sign's fire is still Jupiter's (hopeful, honest,
horizon-facing), but it has begun to aim rather than merely burn.
It's the difference between a spark and a shot.

## The patient archer

A [Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) at this
depth in the decan keeps the sign's whole kit (the candor, the
humor, the appetite for elsewhere) and adds selection. Fewer
targets, chosen better. Born today, you likely recognize the
pattern: enthusiasms that used to scatter now converge, and the
famous Sagittarian honesty increasingly waits for its moment instead
of interrupting on arrival.

## Chosen targets, kept word

In love the aim shows as discernment: this date flirts broadly but
commits narrowly, and when it finally names the person, the naming
tends to hold. Partners get honesty, adventure, and (the quiet
surprise) follow-through. At work the pairing of range and aim is
the strategist's temperament: survey the whole field, then put
everything down one road. Consulting, program leadership, expedition
logistics, editing: anywhere breadth has to end in a decision.

## The near field

The friction is focal length. Eyes set to the horizon miss what sits
at arm's reach: the partner's flat tone tonight, the small task now
three weeks feral, the friend who needed asking twice. Nothing in
this date's telescope shows the near field; it has to be checked
deliberately, like mirrors on a car. The growth is a weekly
close-range pass: what, within ten feet, needs attention before
anything on the map does?

A birthday can promise the Sun's position and no more. The Moon
relocates every two or three days, the Ascendant every couple of
hours; only your birth time pins them. Give the
[birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) the time, and it returns
the chart.
