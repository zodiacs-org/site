A line most people never hear about runs through September 2: right around this date the Sun crosses from
[Virgo's](/virgo/) first decan into its second, so your birth year,
and sometimes your birth hour, decides whether your stretch of the
sign answers to Mercury or to Saturn. The sign itself isn't in
question; this is Virgo whatever the year. The flavor is what varies.

## A birthday on the line

On the Mercury side, the [Virgo Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-virgo/)
runs quick and verbal: the fast diagnosis, the precise phrasing, the
mind that finishes other people's spreadsheets in its sleep. On the
Saturn side, the same attentiveness gains weight, more patience, more
follow-through, a taste for problems that take years rather than
afternoons. Most September 2 people report living in the overlap: a
fast mind bolted to an unusually long attention span, which is a
formidable combination and occasionally an exhausting one.

## Devotion, two ways

In love, both cuts care by doing. The Mercury cut courts with
conversation and remembered details; the Saturn cut courts with
reliability, showing up early, in every sense. Either way, partners
get someone who treats "I've got it handled" as a term of endearment.
Work rewards the blend: research, medicine, engineering, editing, any
field where the first insight matters less than the hundredth careful
step. Deadlines don't scare this date; vagueness does.

## The permanent maybe

The pattern to watch is hedging. A mind that can see both sides of its
own decan line can keep every plan provisional: the apartment that
stays temporary for six years, the almost-commitment, the backup plan
with its own backup plan. Options feel like safety and quietly become
a ceiling. Choosing one good path out loud beats auditioning three
forever.

As for which decan is actually yours: the year and the hour settle it,
the same way they settle your Moon and your rising sign. The
[birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) computes the Sun's exact
degree for your birth minute and reads the rest of the sky while it's
there.
