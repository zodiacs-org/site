July 12 is a border date inside its own sign. The Sun spends it
crossing (or, in many years, not quite crossing) from Cancer's
second decan into its third, so the year you were born, and near the
line the hour, decides which reading is yours: the Mars-toned middle
or the Jupiter-toned close.

## Defender or expander

Both cuts share the [Cancer](/cancer/) core: loyalty, long memory,
care as a first response. The Mars cut aims it, focused,
territorial, braced for whatever's coming. The Jupiter cut opens it,
roomy, generous, always finding one more chair. Most people born
July 12 recognize both settings in themselves, which is exactly what
a birthday on a decan line predicts. The
[Sun in Cancer](/learn/placements/sun-in-cancer/) is the constant;
the emphasis is the variable.

## Two ways to show up

Mars-side, love looks like advocacy: your problems get adopted, your
deadlines get shared, your difficult phone calls get rehearsed with
you. Jupiter-side, love looks like provision: tables set, trips
planned, futures sketched in optimistic detail. Careers track the
same fork: the Mars reading suits crisis work, operations, anything
with a perimeter to hold; the Jupiter reading suits teaching,
hosting, community building, anything with a circle to widen.

## One friction, two flavors

The shared root is care without limits. The Mars version overcommits
to defense, fights joined that were never yours to fight. The
Jupiter version overcommits to supply, promises the calendar cannot
honor. Either way the fix is the same: ration the caretaking to what
you can sustain, and let a few things go unguarded and unfed.
