By January 21 the crossing is usually complete: in the great
majority of years in the table above, the whole day belongs to
[Aquarius](/aquarius/). A handful of years are the exception. The
Sun makes its move partway through the day, so a birth in the
early hours can still land in [Capricorn](/capricorn/). What this
date never gets is a full day of Capricorn. Check the table for
your year before taking any horoscope's word for it.

## New air with mountain manners

Even in the clear-cut years, a Sun this newly arrived in a sign
keeps some dust of the previous one on its boots, helped along
here by the fact that tradition gives Capricorn and Aquarius the
same ruler, Saturn. So the typical January 21 reads as [Aquarius
Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-aquarius/) first (independent,
pattern-minded, principled to a fault) with a [Capricorn
Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-capricorn/) discipline holding the
ideas down. The ideals come with schedules attached. The rebellion
files its paperwork. It's a rare and quietly formidable mix.

## Loyal from a little distance

In love, this date bonds through the mind before anything else.
Conversation is the courtship, and freedom inside the commitment
is non-negotiable. Partners who read the spaciousness as coldness
miss the point: the loyalty is total; it just doesn't hover.
Anniversaries may be approximate. The showing up, when it counts,
is exact. At
work, January 21 suits the long reform (modernizing a field,
building the fairer version of an old thing) where both the
vision and the stamina are required.

## Proving the point too long

The friction: a position held on principle can quietly become a
position held on stubbornness, and this date's Saturn double-dose
makes the handbrake heavy. The growth is scheduling reviews of
your own certainties as rigorously as you review everyone else's.
