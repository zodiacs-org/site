Most sites will tell you July 22 is Cancer, full stop. The sky is less
tidy. This is the date the Sun's move from [Cancer](/cancer/) into
[Leo](/leo/) can actually land on, and whether it did depends on your
birth year, sometimes on your birth hour. The table above is the real
answer: in about half the years since 1940 the whole day belongs to
Cancer, and in the other half the ingress falls during the day itself,
so a late-evening birth comes out Leo. If you've never felt fully
described by either sign's horoscope, this is probably why.

## Two signs, one hinge

The last degree of Cancer and the first degree of Leo share a theme,
care, and disagree about staging. End-stage Cancer protects from
close range: private, tidal, loyal past reason. Day-one Leo protects
from the front: visible, warm, allergic to being overlooked. A July 22
birth on either side of the line tends to carry some of both, the
[Cancer Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-cancer/) memory for people
with the [Leo Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-leo/) instinct for the
room. The mix reads as magnetic hospitality: the person who hosts the
gathering and quietly runs its emotional weather.

## In love and at work

Relationships get devotion that wants witnesses. This birthday bonds
deeply and then wants the bond acknowledged, anniversaries kept,
loyalty returned in public, effort seen. At work it gravitates toward
roles with a face: leading a close team, representing a cause,
building something people gather around. Behind-the-scenes positions
work only when the credit still finds its way home.

## The growth edge

The friction is approval. When the care goes unnoticed, end-July
birthdays can swing between Cancerian withdrawal and Leonine
performance, sulking backstage or overplaying the scene. The steadier
move is asking directly for what both signs want anyway: to matter to
the people they've chosen.

Don't settle for the cusp shrug. Your birth year is in the table
above, and if yours is a split year, the [birth chart
calculator](/birth-chart/) reads the Sun's exact degree for your birth
minute, one answer, no folklore.
