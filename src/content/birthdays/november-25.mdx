The truth arrives quickly on November 25, mid-sentence, usually
funny, occasionally sooner than anyone ordered it. This is
[Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) in its first decan, where Jupiter rules
its own sign and the famous frankness runs at full pressure, with
nothing fitted between thought and speech.

## Candor at speed

For a [Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) born
today, honesty isn't a policy; it's a metabolism. The observation
forms and exits in one motion, wrapped in enough humor that half the
room laughs before the other half winces. It matters that the candor
is almost never cruel. The intent is genuinely informational. This
date simply believes people deserve the real answer, and it extends
itself the same terms: ask a November 25 an awkward question about
their own failings and you'll receive the unvarnished inventory,
cheerfully itemized.

## The honest companion

Love with this birthday is refreshingly legible: you will know where
you stand, in real time, indefinitely. That transparency, plus a
stock of enthusiasm that regenerates overnight, makes this date an
easy person to be around and a hard one to stay angry with. What it
asks is reciprocity. Hedged, managed answers read to it as a locked
room. At work, the voice is the career: teaching, commentary,
advocacy for things actually believed in, any role where saying the
clear true thing is the job description rather than the incident.

## Early arrows

The friction is release timing. A true sentence, fired before its
moment, wounds about as much as a false one, and this date's
sentences are always ready early. The growth isn't dilution; it's
delivery, reading the room's readiness before the send, the way
you'd time any other gift. The truth keeps. It can wait twenty
minutes.

The twenty-fifth fixes a Sagittarius Sun and its Jupiter decan.
Whether the candor fronts a public life or a private one is house
business, set by the hour you were born. The
[birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) will show you which.
