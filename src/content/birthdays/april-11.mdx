With April 11 the ambiguity ends: the Sun stands fully inside the
third decan of [Aries](/aries/), the ten degrees tradition lends to
Jupiter. Mars still drives, but Jupiter now navigates, and the
difference shows immediately. This is fire with a worldview.

## Conviction joins the engine

A second-decan Aries fights for its own; the third decan fights for
its beliefs. The [Aries Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-aries/) here
attaches its considerable push to principles (fairness at work,
loyalty in the family, the right way to treat a waiter) and
defends them with missionary stamina. April 11 people often look
like the most opinionated person in the room and are usually the
most consistent: the code was written early and gets enforced on
themselves first. Strangers receive startling generosity whenever
the code says so.

## Big plans, gladly shared

Love with this birthday is expansive: it wants to build a life that
keeps getting larger (more people at the table, more stamps in the
passport, more projects going), and it courts by inviting you into
the growth. The partner who wants the world kept small will always
be negotiating. At work, April 11 is the natural head of anything
with a mission statement it actually believes; it sells, leads, and
rallies volunteers with the same voice. Institutions that ask it to
enforce rules it finds stupid should expect open war.

## The overpromise habit

The catch with Jupiter is size. April 11 says yes at the scale of
its convictions and then meets the scale of its weeks: too many
causes, too many defended fronts, a schedule that assumes a
lieutenant who was never hired. The growth edge is triage. Pick
the two hills that matter this year and let the others be wrong
without you. Conviction concentrated moves things; conviction
scattered just makes noise.

The date does one job and does it well: it fixes the Sun. The remaining cast arrives with the birth certificate, and the [calculator](/birth-chart/) auditions them all in about a minute.
