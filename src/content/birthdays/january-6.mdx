Halfway through Capricorn's arc, the sign has finished arriving and
simply runs. January 6 gets [Capricorn](/capricorn/) at full signal,
no boundary questions at either end of the story, the Sun deep in the
Venus-ruled second decan, midwinter outside and a long climb ahead.
Mid-sign birthdays wear their sign plainly, and this one wears it
well.

## The middle of the mountain

The [Capricorn Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-capricorn/) here is
self-possessed in a way the edges of the sign aren't: nothing left to
prove about belonging, nothing yet handed over. The Venus tone rounds
the corners. This is a worker who notices beauty, an ambitious
person other people actually enjoy reporting to, a realist with good
manners. January 6 people tend to be identified early as "the
responsible one" and spend their lives being handed things: keys,
budgets, secrets, eulogies. They take the handing as a compliment,
which, from this date, it is.

## Partnership as a joint venture

In love, January 6 builds rather than falls. Foundations first:
trust, then routine, then the kind of intimacy that comes from ten
thousand ordinary evenings done together. It's unglamorous and it
lasts. Grand gestures make this date faintly suspicious anyway; it
would rather be shown a calendar than a bouquet. At work, the date's gift is bearing weight without drama:
deadlines, other people's panic, the quarter that goes sideways. The
risk is that competence attracts load, and load keeps arriving until
something says stop.

## The delegation problem

Which is the growth edge: nothing says stop. This birthday does the
task because asking someone else feels slower, riskier, and vaguely
like an imposition, and then wonders why the calendar has no white
space left. Handing work over badly is how other people learn to do
it well. Let them.
