Nearly every August 24 on record is Virgo. The table above gives the
whole day to [Virgo](/virgo/) in all but two years since 1940. In
those two, the Sun was still finishing [Leo](/leo/) as the day opened,
and only a birth in the hours before the crossing comes out Leo. So
the honest headline: you are almost certainly a Virgo, and the table
plus a birth time will close the almost.

## Virgo, with an afterglow of Leo

The [Virgo Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-virgo/) at the sign's very
start carries its mission plainly: notice, refine, be of use. But Suns
born this close to a boundary often keep a trace of the sign just
departed, a [Leo Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-leo/) ease with
warmth and visibility that first-degree Virgos born later in the week
don't always show. The type: meticulous work, presented without
apology, by someone who can hold the room while explaining the fix.

## Care that notices, warmth that shows

In love this birthday runs Virgo's quiet devotion (the refilled
prescription, the remembered errand) with a Leo streak that actually
enjoys saying it out loud now and then. Partners get practical care
that isn't shy about the occasional bouquet-sized statement. At work
it's the craftsperson who can also present: exacting standards, human
delivery. Roles that are all backstage underuse the warmth; roles that
are all stage starve the precision.

## The modesty-pride seesaw

The friction is a seesaw between Virgo's deflection and the leftover
Leo wish to be recognized: praise gets batted away, and then its
absence quietly stings. People around this date receive contradictory
signals: don't fuss over me; why did nobody notice. The growth edge
is receiving a compliment at face value, out loud, without an audit or
an encore.

For nearly every year the table above says Virgo without hesitating. If yours is one of the split pair, or curiosity runs deeper than one sign, [compute the chart](/birth-chart/) and take the degree, the Moon, and the rooms along with it.
