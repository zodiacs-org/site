Ask five sites what sign September 22 is and you'll get five confident
answers that don't agree. The honest answer depends on the year. This
date sits against the Sun's crossing from [Virgo](/virgo/) into
[Libra](/libra/) (the September equinox), and the table above is the
actual record: in about seven years of every ten since 1940, the whole
day belongs to Virgo, while in the rest the crossing lands partway
through the day, so a birth late enough comes out Libra. No year hands
this entire date to Libra, but plenty of individual September 22
birthdays are Libran, decided by the hour.

## One day, two answers

There is no blended Sun sign at this boundary. Before the ingress, a
[Virgo Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-virgo/) is still Virgo: exact,
discerning, practical, and oriented toward making the thing work. After
the ingress, a [Libra Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-libra/) is Libra:
relational, comparative, and oriented toward balance and negotiation.
The date alone cannot assign either description, much less combine them.

## Love and work, once the sign is known

A September 22 Virgo tends to show care through attention, practical help,
and the small repair that makes daily life run better. At work, the same
placement favors craft, analysis, editing, and useful standards.

A September 22 Libra tends to show care through reciprocity, conversation,
and an active awareness of the other person's position. At work, the same
placement favors negotiation, design, mediation, and decisions that must
hold across a group.

## Different growth edges

For Virgo, the friction is often analysis that keeps refining after the
answer is already usable; the corrective is to define “good enough” before
the work begins. For Libra, the friction is often reopening a decision each
time another viewpoint appears; the corrective is to set a fair process and
then let its verdict stand. Which lesson applies follows the computed Sun,
not the calendar label.

So check the table above for your birth year first. If yours is one of
the split years, the [birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) computes
the Sun's exact position for your birth minute, Virgo or Libra,
settled for good.
