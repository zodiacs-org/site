Ask three sites what sign February 18 is and you can collect three
answers; the honest one is a table. This is a date the Sun's move
from [Aquarius](/aquarius/) into [Pisces](/pisces/) can land on.
Slightly more years than not, since 1940, keep the whole day in
Aquarius; the rest see the Sun cross partway through, which sends
late-hour births in those years to Pisces. And no year on record
here hands the entire day to Pisces. The table
above carries the ruling for your birth year. Sometimes it will
send you to your birth certificate for the hour.

## Where air gives way to water

The last degrees of Aquarius are the perfected observer: ideals
refined, distance calibrated, humanity loved in the aggregate. The
first degrees of Pisces are the opposite motion, the boundary
going soft, one particular person's unspoken state suddenly
audible. Born on the hinge, most February 18 people describe
having both: an
[Aquarius Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-aquarius/) pattern-sight
with a [Pisces Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/)
receptivity, or the reverse. The blend reads as the idealist who
feels it, able to diagram the room and still catch its mood.

## Systems with a pulse

In love this mix wants orbit and depth at once: independence
protected, intimacy real, a partner who survives being understood
in detail and still gets tenderness. In work it's the
systems-and-people profile, research with human subjects, design
with an ethic, care delivered through a well-built process. The
weak fit is any job that asks for only one half.

## Thermostat trouble

The friction is temperature control. This date can hold a feeling
at arm's length, analyzing it into vapor, then swing to absorbing
everyone's weather with no filter at all. Cold read, full flood,
repeat. The work is the middle setting: let the feeling in while
it's still information, before it becomes climate.

A shrug is not an answer when a measurement exists. The table above holds your year; for the split ones the [Sun's exact position](/birth-chart/), computed for your birth minute, ends the Aquarius-or-Pisces question permanently.
