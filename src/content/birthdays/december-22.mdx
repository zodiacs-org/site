Most years, December 22 sounds like a settled question: the first
day of [Capricorn](/capricorn/), say the lists. The record is
messier. In a large minority of years since 1940, the Sun is already
in Capricorn when this date begins; in the slight majority, it is
still covering the last sliver of [Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) and
does not cross until sometime during the day. Born that morning in
one of those years, you are an Archer with a Capricorn birthday
reputation. The table above sorts your year, and no December 22 on
record here stays Sagittarius from midnight to midnight.

## An arrival more than an exit

Where December 21 leans Sagittarius, this date leans the other way:
most people born on it carry the
[Capricorn Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-capricorn/), and even the
exceptions were born hours from the line. Day-one Capricorn has a
distinct flavor. The solstice is the year's floor, where the light
starts its climb back, and ambition here is still warm from the fire
behind it. The
[Sagittarius Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) parting
gifts (humor, appetite, faith in the road) do not vanish at the
boundary. On this date, the builder usually gets to keep them.

## Ambition that remembers the road

In love, the blend is loyalty with range: someone who commits like
earth and honeymoons like fire, keeping the promise for decades while
never quite retiring the detours. At work it is the
strategist-adventurer, comfortable with the ten-year plan precisely
because it once hitchhiked without one. The failure pattern is
imbalance: all Capricorn (grind, gloom, postponed joy) or all
Sagittarius (six starts, no summit). The good years use both.

## Discipline versus the door

The friction: when life tightens, this date hears two instructions,
dig in and get out, at equal volume. Jobs and relationships can end
abruptly after long stoicism, or drag on because leaving felt like
quitting. The growth edge is the third option both signs respect:
renegotiate. Change the terms before choosing between endurance and
exit.
