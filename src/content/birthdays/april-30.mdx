April ends on a hinge inside [Taurus](/taurus/). The 30th rides
the boundary between the sign's first decan, ruled by Venus, and
its second, ruled by Mercury. And which one holds your Sun is
decided by birth year and hour, nothing vaguer. One side of the
line is the Bull at its most sensory; the other is the Bull with
tools in its hands.

## The garden and the workbench

On the Venus side, the
[Taurus Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/) is receptive
comfort: appetite, warmth, an ear for what feels right. On the
Mercury side the same steadiness turns productive: craft, method,
the patient intelligence of hands that measure twice. Most
April 30 people report both channels: they want the beautiful
thing and want to know how it was joined, love the meal and ask
about the technique. It makes them the zodiac's natural artisans,
taste and skill in one body, whichever decan formally claims the
Sun.

## Two dialects of dependable

In love, the Venus channel courts with comfort while the Mercury
channel courts with attention, remembering exactly what you said
in March and acting on it in June. Partners get both: kept cozy
and genuinely heard, a rarer pairing than it sounds. At work the
blend points at making: cooking, carpentry, code with
craftsmanship, gardening that borders on engineering. Pure
ornament bores the Mercury side; pure function starves the Venus
side. The sweet spot has both names on it.

## Slow hands, second thoughts

The friction each side contributes: Venus inertia (good enough,
why move) and Mercury tinkering (almost done, never done). On a
bad month they alternate, and the project sits at ninety percent
for a year, too comfortable to abandon and too imperfect to ship.
The growth edge is making done a decision rather than a feeling.
Version two is allowed; version zero forever is the only real
failure.

Which decan actually owns your Sun is a fact with a decimal point.
The [birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) computes the exact
degree from your date, time, and birthplace and hands you the
Moon, rising sign, and houses while it's at it.
