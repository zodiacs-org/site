September closes with the Sun settled a comfortable week into
[Libra](/libra/), past the equinox headlines, deep enough into the
first decan that the sign runs steady. September 30 gets Venus twice
over, as sign ruler and decan ruler, and wears it as reflex: balance
restored wherever this person happens to be standing.

## Balance as a reflex

By this point in the decan the leveling instinct is unconscious
competence. A September 30 [Libra Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-libra/)
evens things without deciding to: splits the bill fairly in their
head while laughing at the joke, makes sure the absent friend gets
defended, spots the lopsidedness in a plan before it's finished being
described. Fairness this automatic looks effortless and costs
attention all day long.

## The fair-terms partner

Love with this date comes with good terms: real listening,
proportionate effort, conflict conducted like both people plan to
stay. A September 30 partner is constitutionally incapable of the
cheap shot, and notices, always, whether consideration is flowing in
both directions. Careers reward the instinct wherever terms get set:
contracts, arbitration, team leadership, planning at the city scale.

## The silent scoreboard

The friction: all that automatic evening-up gets tracked somewhere,
and September 30 keeps the tally without saying so, expecting the
imbalance to be as obvious to others as it is to them. It rarely is.
The unspoken score grows until it tips over as a grievance nobody saw
forming. The growth is naming the imbalance while it's still small:
"I've planned our last three weekends; your turn," said lightly,
before it needs saying heavily.
