## Gravitas in love and labor

Romantically, this date attracts people looking for shelter and
must choose partners who offer some back. Otherwise it ends up
curating everyone's stability but its own. The love, once given, is
absolute and unshowy: presence at every hard thing, zero flight
risk. At work, January 17 rises the way sediment turns to stone,
gradually, then permanently. Institutions trust this person with
their oldest problems, and the trust is repaid with decades of
patience and no scandals whatsoever.
