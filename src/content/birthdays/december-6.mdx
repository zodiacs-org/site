## The launch reflex

A [Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) generates

city, new field: while others research, this birthday enrolls. The
gift is momentum: things happen around these people, and stuck
situations reliably come loose when they walk in. The cost is a
certain roughness of finish. Launches are frequent. Landings vary.

## Co-pilots wanted

to propose the trip, first to suggest the shared apartment. It pairs
best with someone who enjoys being swept up but owns an anchor: the

anchoring turn out to be a durable division of labor. At work,
December 6 belongs at beginnings: founding, opening, piloting, the
first season of anything. Handing the running of the thing to
steadier hands later is not failure; it is specialization.

## Landing is part of the flight

percent, projects one push from done, drafts owed an apology. Momentum
always prefers the next thing. The growth edge is treating completion
as part of the adventure. The trip does not end at the last airport;

unpacked.
