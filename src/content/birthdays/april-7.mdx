The Sun on April 7 is past the middle of [Aries](/aries/) and still
inside the second decan, the stretch ruled by the Sun in the
traditional system. By now the sign's famous speed has been in
training for weeks, and this date shows the result: the classic
Aries burst is here, joined by something rarer for the sign, the
ability to keep burning after the novelty dies.

## Heat that holds its pace

An [Aries Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-aries/) usually gets
described as a starter, and April 7 starts plenty, but watch it in
week five of a commitment, where most of the sign's cohort has
already wandered off. The Sun decan lends follow-through: this is
the Aries that finishes the training block, ships the second
version, stays through the unglamorous middle. Impatience remains
(queues and committees are still purgatory), but effort no longer
needs constant novelty to keep flowing.

## Constancy, unusually for fire

In love, April 7 breaks the stereotype gently: quick to fall, yes,
but slow to leave. Affection settles into loyal routines (the
standing date, the daily check-in), and partners discover the flame
has a thermostat. What it can't tolerate is stagnation dressed as
stability; the relationship must still be going somewhere.
Careerwise this is the campaign runner rather than the stunt pilot:
long builds, seasons rather than moments. Give it a finish line
months out and real authority over the route.

## When persistence turns to pushing

The catch: stamina plus Aries certainty equals difficulty dropping
a lost cause. April 7 can keep driving a doomed project or a
finished relationship on sheer conditioning, calling it commitment.
The growth edge is telling the difference between the middle that
needs pushing through and the ending that needs admitting. A
trusted outside voice helps; from inside a sprint, every wall looks
like a hill.
