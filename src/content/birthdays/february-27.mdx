A week into Pisces, the water is already deep. February 27 sits late in the first decan, still under Jupiter's expansive management, still the
expansive, faith-carrying stretch, but far enough in that the
inwardness has fully arrived. This is the quiet-generous cut of
[Pisces](/pisces/): large inner rooms, door left mostly open.

## Hospitality of attention

People leave conversations with this date feeling unusually
heard, and they're right. A February 27
[Pisces Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/) listens the way
Jupiter does everything, spaciously. There's room in there for
your whole story, including the parts you hadn't organized yet;
somehow it comes back to you sorted, gentler, with the shame
drained off. By this point in the decan the imagination has
furniture: a settled interior life that guests can actually
visit, not just hear described. Repeat visitors notice one thing
above all. Nothing said in confidence has ever come back to them
from a third party.

## Room for two interiors

The partner this date needs has an inside, someone whose
silences have content, whose answer to "what are you thinking?"
is occasionally astonishing. It offers enormous attention and
needs real solitude in exchange, and that trade goes best stated
plainly rather than enacted mysteriously. At work, depth beats
breadth every time: research, counseling, editing, craft,
long-form anything. One meaningful conversation outperforms a
day of room-working; managers who measure this person by meeting
visibility will misread them completely.

## The unannounced retreat

The friction: the retreat inward happens without notice.
Partners find the lights on and nobody home (pleasant,
functional, entirely absent) and begin narrating reasons, most
of them worse than the truth. The work is leaving a note: say
you're going under, say roughly when you'll surface, and the
solitude stops reading as abandonment.
