This is the last date that belongs to [Sagittarius](/sagittarius/)
outright. December 20 sits in the sign's final degrees (still
comfortably inside them; the boundary drama starts tomorrow, not
today), with the Sun decan lending its glow to the exit. Signs
distill at the end of their run, and what distills here is the
essence: faith, candor, and the itch for one more horizon.

## An archer at full draw

The late-degree
[Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) has the
whole sign behind it the way a drawn bow has the archer's full reach:
everything gathered, nothing spent yet. December 20 people carry an
urgency their mid-sign cousins lack: plans made as if the window
were closing, truths told as if the flight boards in an hour. It
reads as intensity, and it is, but the source is generosity: this
date genuinely believes the good things should happen now, to
everyone, before the season changes.

## Company for the last mile

In love, that urgency becomes decisiveness. December 20 knows early
and says so, which either thrills a partner or startles them
backward; the keeper is the one who matches the pace without needing
to set it. At work, deadlines are this birthday's native climate. It
does its finest work when something real ends soon, and it organizes
drifting teams simply by refusing to drift alongside them.

## Endings, done honestly

The friction is the exit reflex, sharpened by a birthday this near
the sign's edge. Everything can look finished a little early: jobs,
arguments, chapters with one more page in them. The growth edge is
verifying the ending: asking, before the door swings, whether the
thing concluded or merely stopped being novel. Some of this date's
best rewards sit just past the point where it usually leaves.

The Archer's last full degree is yours without a table or an asterisk. The rest of that night's sky belongs to the clock, and the [calculator](/birth-chart/) reconstructs it to the minute.
