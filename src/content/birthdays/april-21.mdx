The zodiac's most patient sign starts its year here. April 21 is
the first date wholly inside [Taurus](/taurus/), opening degrees,
Venus-ruled first decan, the boundary a safe day behind. Where last
week's birthdays carry leftover sparks, this one starts fresh on
solid ground, a quietly enviable position on the calendar.

## Earth, newly arrived

Day-one Taurus is the sign at its most literal: settle, taste,
build. The [Taurus Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/) here
hasn't acquired the later sign's elaborations. It wants good
meals, kept promises, a bed worth returning to, and the time to
enjoy each without a countdown running. There's a freshness to this
cut of the Bull: the steadiness hasn't hardened into doctrine yet,
and curiosity about comfort (new recipes, new routes to calm)
stays genuinely open. People find April 21 restful the way an
unhurried room is restful: nothing is being performed, and the ease
turns out to be structural.

## Slow love, sound work

Affection here moves at ground speed: interest, then attention,
then roots, no stage skipped, none rushed. What April 21 offers a
partner is presence without performance; what it asks is
consistency, since wobble reads as warning. At work this date lays
foundations, the colleague whose estimates hold, whose midweek
output matches Monday's promise, whose calm makes deadlines feel
less like cliffs. Trades, crafts, kitchens, gardens, care:
anywhere quality accumulates, this birthday thrives.

## Digging in before surveying

The friction of young fixity: April 21 can plant its feet on the
first patch of ground it likes, before checking whether the ground
deserves it, the adequate job kept a decade, the good-enough
arrangement never renegotiated. The growth edge is separating
steadiness from settlement. Stay slow, but survey first; roots do
their best work in chosen soil, and a season of looking costs less
than a decade of settling.

Everything above is Sun-sign weather. The climate (where the stubbornness builds, what it protects, how it greets strangers) is chart-wide, and the [birth chart](/birth-chart/) maps it from your exact hour and town.
