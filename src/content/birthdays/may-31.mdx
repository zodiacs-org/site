May closes on a boundary of the quieter kind: May 31 sits where the
first decan of [Gemini](/gemini/) hands over to the second, so your
birth year, sometimes your birth hour, decides whether your Sun
runs pure Mercury or picks up the Venus tone tradition assigns to
the sign's middle third. No cusp, no sign question; just two cuts of
Gemini, and the timing chooses yours.

## One sign, two seasonings

On the Mercury side, this is the classic quick study: verbal,
restless, question-first. On the Venus side, the same machinery
acquires charm as a directive, conversation aimed at delight,
curiosity that flirts, the social graces upgraded from feature to
purpose. Most May 31 people report living as the blend anyway: a
[Gemini Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-gemini/) whose wit
consistently lands warmer than wit usually does. Strangers remember
the conversation; friends remember being remembered.

## Adored company, wandering calendar

In love, both tones agree: connection happens through talk and dies
of boredom, not conflict. The Venus lean adds a taste for beauty to
the mix (the well-chosen restaurant, the gift with an aesthetic
argument) and a stronger need to be liked, which partners can
either exploit or protect. Work sits happily where communication
meets taste: brand voice, hosting, design-adjacent writing, teaching
with flair, the diplomatic end of any trade.

## Agreeableness and its costs

The shared friction is approval-seeking wearing charm's clothes. A
May 31 can edit opinions mid-sentence to match the room (Mercury
supplies the speed, Venus the motive), and after enough edits the
original view goes missing. The growth edge is disagreeing
pleasantly, early, in small amounts, and discovering that the room
holds. Charm that can say no becomes trustworthy; that's the
upgrade.

May signs off with a Gemini Sun in full stride. Stride toward what is the chart's clause (houses by the hour, Moon by the minute), and the [calculator](/birth-chart/) executes it cleanly.
