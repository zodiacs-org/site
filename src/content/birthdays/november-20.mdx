Scorpio in every year on record here: November 20 carries no cusp
footnote, whatever the neighboring dates get up to. What it does
carry is position: the Sun deep in [Scorpio](/scorpio/)'s closing
degrees, Moon decan, with the sign's whole arc behind it and the
handover to Sagittarius visible ahead, visible, but not, for this
date, in play.

## The sign's last full stretch

A [Scorpio Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-scorpio/) this late tends
to arrive seasoned, intensity that has read its own operating
manual. The obsessive focus is intact but chooses its targets; the
suspicion has mellowed into discernment; the privacy remains and has
stopped being a fortress. People born today often report feeling
older than their year all through childhood, then exactly the right
age from thirty-five on.

## Good at endings, better at loyalty

This date's rare skill is the ending handled well: the job left
cleanly, the friendship released without a war, the chapter closed
with the door unslammed. Scorpio is the zodiac's specialist in
transformation, and the sign's last stretch has had the most
practice at it. In love that reads as depth without melodrama, full
commitment, minimal theater. At work it suits transitions:
turnarounds, successions, restructures, palliative care, the rooms
where something has to end well for anything to begin.

## Leaving first

The friction is preemption. Practiced enders can start closing
things at the first scent of decline, leaving jobs, people, cities
a season before they needed leaving, just to keep hold of the exit.
Not every dip is a death; some things sag and then recover, if
someone stays to see it happen. The growth is riding out one dip per
relationship before drafting any farewell.
