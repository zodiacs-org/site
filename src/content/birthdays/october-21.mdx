statement: everything Libra spent a month refining, present at once,
with the Mercury decan supplying final polish and the last degrees
adding a quiet urgency.

## Everything the sign learned

Late-degree Libra runs the full repertoire (the ease of the Venus
days, the spine of the Saturn stretch, the articulacy of Mercury's)
under a smoothness that hides how much is going on. There's a pull
toward completion here too: this date likes resolving things, closing
loops, leaving arguments settled and rooms easier than they were an
hour earlier. A [Libra Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-libra/) at the
end of the arc doesn't practice the sign anymore; it teaches it,
mostly by accident.

## Devoted, and discerning

Partnership stays the home game, chosen late-sign style: fewer
candidates, clearer terms, deeper hold. This birthday knows exactly
what it's agreeing to, which makes the agreement worth more. At work
it's the finisher: deals signed rather than discussed, drafts marked
final rather than circulating forever, the person called in when a
thing needs to actually end.

## Staying past the verdict

Leaving feels unfair (to the partner, the team, the version of the
plan everyone approved), so October 21 can stay in an almost-right
arrangement long after its own scales have ruled. The growth is
accepting that a fair ruling sometimes ends the case, and that
delivering it kindly is still far kinder than never delivering it.
