The Moon-ruled middle of Pisces gets its last full date on March 9
(from tomorrow the third decan starts pulling), and something about
that position shows in the people: this is the birthday that steers
by feel and rarely runs aground.

## Navigation by feel

Ask a March 9 [Pisces](/pisces/) how they knew (the apartment was
wrong, the hire was right, the friend needed calling that exact
night) and you'll get a shrug where a method should be. The
[Pisces Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/) reads undercurrents as
a native language, and at the deep end of the Moon's decan the
reading turns navigational: hunches here aren't decoration, they're
the instrument panel. The track record is usually good enough that
friends stop asking for reasons and start asking for readings. The
skill isn't mystical so much as perceptual, a thousand small
signals, read constantly, summarized as a feeling before the
conscious mind has filed its report.

## Choosing people, choosing work

In love, the March 9 filter works fast: it knows in the first hour
what more methodical minds take months to conclude. The risk isn't
bad readings. It's overriding good ones out of politeness, then
staying too long in what the gut flagged early. Work rewards this
date wherever judgment outruns data: hiring, early-stage anything,
triage, curation, coaching. It struggles most under managers who
require every feeling to be footnoted before it counts.

## Drift, the honest risk

A gift for currents comes with a temptation: letting them decide.
March 9 can mistake going-along for choosing, and wake up mid-river
in a life assembled by other people's momentum. The corrective isn't
less intuition. It's pointing the intuition at the biggest questions
on purpose: is this the work, the town, the person? The gut answers
those too, if formally asked.
