## Conviction wearing good manners

## The velvet no

The friction: pleasant refusal is still refusal, and the pleasant
part can hide how load-bearing the no is. Partners discover walls

about it" was a burial. The work is honest labeling. Flag which
positions are actually open, and let the closed ones be visibly,
kindly closed.
