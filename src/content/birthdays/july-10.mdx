This is the deep middle of [Cancer](/cancer/): July 10 puts the Sun
well into the second decan, past every boundary question the month
opens and closes with. Dates like this get the sign entire, and in
this stretch tradition adds Mars, which gives the tenderness a
working strength.

## The center of the sign

July 10 people become fixed points. Not loud ones, the gravitational
kind: the friend whose kitchen is the default meeting place, the
sibling who keeps the family's calendar and its history, the one
everybody quietly assumes will still be there in twenty years. A
[Cancer Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-cancer/) at mid-sign holds
people the way a keel holds a boat, unseen, constant, decisive in
weather.

## The anchor in every room

In love, the offer isn't drama; it's permanence, the partner who
makes hard years survivable and ordinary ones warm. Anyone dating a
July 10 should know the commitment arrives early and deepens
annually. At work the pattern is institutional memory and de facto
deputyship: the person who actually runs the place, whatever the
title says. Organizations discover the dependence exactly once, in
the two weeks this person is away.

## Gravity has a downside

Everyone leans on the anchor; nobody thinks to check what the anchor
is carrying. The cost of permanent dependability is that your own bad
week has no scheduled slot. The growth is letting someone carry you
occasionally, out loud, by request, before the strain does the
asking for you. The people who orbit you would mostly be glad to.

An anchor is only as good as the seabed under it. The date sets the anchor; your birth hour maps the ground (Moon, rising, the harbors of the houses), and the [chart](/birth-chart/) draws the whole seabed survey.
