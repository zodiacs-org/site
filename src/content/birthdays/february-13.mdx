Fairness is the tell for this birthday. February 13 sits in the
Venus-ruled final decan of [Aquarius](/aquarius/), the arbiter's
planet layered over the zodiac's systems auditor, and the result
is a person constitutionally unable to watch a thumb rest on a
scale, anyone's scale, including their own side's.

## The arbiter's instinct

An [Aquarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-aquarius/) already
audits rules for a living; Venus's stretch adds a feel for the
people the rules land on. February 13 notices who hasn't spoken,
whose line got repeated without credit, which policy is neutral on
paper and lopsided in practice. The objections arrive calm and
specific, which unsettles rooms far more than anger would. Friends
learn to bring this person their disputes the way you'd bring a
watch to a watchmaker, expecting precision, and getting it. The
rulings hold up, too, because both parties got heard all the way
out.

## Fair play at close range

In love the reciprocity is scrupulous, occasionally to the point
of arithmetic: whose turn, whose sacrifice, whose family this
year. The generous reading is true (nobody will guard your
interests more honestly), though partners sometimes wish the
ledger would take a weekend off. At work the fit is anywhere
judgment must be trusted: review boards, arbitration, policy,
refereeing in every sense, team leadership where the calls have to
be clean. Reputation compounds fast, because people talk about a
fair one.

## Justice without a verdict

The friction: living as referee means never quite playing.
Neutrality can become a perch (above the dispute, outside the
mess, exempt from wanting things) and some conflicts require a
side, including, sometimes, your own. The work is partiality on
your own behalf: stating a preference before the poll closes, and
letting it be as loud as anyone's.
