Tradition prints May 21 as Gemini's opening day, and sometimes the
sky agrees. In about one year out of four, the Sun is already in
[Gemini](/gemini/) when the date begins. In most years, though, the
crossing happens somewhere inside May 21 itself, at whatever hour
the ephemeris pleases, leaving the early part of the day to
[Taurus](/taurus/) and the rest to the Twins. Check the table above:
a sign means the whole day was one thing; a time means your birth
hour decides.

## Twins with topsoil

Whichever side of the line, May 21 people describe a similar mix:
Gemini's appetite for input planted in Taurus soil. The [Gemini
Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-gemini/) curiosity is present (the
questions, the tabs, the talking to strangers), but it moves at a
walkable tempo and prefers its ideas to end up as objects. Call it
the difference between wind and weather vane: this birthday
registers every current while staying bolted to the roof. The
[Taurus Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/) contribution is
exactly that bolt.

## Conversation you can build on

In love the blend is disarming: chatty warmth that actually follows
through. This date courts by talking, then shows up Saturday with
the tools, which is not a combination partners forget. Work sits
happily where language meets material: explaining products,
documenting a craft, negotiating over real goods, teaching hands-on
subjects. Pure abstraction bores the earth half; pure repetition
suffocates the air half.

## When the halves trade vices

The friction shows up as vice-swapping: Taurus stubbornness
defending Gemini whims (a fleeting interest suddenly held like a
fortress) or Gemini restlessness undermining Taurus commitments
right at the boring-but-crucial middle. The growth edge is matching
the tool to the task: hold hard to the things chosen on purpose,
stay light about the ones that drifted in. Sorting which is which
takes about one honest evening.

Two signs, one date, and an answer that exists: the table above for
your year, and, for split years, the [birth chart
calculator](/birth-chart/) for your exact minute, which settles the
Sun and maps the Moon, rising sign, and houses in the same pass.
