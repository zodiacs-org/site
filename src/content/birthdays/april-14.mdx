A birthday in the heart of the Jupiter decan comes with an extra
setting most of [Aries](/aries/) lacks: the urge not just to do the
thing, but to bring people along and narrate the route. April 14 is
the sign's teacher-explorer, first up the hill, then back down for
the stragglers. Ask its friends: nobody leaves this person's
company without a recommendation to follow up on.

## Fire that hands you a map

The third-decan [Aries Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-aries/) pairs
Mars confidence with Jupiter's instinct for meaning, and the blend
naturally mentors. April 14 people collect younger colleagues,
coach rec leagues, explain their craft to anyone who asks and
several who didn't. The generosity is real (knowledge here is for
spending, not hoarding), and so is the enjoyment of the podium. At
its best this date raises everyone's ceiling; even its bragging
tends to be instructional. Watch one explain something twice. The
second pass is always better, because teaching is itself the craft
being practiced.

## Growing people, growing projects

In love, April 14 shows devotion by throwing itself behind a
partner's becoming: the course encouraged, the draft read, the pep
talk at two in the morning. It must guard against turning the
relationship into a syllabus. Partners want to be loved as they
are, not only as they're progressing. At work the fit is anywhere
growth is the product: education, coaching, onboarding, team
leadership, the demanding-but-beloved manager whose alumni stay in
touch for decades.

## Preaching past the bell

The friction is volume of counsel. Advice keeps flowing after the
request has ended, and April 14 can mistake an audience's
politeness for appetite. The growth edge is the closed mouth as a
form of respect: let people be wrong at their own speed sometimes.
It's how this date learned everything it now teaches. Mentorship,
done well, includes the mentor going quiet.

The date fixes a Jupiter-decan Aries Sun and stops there. Whether
the teaching lands in classrooms, kitchens, or boardrooms is house
business, set by birth hour. The
