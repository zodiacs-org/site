Every quick reference files May 20 under Taurus, and in most years
the whole day is. But this is a date the Sun's move into
[Gemini](/gemini/) can land on (it does in roughly one year out of
four), and when it does, the day splits: births before the crossing
are [Taurus](/taurus/), births after it are Gemini. The table above
carries the year-by-year verdict; if your year shows a time, the
hour on your birth certificate casts the deciding vote.

## The Bull's last word, the Twins' first

End-stage Taurus and day-one Gemini make stranger neighbors than
most boundary pairs: fixed earth handing to mutable air, the keeper
meeting the courier. Yet May 20 people on either side tend to report
the same blend, the [Taurus
Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/) groundedness threaded with
the [Gemini Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-gemini/) itch to know.
It reads as the practical person who asks real questions: settled in
body, restless in mind, equally at home in the workshop and the
group chat.

## Steady hands, roving attention

In love this blend offers reliability that never goes stale, the
partner who both remembers the anniversary and brings something new
to say at it. What it needs back is someone fluent in both channels,
comfort and conversation, because starving either one makes the
whole arrangement sag. At work, boundary births thrive at
translation points: teaching a craft, selling something real,
writing about physical things, managing makers. One foot in matter,
one in language.

## The tug-of-war inside

The friction is an internal disagreement about pace. The Taurus half
votes to stay, finish, and settle; the Gemini half is already
skimming the exits, and decisions can deadlock for months while the
two sides argue. The kind move is dividing the territory: let home
and money run on earth time, and give a project or two to air. Both
halves keep a domain; neither runs the whole government.
