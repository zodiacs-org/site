September opens with the Sun already well into [Virgo](/virgo/), still
inside the sign's first decan, the stretch tradition assigns to
Mercury, which also rules the sign itself. That doubling is the note
worth knowing: no other part of Virgo is this purely Mercurial.
Analysis, language, and the itch to improve things run undiluted here,
without the ballast the later decans add.

## Mercury, twice over

A [Virgo Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-virgo/) already runs on
noticing; the first decan turns the dial up. September 1 people tend
to be quick, quick to spot the flaw, quick to phrase it, quick to see
the second-order consequence nobody else has reached yet. The mind
works like a workshop with the lights always on. It makes them superb
explainers and fixers, the colleague who understands the tangled
situation in one pass. The cost of all that speed is that the machine
rarely idles: even at rest, something is being mentally reorganized.

## Love in the details

Affection from this birthday is specific. Not grand declarations but
the right question at the right moment, the errand handled before it
was mentioned, the memory for exactly what you said in March. Partners
who want performance may miss it; partners who want to be known will
feel unusually seen. At work, the fit is anywhere information needs
sorting into sense: editing, analysis, diagnostics, teaching,
operations. Give this date a vague brief and it will return a plan
with the ambiguities flagged.

## The loop that won't close

The friction is rumination. With Mercury doubled, a decision can be
examined from twelve angles, and then twelve more, until examining has
quietly replaced deciding. The tell is research that continues after
the answer is clear. The move is a deadline (self-imposed, slightly
too soon), because this mind does its best work against a constraint
and its worst work in an open loop.
