Pisces season begins around this date, with the accent on
"around." In a large share of years since 1940, February 19 starts
with the Sun still in [Aquarius](/aquarius/) and ends with it in
[Pisces](/pisces/), the changeover landing somewhere in between;
in the remaining years the whole day is already Pisces. No year
gives the full day to Aquarius. So the question splits cleanly:
the table above answers for your birth year, and in the split
years your birth hour finishes the job.

## First water after long air

Day-one Pisces arrives like thaw, feeling before form, mercy
before assessment, imagination running under everything. Final-
degree Aquarius leaves like frost: shapes held precise to the very
end. A birth on this hinge tends to carry the
[Pisces Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/) porousness braced
by an [Aquarius Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-aquarius/)
architecture: tides, but with sea walls; empathy that arrives
with a diagram. Friends get read accurately and treated fairly, a
pairing neither sign manages alone.

## Feeling with an operations manual

In love this blend notices everything and then wants to discuss
the noticing: closeness with fresh air in it, devotion that never
turns into surveillance. It needs a partner who can handle both
registers: the 1 a.m. confession and the next morning's whiteboard.
At work it's the empath who can build the system: care professions
with an operations brain, art with a shipping schedule, product
roles that translate what users feel into what gets made.

## Two exits, both open

The friction: this date holds two escape routes (up into theory,
out into daydream) and fluency in both means a conflict can be
dodged for years, elegantly, without a single raised voice. The
work is choosing the room over either exit when it matters: stay,
say the unabstracted thing, let it be uncomfortable for one whole
minute.

Your sign is a fact with a lookup procedure: the table above for
the year, and for split years the
[birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/), which computes the Sun's
true degree from your birth minute and place. One answer, kept.
