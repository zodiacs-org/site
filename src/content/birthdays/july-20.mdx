Two days from the sign's border, July 20 still belongs to Cancer
outright (late degrees, Jupiter decan, nothing ambiguous about it),
and late-sign Suns tend to carry their sign's widest view. This is
the homebody with a telescope.

## The homebody with range

A [Sun in Cancer](/learn/placements/sun-in-cancer/) this late in the
sign keeps the full kit (the loyalty, the radar, the long memory)
and adds Jupiter's appetite for distance. July 20 keeps a home base
and a wide orbit: the kitchen is sacred and so is the passport. It
nests in order to launch, and launches in order to have somewhere to
come back to.

## Partners in the expedition

In love this date wants a co-traveler who also loves coming home,
the relationship as base camp, not cage. Partners who read the
wandering as flight risk misread it; the leaving and the returning
are one system, and [Cancer](/cancer/) always returns. At work,
July 20 excels at translation across distance: between departments,
generations, cultures, the branch and the head office. The sign
supplies the trust people extend to it; Jupiter supplies the range it
covers on their behalf.

## Restlessness in a homebody

The friction is the oscillation itself: at home, dreaming of
elsewhere; elsewhere, homesick by Tuesday. In bad seasons this
becomes unsettling a perfectly good life out of fear that it's too
small. The growth is enlarging from the base instead of trading it in.
Make the camp bigger, not newer.
