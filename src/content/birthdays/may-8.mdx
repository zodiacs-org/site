Ask someone born May 8 whether the expensive version is worth it and
you'll get a real answer, brand ignored, materials weighed, verdict
delivered. Value judgment is this date's home turf. The Sun sits in
the later reaches of [Taurus's](/taurus/) Mercury decan, where
Venus's sense of worth and Mercury's arithmetic keep the books
together.

## The appraiser's eye

Every Taurus placement has an instinct for quality; the May 8
version can price it. This is the friend consulted before big
purchases, the one who knows which repairs are worth it and when to
walk away from a deal. The instinct isn't greed. It's calibration,
and it extends past money to time, effort, and people. A [Taurus
Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/) wants to build things that
last; this decan's contribution is knowing what they cost. Waste offends
this date more than expense ever will; the two get confused only by
people who shop faster.

## Provider instincts

In love this birthday runs warm and practical: security is the
romance. Expect the emergency fund, the well-chosen gift bought
months early, the quiet covering of a partner's lean season. What it
wants back is being valued accurately. Take a May 8 for granted and
watch the ledger quietly close. At work the fit is anywhere
valuation is the craft: buying, estimating, insurance, antiques,
procurement, appraisal of any kind. The number this date names
tends to be the right one. Ask early, before the purchase, not
after.

## When worth becomes scorekeeping

The friction is auditing the unauditable. Affection tallied, favors
remembered a shade too precisely, generosity that arrives with an
invisible invoice. The growth edge is letting some accounts stay
open, giving where the return can't be measured and deciding that's
fine. The security this birthday wants most was never for sale
anyway.
