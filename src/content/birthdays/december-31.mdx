New Year's Eve is the one [Capricorn](/capricorn/) birthday with a
built-in party, and it comes with a technicality worth knowing: the
date falls a hair from the line dividing Capricorn's first decan from
its second. Which side your Sun lands on comes down to birth year and
hour: the strict Saturn opening of the Sea-Goat, or the warmer Venus
middle.

## Saturn's cut and Venus's cut

On the Saturn side, the
[Capricorn Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-capricorn/) is the sign
undiluted: duty-first, rich in patience, dry as good stone. On the
Venus side, the same spine acquires taste. This cut builds beautiful
things, not merely sturdy ones, and its discipline extends to how a
table is set and how an apology is worded. Most December 31 people
report the mix: a worker's hands with an aesthete's eye, ambition
that cares how the finished thing looks on the shelf.

## Two styles of devotion

Saturn-side love is proven by endurance, years of presence, promises
that outlive their circumstances. Venus-side love adds courtship that
never fully retires; this cut understands that beauty, like anything
built, is also maintenance. Work follows the same fork: pure Saturn
suits law, engineering, stewardship of serious things, while the
Venus lean adds design, hospitality, architecture, crafts where the
structure must please the eye as well as hold the roof. Either way
the Capricorn constants apply: mastery over flash, the long arc over
the loud one.

## What both versions guard

The shared friction is control of the occasion. Saturn manages
outcomes, Venus manages appearances, and a birthday spent hosting the
world's countdown learns both habits early. The growth edge is the
unmanaged hour, a midnight without duties, an evening allowed to
assemble itself. Some of the best ones do.

Which decan claims you is not a matter of taste. Your Sun sits at
one exact degree, set by year and hour. The
[birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) takes your birth time and
returns the degree, the Moon, the rising, and everything else the
party was too loud to mention.
