By February 20 the question is settled: this is
[Pisces](/pisces/) in every year. The boundary negotiations
belong to the two dates before it. The Sun stands early in the
sign's first decan, the stretch tradition gives to Jupiter,
Pisces's own classical ruler. Sign and decan ruler match, which
makes this the least diluted Pisces of them all: the sign at full
concentration, right as winter starts to give.

## The undiluted fish

Everything the sign is known for arrives here at strength. A
February 20 [Pisces Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/)
registers the room's undercurrent involuntarily, thinks in images
before sentences, and extends kindness without checking
credentials first. Jupiter's rulership scales it up: feelings at
landscape size, generosity defaulting to yes, an imagination that
doesn't do miniatures. Where later decans of the sign filter the
intake, this one runs open, more signal, more cost.

## Devotion and the translating trades

In love this date is attentive past reason, the partner who
knows something's wrong from the sound of keys in the door.
Romance means being genuinely accompanied: your inner life gets
company here, not just your schedule. The person who treats that
attention as a utility will drain it. Work goes best in the
translating trades (art, teaching, care, counsel, story), any
job where reading people is the skill itself. Rigid metrics and
hostile floors wear this chart down fast; porous instruments need
decent housing.

## Feelings with no return address

The friction: absorbing states that were never yours. The
colleague's dread and the stranger's grief walk in freely and get
filed as your own, and by Friday the inventory is unmanageable.
The work is sorting: regular solitude, taken as maintenance, not
collapse, until it's clear which feelings actually have your name
on them. Return the rest unopened.

this legible from a calendar. Moon, rising, houses: all of it
waits on the birth minute itself, and the
