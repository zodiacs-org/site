Seven days into March, the fish grows a shell of its own. The Sun on
March 7 sits deep in the Moon's share of [Pisces](/pisces/), and this
far into the decan the tending instinct hardens into something
sturdier: not just noticing what people need, but standing between
people and what threatens them.

## The guardian current

A [Pisces Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/) usually gets
described in watercolors: gentle, dreamy, porous. March 7 people
recognize the description and also know the other thing: the iron
that appears when someone in their circle is in trouble. This is the
friend who answers at 2 a.m. without making it a story, the sibling
who quietly pays the bill, the roommate who walks you home. The
softness is real; it's the perimeter that gets defended.

## Who it protects and what it builds

In love, March 7 gives shelter and needs reminding that it can accept
some. Partners are watched over with a thoroughness that can pass for
telepathy, and the same partner may take years to learn what this
person worries about for themselves. Professionally the guardian
wiring points at stewardship: team leads who absorb blame downward
and pass credit up, nurses and social workers, the ops people who
keep systems alive at 3 a.m. Wherever someone has to care whether it
all holds, this birthday already does.

## The cost of carrying everyone

The pattern to watch is load-bearing without load-sharing. A March 7
can carry six people's troubles for a year and call it a good year,
then wonder, privately and late, who carries theirs. Guarding is
love, but it is not the only love; letting someone see you unarmored
is the harder gift. Start with one person. It generalizes.
