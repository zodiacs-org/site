The first full day inside Sagittarius' second decan is December 3, and
it changes the recipe. Tradition hands this middle stretch of the sign
to Mars, so the [Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) essentials (candor,
appetite, faith in the future) arrive with a push behind them. Where
the sign's opening days wander toward the horizon, this date marches
at it.

## What Mars adds to the Archer

A [Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) converts
experience into opinion quickly; Mars makes sure the opinion does
something. December 3 people are starters: of projects, of debates,
of trips announced on Tuesday and taken on Friday. The optimism is
load-bearing here: it is not that they expect nothing to go wrong, it
is that they trust themselves to handle whatever does. From the
outside it reads as courage. From the inside it feels more like
impatience with standing still.

## Allies at home, missions at work

In relationships, this date wants a teammate more than an audience,
someone to build plans with, argue cheerfully with, and set out
beside. Grand gestures come easily; steady maintenance takes practice,
and the partners who last are the ones who say so plainly. At work,
the best fit is a mission with resistance in it: a problem others gave
up on, ground nobody has mapped, a deadline that scares the reasonable
people. Management that explains why gets this birthday's best years.
Management that says "because policy" gets the two-week notice.

## Impatience and its bill

The friction is the middle of things. Mars-decan Sagittarius starts
brilliantly and fidgets once the novelty burns off, and the abandoned
efforts pile up like base camps below an unclimbed summit. The kind
version of the fix: pick fewer mountains and walk the whole route.
Completion, it turns out, is its own kind of adventure.
