There is a person every group quietly appoints to settle things, who
holds the trip budget, arbitrates the roommate dispute, rules on
whose turn it actually is. October 5, mid-[Libra](/libra/) and deep
in the sign's Saturn-ruled second decan, is that appointment in
birthday form: not the loudest voice in the room, just the one both
sides agree to abide by.

## The appointed referee

The Saturn decan gives Libra's fairness a procedural spine. This date
doesn't merely feel imbalance. It adjudicates: hears both accounts,
asks the clarifying question, and issues a finding people accept
because the process was visibly clean. The respect this earns is real
and slightly lonely; referees rarely get to play. Friends sometimes
forget the arbiter has preferences too, mostly because the arbiter
never mentions them. The instinct shows early. This is the child who
divided the dessert and let the other kid pick first.

## Devotion, drafted carefully

In love, choosing takes time because choosing is taken seriously, and
once made the choice holds through weather that scatters flashier
matches. Partners should learn the house rule early: this birthday
shows love by being counted on. At work it gravitates toward trust:
the final edit, the keys to the venue, the jobs where someone has to
be believed. A [Libra Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-libra/) wants
counterparts; this one wants counterparts who keep their word. Flaky
brilliance holds no appeal here; dependability is the attraction.

## The verdict that stalls

The friction is the open case. Weighing feels responsible, so the big
personal decisions (leave or stay, this city or that one) can sit
in deliberation for years while the options quietly expire. The
growth move is a docket with dates: give each question a deadline,
rule on it, and let the ruling stand unless real news arrives. An
imperfect decision teaches; a pending one just ages.

Balance is the practiced skill; the venues are assigned elsewhere. Courtroom, kitchen table, gallery, ward: the houses of your birth minute make the booking, and [the chart](/birth-chart/) confirms it.
