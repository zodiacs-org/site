Mid-April fire tends to pick its fights on principle. April 13
belongs to [Aries](/aries/)' third decan, the one tradition files
under Jupiter, and it produces the sign's advocates: people
whose temper runs hottest on someone else's behalf.

## The advocate's build

Where the early sign fights to win and the middle fights to be
seen, the April 13 cut fights because it's right, or believes so
with enough force that the difference takes years to notice. The
[Aries Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-aries/) here comes with a
justice reflex: line-cutters get confronted, bullies get named, the
absent colleague gets defended in the meeting they missed. It is
genuinely brave in the specific way that matters most, brave in
front of people who could make its life harder. Power reads to this
date as a dare, not a deterrent.

## Standing up for two

Partners of an April 13 acquire a permanent defender, which is
wonderful in a crisis and occasionally exhausting at dinner. Not
every sigh needs a champion. The relationship works when the
advocacy points outward, at shared causes, rather than inward as
constant correction. Careers with a fairness axis fit best: law,
organizing, medicine, journalism, teaching, refereeing anything.
This date needs its work to have a side it can be on; neutral jobs
turn it into the office ombudsman anyway.

## Righteous and occasionally wrong

The growth edge is the certainty itself. A cause-fueled temper
feels clean from inside (it arrives with a verdict attached), and
that makes April 13 slow to notice when the facts were incomplete
or the target mischosen. The discipline is boring and essential:
check the story before the charge, and when wrong, apologize at the
same volume the accusation used. Credibility spent loudly is slow
to rebuild.
