## Authority that whispers

Composure is the instrument here. This birthday doesn't win arguments

[Libra Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-libra/) is built for the
two-sided view; Saturn adds the patience to hold that view under
pressure, in rooms where everyone else has picked a corner and
started shouting from it.

## The steady seat

keel a household organizes itself around. The need underneath is

pure agreeableness bores this date within a season. At work it
belongs wherever calm runs the room: chairing, mediation, diplomacy
formal or informal, the job of being the person nobody wants to
disappoint.
