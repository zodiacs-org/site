August 22 usually belongs to [Leo](/leo/), but usually is doing quiet
work in that sentence. In roughly four years out of five since 1940,
the whole day sits inside the Lion's last degrees. In the remaining
years the Sun crosses into [Virgo](/virgo/) partway through the day,
and a birth after the crossing comes out Virgo. The table above sorts
the years; if yours is marked as split, the birth hour casts the
deciding vote.

## Late Lion, early editor

The two possibilities are less opposed than advertised. The final
degrees of the [Leo Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-leo/) are Leo at its
most concentrated, proud, warm, intent on work that lasts. The
opening degrees of the [Virgo Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-virgo/)
take that intensity and hand it a red pen: precision, usefulness,
standards. People born on this boundary often describe both at once,
a performer's heart with an editor's conscience, wanting the thing to
be admired and to be right.

## Hearts kept, standards kept

In love the blend shows as devoted maintenance: grand gestures on the
anniversaries, small repairs in between, the partner celebrated in
public and looked after in detail. It's a formidable combination when
appreciated and a quietly resentful one when not. At work this
birthday polishes what it performs: the presentation rehearsed and
fact-checked, the launch both dazzling and on budget. Careers that
reward finish, craft with an audience, suit it best.

## The applause-perfection knot

The friction is a double bind: Leo's need to be seen tied to Virgo's
certainty that the work isn't ready to be seen yet. It shows up as the
project polished past its deadline, the compliment deflected on
technical grounds. The kindly fix is separating the two judges. Let
the crowd enjoy what the editor already knows could be better.

The table above answers most years outright. For the split ones, or if you'd rather hold the degree than the folklore, the [calculator](/birth-chart/) fixes the Sun's true degree for your birth minute and closes the case.
