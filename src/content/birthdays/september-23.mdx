The September equinox usually falls on this date, and it isn't
background scenery. The equinox is the very moment the Sun leaves
[Virgo](/virgo/) and enters [Libra](/libra/). That puts September 23
birthdays right at the hinge. In most years in the table, roughly
seven in ten, the crossing happens during this day, so the morning
can belong to Virgo and the evening to Libra. In the remaining years
the whole day is already Libra. Not one year keeps the entire day
Virgo, which is why the table above, plus your birth hour, is the only
honest way to answer the sign question here.

## The equinox in a birthday

There's a fitting symmetry in being born while daylight and dark run
level, because the two candidate signs balance each other the same
way. The late [Virgo Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-virgo/) is
precision: what's true, what works, what needs fixing. The new
[Libra Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-libra/) is proportion: what's
fair, what fits, what the other person sees. Most September 23 people
report the blend regardless of their side of the line: an exacting
mind with a diplomat's steering.

## Partnership, either way

Both signs take relationships seriously; they differ on method. The
Virgo current cares by maintaining: errands run, health guarded, the
detail noticed. The Libra current cares by attending: fair terms,
real listening, the well-composed evening. Partners of this date tend
to get both, alternating. At work the combination suits
quality-plus-people roles: client services with actual standards, law,
editing, design review, product taste-keeping.

## A choice everyone must like

The friction: this birthday wants its decisions to be simultaneously
correct (the Virgo demand) and universally approved (the Libra one),
a standard reality rarely offers. Choices get polled, refined, and
re-polled while the option expires. The growth is accepting that a
right call someone dislikes is still a right call. Consensus is
lovely; it is not a prerequisite.
