Ask around about this birthday and the first report is generosity, with logistics attached: actual meals, actual rides, the spare room
genuinely offered. July 14 sits in Cancer's third decan (Jupiter's,
by the old count), where the sign's care acquires surplus.

## The open-handed Crab

A [Sun in Cancer](/learn/placements/sun-in-cancer/) tends people by
instinct; the Jupiter tone tends them at scale and without ceremony.
July 14 cooks for twelve when five confirmed, keeps the freezer half
full of other people's future emergencies, and treats abundance less
as luxury than as weatherproofing: enough soup, enough blankets,
enough time, so that nobody it loves ever meets a shortage alone.

## Feast logic in love and work

Partners of a July 14 get provisioning as a love language, the
favorite dish learned by heart, the tank quietly refilled. What this
birthday wants back is participation: a co-host, not an audience,
someone who stacks chairs at the end of the night. At work,
[Cancer](/cancer/) of this cut runs toward feeding systems in every
sense (hospitality, food, education, operations with a human face)
and excels wherever the job is making sure everyone has what they
need slightly before they need it.

## Giving until it tilts

The lean here is one-directional flow. Give long enough without
receiving and the imbalance stops being visible to anyone. Other
people quit offering because the answer was always "I'm fine." The
growth is accepting help badly and often until you get better at it,
and letting someone else bring the dessert without renegotiating the
menu.

The Sun describes the giving; the rest of the chart says where it
flows and what refills it. A date, a time, and a place are the whole
recipe. The [birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) takes all three.
