The write-ups mostly miss this Taurus: the one whose advice you end up quoting. May 2 sits in the sign's second
decan, the stretch traditional astrology gives to Mercury, and the
borrowed planet shows. This is [Taurus](/taurus/) with its senses
intact and its commentary running, steady on the outside, sorting
everything into usable order on the inside.

## The counselor's cut of the sign

The base layer is standard fixed earth: patience, appetite, loyalty
with a long half-life. Mercury adds articulacy. May 2 people are the
friends whose advice sounds obvious the moment it's said and somehow
wasn't before, practical minds that turn a tangle into a short
list. A [Taurus Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/) forms
opinions slowly and holds them firmly; this decan learns to say them
well, which is why people keep asking.

## Slow to commit, easy to keep

Love moves at Bull speed: a long appraisal, then a decision that
stays decided. What the Mercury tone contributes is conversation:
this is a Taurus that courts by talking, wants a partner worth
debating dinner with, and files away what you said in March. At work
the same mix suits roles where sense gets made of material things:
buying, appraising, advising, teaching a craft. Fast-pivot cultures
grate; considered ones promote this date eventually and permanently.

## Advice given, advice refused

The friction: a mind this good at counsel can be strangely deaf to
it. May 2 birthdays hand out clarity generously and receive
suggestions like renovation notices. The pattern is easiest to spot
in hindsight, which is where this birthday keeps most of its
apologies. The growth edge is symmetry,
letting one trusted voice into decisions already half-made. Being
persuaded now and then isn't a defeat; it's proof the listening runs
both ways.
