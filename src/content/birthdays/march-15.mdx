## Slow-burn love, long-game work

downstream. Sprint cultures misread this person early and regret it
later.

## Outlasting the wrong things

The friction: endurance this good stops checking what it's enduring.
A March 15 can outlast a bad job, a bad lease, a bad silence,
treating the staying itself as the win long after the thing stopped
deserving it. Stamina is a tool, not a verdict. The question worth
scheduling: am I persisting toward something, or just persisting?
