A few dates refuse to pick a decan. October 13 is one: the boundary
between [Libra's](/libra/) Saturn-ruled middle and Mercury-ruled
final third falls right around it, so birth year and hour decide
which stretch your Sun actually occupies. The sign is certain either
way. The question is whether it runs on weight or on wit.

## Weight or wit, by the hour

The Saturn cut carries gravity: standards, the long view, Libra as
magistrate. The Mercury cut carries speed: phrasing, the instant
social read, Libra as advocate. Most October 13s report both in
rotation, the quick tongue that abruptly goes formal when something
actually matters. A [Libra Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-libra/)
holds the middle ground either way; the decan sets which instrument
it holds it with.

## How each version pairs and works

The Saturn side loves steadily and builds careers inside
institutions; the Mercury side loves conversationally and works
wherever language is leverage. In practice this birthday tends to
choose partners who can keep up and careers that alternate, seasons
of rigor, seasons of talk. Law, editing, and negotiation keep
finding people born on this date, whichever decan claims them.

## The double edge

The shared friction: judgment plus articulacy equals the perfectly
phrased verdict, accurate, elegant, and harder to forget than it
needed to be. The growth is a matter of aim. Keep the sharpness for
problems and blunt it for people; an October 13 who learns that
becomes formidable and beloved in the same season.

The refinements above are the date's whole testimony. For motive, temperament, and address, the parts biography actually turns on, call the birth minute to the stand and let [the chart](/birth-chart/) examine it.
