By the fourth of May, spring has stopped auditioning and settled
into leaf, dependable light, dependable green. Birthdays here get
[Taurus](/taurus/) in the same mode: fully itself, mid-arc, in the
second decan that tradition gives to Mercury. That pairing (Venus
senses, Mercury words) produces the zodiac's natural critic, in the
honorable sense: someone who knows why the good thing is good.

## Taste with a vocabulary

Most Taurus placements can tell great bread from good bread
blindfolded. The May 4 cut can also tell you how the crumb differs
and which bakery to stop defending. The [Taurus
Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-taurus/) instinct for quality gets
Mercury's gift of articulation, and the combination is quietly
authoritative. This is the friend whose recommendations get saved,
the colleague whose "it's fine" ends debates. The taste was earned
the slow way (years of touching, tasting, comparing), and the words
simply publish the findings.

## Loved carefully, reviewed kindly

Romance with this birthday is sensory and specific: they remember
the wine, the song, the exact blue of the coat you wore. The risk is
that specificity cuts both ways. A partner can feel appraised. The
warmth is real; it just arrives with footnotes. In work, May 4
belongs where discernment is the job: editing, sourcing, quality
control, criticism, curation, hospitality at the level where details
are the product. Pay them to have standards and everyone wins.

## The review that never ends

The friction point is turning evaluation off. A mind trained to
notice flaws will notice them in the vacation, the gift, the honest
effort. And saying so, however gently, taxes the people trying. The
growth edge is scheduled approval: praising the eight things that
worked before the two that didn't. Standards keep their power when
they aren't the only voice.
