November ends at full stride. By its last day the Sun is closing in
on a third of [Sagittarius](/sagittarius/), still inside the first
decan, Jupiter ruling Jupiter's sign, and the birthday inherits
the month's whole acceleration: fire established, direction chosen,
nothing yet gone stale. If the sign's first dates are the spark,
this one is cruising speed.

## Momentum with a heading

By this point in the decan, a
[Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) has usually
converted restlessness into trajectory. The traveling still
happens (geographic, intellectual, social), but it accumulates
toward something: the expertise, the far-flung people who all
somehow know each other now, the map with a route drawn on it rather
than pins scattered at random. People born today are often the
friend whose life looks improvised and turns out, on inspection, to
have had a bearing the whole time.

## Devotion for the distance

In love this date wants a direction held in common, not a schedule,
a heading: the shared sense of where the two of you are pointed.
Grand plans are courtship; revised grand plans are intimacy.
Partners who need certainty about year ten will find this birthday
offers something better than certainty, a genuine willingness for
year ten to be interesting. At work, give it the long route:
projects with legs, roles that widen, work that crosses borders of
any kind it can find.

## Too many arrows

The friction is abundance: three callings, five projects, this
year's two continents, all genuinely loved, none fully fed. Spread
wide enough, even fire cools. The growth is triage by heading: keep
what lies along the bearing, release what merely glitters beside
it, and notice how rarely the released things get missed.

The month's last date settles the Sun and its decan; everything
personal is still open. Hour and city give the Moon, Ascendant, and
houses. The [birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) turns your
exact moment into the whole map, and the map is better company than
a guess.
