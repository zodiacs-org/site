Introductions are a small art form, and May 25 practices it
compulsively: the friend who has a person for every problem and
cannot rest while two compatible strangers remain unintroduced. This
is early [Gemini](/gemini/), Mercury-ruled twice under the
traditional decans, the connector's corner of the zodiac.

## The go-between gift

Some minds index facts; this one indexes people: who knows what,
who needs whom, which two acquaintances would either marry or start
a business. The [Gemini Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-gemini/) is
built for circulation, and the first decan runs it wide open. May 25
keeps alive the loose ties others let lapse, which looks effortless
and is actually a discipline: the remembered birthday, the forwarded
article, the "you two should talk." The pleasure isn't favors owed;
it's watching the current move once the wire connects.

## Loved widely, booked solid

Partners of a May 25 marry the network too, the calendar of
gatherings, the phone that chirps, the doorbell. The love itself is
attentive and light-footed; what it needs is a partner who doesn't
measure affection by exclusivity of attention. Careers that pay the
gift well: partnerships, community roles, agenting, producing,
coalition work. This date's real résumé is its contact list, and the
list answers when called. Few placements turn goodwill into
opportunity so smoothly, or notice so late that no career was ever
planned. One simply assembled.

## A thousand ties, three anchors

The friction is dilution. A thousand warm connections can crowd out
the three deep ones, and May 25 can reach the end of a crowded
Friday feeling strangely unaccompanied. The growth edge is naming an
inner circle and funding it first, the standing dinner, the
no-agenda call. The network survives on light maintenance; the
anchors need actual time. Fifty acquaintances will understand.

Quickness is the date's donation; the itinerary is the hour's. Which borders the curiosity actually crosses shows in the chart your minute makes. [Stamp the passport](/birth-chart/) and go.
