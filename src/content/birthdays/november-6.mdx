## Protection versus permission

The friction: protection extended without consultation becomes
management of other people's lives. Deciding what someone can handle
is intimacy's counterfeit, warm, close, and quietly over their
head. The growth is asking first. Defended adults stay; managed ones
eventually make a break for it, and the guardian is left holding a
perimeter around an empty room.
