No date in November splits more often than this one. In most years
since 1940, the Sun's move from [Scorpio](/scorpio/) into
[Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) happens during November 22 itself,
morning births landing on the Scorpion's side, late ones on the
Archer's, with the changeover hour drifting from year to year. A
handful of mid-century years stayed Scorpio all day; a recent
handful ran Sagittarius end to end. The table above sorts your year,
and if it shows the day split, your birth hour is the entire answer.

## Water handing off to fire

The two signs at this hinge could hardly differ more. The
[Scorpio Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-scorpio/) runs fixed and
deep, attention concentrated, feeling pressurized, trust rationed.
The [Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) runs
mutable and outward, attention roaming, feeling aired immediately,
trust extended on arrival. People born on the hinge tend to
oscillate rather than average out: intense weeks, expansive weeks, a
private depth wearing a traveler's coat.

## Devotion with the windows open

In love the hinge wants two things at once, the total bond and the
open road, and does best with partners offering both: loyalty
without surveillance, adventure without threat. When it works, it is
formidable: passion that can also laugh at itself. At work the
combination reads as investigator-explainer (digging like a
Scorpio, reporting like a Sagittarius), suited to fieldwork,
journalism, medicine with teaching attached, any craft that first
goes deep and then goes public.

## Grip and exit

The friction is the toggle: under stress one hand grips (control,
jealousy, the closed door) while the other reaches for the exit:
the trip, the new plan, the sudden philosophical case for leaving.
Partners can get whiplash. The growth is noticing which reflex has
fired and asking what the fear underneath actually is: being
betrayed, or being caged. They have different remedies.

Whichever side of the line you suspect you're on, don't guess. The
table above knows the year; when the day split, the
[birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) works out the Sun's degree
at your exact minute and settles it for good.
