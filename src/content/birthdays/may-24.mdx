A good question, asked with real interest, opens almost any door,
and May 24 was born holding a keyring of them. This is early
[Gemini](/gemini/), first decan, Mercury doubled: the interviewer's
birthday, the census-taker of whatever room it enters. Ten minutes
in a queue is enough for this date to learn a stranger's trade,
hometown, and current dilemma, with the stranger somehow doing all
the talking.

## Curiosity with good manners

Lots of people are curious; May 24 is curious about you,
specifically, which is rarer and lands differently. The questions
come genuinely wanting answers (hometown, trade secrets, the story
behind the scar), and people leave the conversation liking
themselves more, a feat they credit to this birthday forever after.
The [Gemini Sun's](/learn/placements/sun-in-gemini/) appetite for
information, pointed at humans, becomes something close to
hospitality.

## Asked out, asked everything

Dating a May 24 means being studied, in the flattering sense:
preferences logged, opinions revisited, the follow-up question three
weeks later that proves the listening was real. The reciprocal need
is being found interesting back. Indifference starves this bond
faster than conflict ever could. Work follows the gift: journalism,
research, recruiting, oral history, user interviews, the
listening-heavy end of care work. Anywhere the job is getting people
to say the true thing. Cocktail hours count double.

## The one-way mirror

The friction: questions make superb armor. A May 24 can conduct a
friendship for years from behind the notepad, warmly informed about
everyone and quietly unknown itself. The growth edge is volunteering
material unprompted (the fear, the wish, the embarrassing hope) at
the depth usually reserved for other people's files. Interest flows
both ways, or eventually it stops flowing. The first unprompted
confession feels like walking without the railing; the second feels
like walking.
