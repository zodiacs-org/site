About one person in 1,500 has this birthday, and the calendar only
prints it every four years. Astrologically there's no ambiguity to go
with the rarity: February 29 is [Pisces](/pisces/) in every leap year
on record here, with the Sun a third of the way through the sign. The
interesting wrinkle is finer. The date sits almost exactly on the
line between the sign's first and second decans, so birth year and
hour decide whether yours is the Jupiter-toned or the Moon-toned cut
of Pisces.

## The hinge Pisces

A [Pisces Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-pisces/) runs porous:
imaginative, tuned to the unspoken, quick to absorb the weather of a
room. On the Jupiter side of the hinge that porousness turns outward,
generous, philosophical, drawn to meaning-making. On the Moon side it
turns toward people, protective, moody in honest ways, gifted at
knowing what someone needs an hour before they do. Most leap-day
births describe living between the two: a big-picture dreamer with a
caretaker's reflexes.

## In love and at work

In relationships this birthday gives more than it logs. Partners get
imagination, patience, and a person genuinely interested in their
inner life; the risk is that Pisces keeps no receipts and then feels
the imbalance all at once. Work goes best where empathy is the skill
rather than a tax: teaching, design, care, story, anywhere the job is
translating between what people feel and what gets made. Rigid
environments drain this date fast.

## The growth edge

The friction is edges. Leap-day Pisces can treat boundaries as
unkindness and drift into arrangements nobody quite chose. The work is
saying the true sentence a beat earlier (this far, not further)
before the tide does the deciding. The people worth keeping take it
fine.

As for the birthday itself: the chart doesn't care that the date is
rare. Your solar return, the moment the Sun comes back to its natal
degree, lands every year, February 28 or March 1 in common years, and
the [birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) computes your natal Sun to
the arc-minute from the real date, no substitute required.
