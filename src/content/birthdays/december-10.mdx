Ten days into December, the Mars decan of
[Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) is in its late stretch, and the birthdays
here get its most durable cut. The stereotype says fire signs sprint
and fade. December 10 is the counterexample: heat that holds,
enthusiasm with a maintenance schedule, an Archer built for the
marathon as much as the launch.

## Stamina, not just spark

A [Sagittarius Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-sagittarius/) supplies
the ignition; this decan's Mars keeps feeding the flame. December 10
people finish the degree while employed, train through the winter,
stay curious about a subject long after the novelty has worn off.
Their optimism is less fireworks than furnace, steady output, low
drama, surprising totals. People underestimate them at the start of
long efforts and have stopped by the middle.

## The long haul, together

In love, this is the fire sign you can actually build with. The
courtship may start fast (it usually does), but what distinguishes
December 10 is year seven: still planning trips, still asking real
questions, still amused. It wants a partner for the whole route, not
just the scenic parts, and it notices who packs for weather. At work,
hand it the long project everyone fears (the migration, the
multi-year study, the rebuild), then check in occasionally. It
neither needs nor wants a cheering section.

## Learning to idle

The friction is that the engine never quite gets switched off.
December 10 treats rest as a pit stop: grudging, brief, scheduled
around the next leg. Burnout does not announce itself in a
temperament like this. It just arrives as flatness where the
appetite used to be. The growth edge is idling with no finish line in
sight, and trusting the fire to relight. It always has.
