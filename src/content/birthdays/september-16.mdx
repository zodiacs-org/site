Born on September 16, you get Virgo with a finisher's instinct. The
Venus decan runs this stretch of [Virgo](/virgo/), and its signature
here is completion, not just doing the work but landing it, sanded
and squared, the last ten percent most people skip done with visible
pleasure.

## Craft, finished well

Where early Virgo diagnoses and middle Virgo constructs, this part of
the sign completes. A September 16
[Virgo Sun](/learn/placements/sun-in-virgo/) is drawn to crafts where
finish separates good from fine: bookbinding, code, print, kitchens,
woodshops. Even outside those fields the instinct shows: the email
reread once more, the gift wrapped like it matters. People trust this
date with final steps because final steps are where it comes alive.
There's even a physical tell. Watch the hands: something is always
being squared, aligned, brought flush with an edge.

## Love that arrives early

As a partner, September 16 is the one who has already handled it: the
reservation exists, the car is fueled, the thing you mentioned once in
passing appears on your birthday. Affection is logistical and
specific, and it wants to be noticed in kind, not thanked
extravagantly, just seen. Grand gestures embarrass this date slightly;
finished ones never do. Work-wise, roles with visible deliverables
suit best; endless-process jobs where nothing is ever done starve the
finisher's appetite. Their output needs little checking; there's
rarely anything left to catch.

## One more coat

The hard part is knowing when done is done. A finisher's eye can
always find one more pass (the varnish after the varnish), and
improvement past a certain point is just postponement wearing work
clothes. The discipline is external criteria: define finished before
starting, in writing, then obey your own spec. Release on the spec,
not the mood. Three projects nearly done cost more peace than one
delivered and two not started.

Your date settles one placement. The Moon, the other planets, the
angles, the houses: those come from the hour and the town, and the
[birth chart calculator](/birth-chart/) reconstructs them exactly.
