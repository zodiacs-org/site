## The week at a glance

Times are in UTC. The linked pages show them in your own time zone.

- **Tuesday 24 November, 14:53:** [Full Moon in Gemini](/full-moon/2026-11-24/), the Beaver Moon, and a supermoon: the Moon is 360,764 km away, close to its nearest.
- **Wednesday 25 November, 23:37:** Mars moves into Virgo.
- **Saturday 28 November, 12:04:** Venus and Jupiter sit 60 degrees apart, an angle astrologers call a sextile.
- **Sunday 29 November, 11:22:** [Uranus and Pluto reach an exact trine](/events/uranus-trine-pluto-2026-11-29/), 120 degrees apart.

## What astrologers read in it

A full Moon is the Moon opposite the Sun, fully lit. Astrologers read it as a moment when something comes out into the open. This one is in Gemini, the sign of talk and ideas, so they read it as a week for saying things out loud.

Mars, the planet astrologers link with drive, moves into Virgo, the sign of detail and useful work. They read that as energy that wants a checklist rather than a fight.

On Saturday, Venus (what we enjoy and who we like) and Jupiter (growth and generosity) meet at an easy angle. Astrologers read it as a good day for a kind gesture or a plan with friends.

Uranus and Pluto are two of the slowest planets, so their trine on Sunday is read as a background tone for the months around it, not a single day: slow, steady change that is easier to go along with than to resist.

## Try it this week

Write down one conversation and one chore you have been avoiding. Have the conversation by Tuesday's full Moon, and start the chore after Mars changes sign on Wednesday.

## Come back next Monday

The Sun and Saturn meet at an easy angle (a trine) on Monday 30 November, and mid-December brings three turns in a row: Saturn and Neptune start moving forward again on 10 and 12 December, and Jupiter starts moving backward on 13 December.
