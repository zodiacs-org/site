Most of the chart's relationships arrive unchosen: parents, siblings,
coworkers. The eleventh house holds the chosen ones: friends, allies, and
the groups whose membership says something true about you.

## What the eleventh house covers

Friendship first, in the durable sense, not the crowd at the party but
the people who help you move apartments. Then groups and networks: teams,
unions, clubs, scenes, the online community that half raised you. The
eleventh describes how you operate in the plural, joiner or lone
delegate, founder or reliable member, the one who organizes or the one who
shows up early to help.

The old texts called this the house of "good spirit," and gave it
benefactors: help that arrives through goodwill, the friend of a friend
who knows about the opening. And it holds the classical hopes and wishes,
the long-term future you're steering toward. These are the concrete kind
of hopes (the ten-year plan, the life you'd build given the room), not
idle wishing. The [tenth house](/learn/houses/tenth-house/) is the title;
the eleventh is what you want the title *for*, and who you'd want in the
room when it finally pays off.

## The sign on the cusp

Your own cusp sign sets the social architecture. Like every
cusp it depends on birth time; a [birth chart](/birth-chart/) will place
it along with the rest.

Cancer on the cusp keeps a few friends for decades and feeds them
regularly. Aries makes friends fast and in motion, side by side, mid
project, rarely face to face over coffee. Virgo shows friendship by
fixing your résumé at midnight and expects nothing back except that you
actually send it. Scorpio maintains a short list of allies bound by
something closer to oath than acquaintance. None of these is more social
than another; they're different answers to what a friend is for.

## Planets in the eleventh house

The Sun here routes identity through the cause or the crew, the natural
chair of the committee it swore it was only visiting. Venus makes
friendships with the warmth of romances and keeps the circle small,
curated, and lovely. Saturn keeps few friends and load-bearing ones,
friendship as quiet duty, in the best sense of both words. The Moon needs
belonging the way other placements need privacy. The group chat
functions as a hearth, and going quiet in it counts as leaving home.
[Uranus](/learn/planets/uranus/) in the eleventh can bring the wide, odd
network, allies from every walk of life, none of them predictable and all
of them vivid.

An empty eleventh house doesn't sentence you to solitude. The cusp ruler
handles recruitment. The friendships still form; the chart just delegates
the hosting.
