Aries opens the zodiac, and [Uranus](/learn/glossary/#uranus) declines to wait its turn even
there. The most recent era ran from 2011 to 2018 (first crossing in
May 2010, final pass ending in March 2019), which makes this the
signature of a generation still mostly in school.

## How it shows up

[Uranus](/learn/planets/uranus/) spends about seven years in each sign,
so the sign works like a class photograph: everyone born inside the
window shares it, and it describes the cohort’s flavor of rebellion,
not any one member’s character. The personal reading lives in the house
and aspects; the sign just sets the generational weather.

In [Aries](/aries/), the weather is ignition. Aries governs the first
move (who may act, and how much permission acting requires) and
Uranus here treats permission as paperwork filed after the fact. Expect
a cohort quick to start, quick to abandon what stalls, and unconvinced
that waiting your turn is a virtue rather than a bottleneck.

## The argument with permission

Every Uranus generation picks one quarrel with convention, and this
one’s is with the queue. Apprenticeships, seniority, the long on-ramp:
structures that ration beginnings are exactly what a Uranus-in-Aries
cohort routes around. The tradition would predict self-taught skills,
self-issued titles, and careers that enter sideways, at speed, without
checking whether the door was load-bearing.

The bill runs in the same direction as the gift. A generation that
treats starting over as a universal solvent can leave a trail of
abandoned second acts, and Aries lends the rebellion a temper, the
exit made in anger rather than on principle. The mature version keeps
the ignition and adds a fuel gauge: it learns which beginnings deserve
a middle.

## In your own chart

Born between 2011 and 2019, you share the sign with your whole class;
what’s yours alone is the house, which only a birth time can place, and
any close aspect Uranus makes to your inner planets. A third-house
Uranus in Aries interrupts conversations; a tenth-house one interrupts
industries. Run your [birth chart](/birth-chart/) to see which
department of your life holds the ignition switch. That’s where the
generational story becomes a personal one.
