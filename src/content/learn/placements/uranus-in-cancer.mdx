Home is the part of life that’s supposed to hold still, which is
exactly why the tradition remembers [Uranus](/learn/glossary/#uranus)’s visits to Cancer. The most
recent era ran from 1949 to 1955, with a final pass ending in June
1956; the next opens with a first crossing in August 2032 and settles
in May 2033.

## How it shows up

At about seven years per sign, [Uranus](/learn/planets/uranus/) marks
generations rather than individuals. The sign is a cohort’s shared
flavor of rebellion, and the house and aspects decide what any one
chart does with it. In [Cancer](/cancer/), the rebellion is domestic.
Cancer holds home, family, roots, the whole question of where you
belong and who belongs to you; Uranus in this territory produces a
generation that treats every answer as revisable.

Read in the living cohort (mid-century born, now grandparent-aged),
the signature is family redefined as something built rather than merely
received: households in new shapes, roles swapped, belonging practiced
as a verb.

## What gets renegotiated

The Cancer questions are tender ones. What do I owe the people who
raised me? Where is home when the address keeps changing? A
Uranus-in-Cancer generation answers in revisions: the family assembled
from scratch, the tradition kept but rewritten, the nest built to be
portable. Security stops being a place and becomes a skill.

The friction is the point. Cancer clings and Uranus bolts, so the
cohort lives inside the negotiation between them. At its worst that
looks like flight from every comfort just as it settles; at its best,
a talent for turning strangers into kin that steadier generations
quietly envy.

## In your own chart

If Uranus occupies Cancer in your chart, you’re likely of the
mid-century cohort yourself, reading a parent’s or grandparent’s chart,
or preparing for a child born after 2033. Either way the sign belongs
to the era; the house belongs to the person, the room where a
generation’s renovation of home became one particular floor plan. Run
the [birth chart](/birth-chart/) and find the house first; the aspects
to the inner planets then say how loudly the theme plays.
