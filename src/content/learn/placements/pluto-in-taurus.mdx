Nowhere does [Pluto](/learn/glossary/#pluto) move more slowly. Taurus sits at the far end of the
planet’s range (around three decades for one crossing), and the pace
suits the territory: transformations of the material world don’t
hurry.

## How it shows up

A Pluto sign belongs to everyone born under it. The eccentric orbit
holds the planet in each sign for 12 to 31 years, marking a whole
cohort’s
relationship to power and transformation while the house and aspects
handle the personal part. [Pluto](/learn/planets/pluto/) in
[Taurus](/taurus/) hands that cohort the heaviest portfolio: land,
food, ownership, security, the base layer everything else stands on.

No one alive carries it. The previous crossing ended in the nineteenth
century, and the next begins with two brief passes in 2095 and 2096
before settling in from March 2097 and running past the end of the
century.

## What owning is for

Pluto transforms whatever a sign values, and Taurus values what can be
held: ground, harvests, houses, reserves. Eras with Pluto here
renegotiate who owns the base layer, and the nineteenth-century
version of that argument redrew farms, factories, and fences across
several continents, a reminder that questions about soil and labor
are power questions wearing work clothes.

The pattern any Taurus cohort tends to repeat, past or future:
security pursued until it becomes its own kind of power, followed by
the slow discovery of what is actually worth keeping. The failure mode
is holding for holding’s sake. The mature version knows the difference
between a foundation and a hoard.

## In your own chart

Everyone reading this has Pluto somewhere else, and the house it
occupies is where its generational theme becomes your particular
ground. A second-house Pluto makes the security question direct,
while an eleventh-house one works the same appetite for permanence
through groups and long alliances. Run your
[birth chart](/birth-chart/) to locate the house, and let aspects to
your inner planets say how loudly the theme runs. As for Pluto in
Taurus itself, file it under history and patience: the charts that
carry it are your great-great-grandparents’, and the next children to
inherit it arrive in 2095.
