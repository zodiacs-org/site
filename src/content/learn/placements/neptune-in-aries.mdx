[Neptune](/learn/glossary/#neptune) crossed into Aries on March 30, 2025, backed into Pisces for one
last winter, and settled in for good on January 26, 2026. Nearly everyone
born with this placement is still learning to walk. Neptune spends about
14 years in a sign, so the sign belongs to a generation (it names what a
whole cohort will idealize, dissolve, and dream in), while the house and
aspects in an individual chart decide how much of it any one person
carries.

## How it shows up

[Neptune](/learn/planets/neptune/) is the collective imagination;
[Aries](/aries/) is the territory of firsts: nerve, independence, the
act taken alone before anyone approves it. A generation raised under this
sky tends to make heroes of people who start things: the founder, the
volunteer, the one who went first and explained later. Where the Pisces
cohort just before them absorbed everything, this one aims. Expect their
stories, games, and causes to organize around the moment of stepping
forward, and expect them to treat courage as the thing everyone is
quietly assumed to owe.

The era runs to May 2038, with a last pass from October 2038 to March
2039, which makes this the Neptune we are all inside right now. The
ideals are being installed in real time.

## The glamour and the fog

What this cohort will romanticize: the clean break, the self-made
escape, the fight that finally settles it. What the fog blurs: the
difference between anger and clarity, between momentum and direction.
Neptune idealizes whatever sign it occupies, and Aries idealized is
heroism, genuinely beautiful, right up until every disagreement feels
like a war of liberation. The mature expression keeps the nerve and
drops the crusade. The unripe one finds a new enemy each week and calls
it purpose. Watch the era’s advertising: whatever it sells, it will sell
as bravery.

## In your own chart

If you were born since 2025, or you’re reading for someone who was, the
sign is the cohort’s and the house is the story: an Aries Neptune in the
third house dreams in dares and headlines; in the tenth, the dream is a
public role. Aspects to the Sun, Moon, or Mercury turn the generational
tide into a personal current. [Run a birth chart](/birth-chart/) to see
the house and the contacts. For everyone older, this Neptune is a
transit, the same fog, experienced as years rather than as character.
