Between late 1981 and 1988 (first toe across in February 1981, final
pass ending that December), [Uranus](/learn/glossary/#uranus) moved through Sagittarius, and the
generation born under it is now in its late thirties and forties. Its
assignment, as the tradition reads it: take everything filed under
“belief” and check it against the horizon.

## How it shows up

[Uranus](/learn/planets/uranus/) takes about seven years per sign, so
the sign marks a cohort, not a character: the shared flavor of a
generation’s rebellion, personalized only by house and aspect. In
[Sagittarius](/sagittarius/), the rebellion aims high and far: at
religion, universities, publishing, nations, every institution that
claims to know what’s true and where the edges are.

The signature is conviction held loosely and distance held cheap, a
generation that migrates between countries, careers, and cosmologies
with a facility that alarmed its elders, and that treats “because we’ve
always believed it” as an opening bid.

## What this generation questions

Dogma first, sacred and secular both. A rigid atheism reads as just
another catechism to this placement. Credentials second: the notion
that understanding lives only where it’s accredited. Distance third,
meaning the assumption that where you started should bound where you
end up.

The risk is churn. Beliefs swapped at Uranian speed can harden into
their own orthodoxy, the fixed principle that nothing may stay fixed,
and a permanent seeker can mistake motion for meaning. The mature
version still travels, but it starts keeping whatever survives the
trips: a working faith assembled from tested parts.

## In your own chart

Born between 1981 and 1988, you share the sign with your whole cohort,
and, at the moment, the checkpoint: these are the years that generation
crosses its Uranus opposition, the review of deferred freedoms that
arrives around 42. What’s individual is the house and the aspects. Run
your [birth chart](/birth-chart/) to find the room where the horizon
argument happens in your life, and which of your inner planets Uranus
argues with most.
