Every [Pluto](/learn/glossary/#pluto) generation inherits a question. The one born between 1995
and 2008 got a load-bearing one: what is worth believing, and who gets
to say so?

## How it shows up

[Pluto](/learn/planets/pluto/) crosses a sign in anywhere from 12 to 31
years (the orbit is lopsided), so its sign never describes a person. It
describes a generation’s shared project, and leaves the personal reading
to the house and aspects. This generation’s project is
[Sagittarius](/sagittarius/) territory: belief, meaning, truth, and the
distance between here and elsewhere. Pluto entered in January 1995,
settled in that November, and left in January 2008, returning once from
June to November of that year, thirteen years of births raised with
every worldview on earth visible at once, each in earshot of the others.

The generational tell is fluency in other people’s certainties. These
are people with an ear for how belief sounds from the inside, a
passport-stamp approach to ideas, and a reflexive suspicion of anyone
claiming the view from nowhere.

## Belief under pressure

Truth used to arrive with credentials (the pulpit, the lectern, the
byline), and power sat with whoever issued them. This cohort watched
certainty get loud and trust get quiet, which is a Pluto curriculum in
itself. Their long renovation is the question of how conviction gets
earned: not whether meaning exists, but what a belief has to survive
before it deserves the name.

The failure modes are symmetrical: irony that never lands anywhere, or
total conviction adopted overnight and defended like territory. The
mature version is belief that has survived its own questioning: held
firmly, worn lightly, and revised in public when the evidence asks.

## In your own chart

Everyone born from 1995 through 2008 shares the sign, so the personal
information lives elsewhere. The house shows where the search actually
runs (in the ninth, through education, travel, and doctrine; in the
third, through the nearer world of neighbors, siblings, and daily talk),
and aspects to your inner planets say how directly it runs through you.
Born near the edges, in 1995 or 2008? The sign was changing hands; run
your [birth chart](/birth-chart/) and let the computed positions call
it.
