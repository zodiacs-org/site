Most [Uranus](/learn/glossary/#uranus) placements break something you can point to. In Pisces the
planet breaks the frame instead, quietly, the way fog takes a
coastline. The era ran from December 2003 to May 2010, with a first
crossing earlier in 2003 and a final pass ending in March 2011, and its
carriers are now teenagers and young adults.

## How it shows up

[Uranus](/learn/planets/uranus/) keeps each sign about seven years, so
the sign reads at the scale of a generation, its common style of
slipping the script, while the house and aspects handle the personal
translation. [Pisces](/pisces/) governs the boundary layer: dream and
waking, self and other, art and faith and escape. Uranus there raises a
cohort that holds every category loosely, including the ones earlier
generations used to define themselves.

The tell is an ease with ambiguity that reads, from outside, as either
wisdom or drift. Labels are tried on and returned; identity is treated
as a medium, not a verdict.

## What the cohort dissolves

The hard walls, mostly: between genres, between belief systems,
between the practical and the imagined. The tradition expects this
generation’s spirituality to be assembled rather than inherited, its
compassion aimed directly at whoever is drowning nearest, its art
suspicious of edges. Where a Uranus-in-Scorpio type exposes and a
Uranus-in-Aquarius type reorganizes, this cohort simply stops agreeing
that the wall is there.

The undertow is real. Dissolving is also how escape works, and a
generation this permeable can drift, into fantasy, into numbness,
into the borrowed feelings of whatever room it’s in. The mature version
keeps the porousness and adds a keel: imagination with a rudder,
compassion with office hours.

## In your own chart

Born between 2003 and 2011, you hold the sign in common with your
cohort; the house is where the water actually enters your life. A
twelfth-house Uranus in Pisces doubles the inwardness; a tenth-house
one makes a public role of the imagination. Run your
[birth chart](/birth-chart/) to place it. The house, and the aspects
to your faster planets, are where this generational tide becomes your
particular weather.
