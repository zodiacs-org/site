Divorce went from scandal to paperwork while this cohort was growing
up, and the cohort, born between 1971 and 1984, carries [Pluto](/learn/glossary/#pluto) in the
sign of partnership. The two facts are not unrelated.

## How it shows up

[Pluto](/learn/planets/pluto/) crosses a sign in 12 to 31 years (the
orbit is that uneven), so the sign names a generation’s shared
renegotiation of power and leaves the personal reading to the house
and aspects. This generation’s file sits in [Libra’s](/libra/) domain:
partnership, fairness, the contract, the other person.

The crossing ran from October 1971, with one dip back into Virgo in
the spring of 1972, continuously to November 1983, and closed with a
final pass from May to August 1984, thirteen years of births, one of
the planet’s faster crossings.

## The terms of partnership

Marriage entered this generation’s childhood as a default and left it
as a decision. Watching adult commitments end (at kitchen tables, in
custody schedules) taught the cohort early that a bond is an
arrangement of power as well as affection, and it has spent adulthood
redrafting the arrangement: partnership delayed until chosen, fairness
negotiated out loud, the household treated as something both people
sign rather than inherit.

The failure mode is a weighing that never ends, fairness litigated
until nothing is left to be fair about, every option held open past
its expiration. The mature version is why this cohort’s committed
relationships tend to hold: terms named, weight balanced, the exit
acknowledged and then deliberately not used. Fairness, on this
reading, is not the absence of power but its most careful
distribution.

## In your own chart

The sign belongs to the generation; the house is yours. A
seventh-house Pluto in Libra makes the renegotiation a lifelong,
up-close project, while a fourth-house one reworks the family
blueprint it grew up inside. Aspects to the Sun, Moon, or Venus pull
the theme from background to biography. Run your
[birth chart](/birth-chart/) to see where it lands, and if you were
born in late 1983 or mid-1984, near the Scorpio border, the computed
positions will settle which generation you actually belong to.
