[Pluto](/learn/glossary/#pluto) is in Aquarius as you read this. It settled in for good on
November 19, 2024, and stays until 2043, so the generation that
carries the placement natally is currently under four, and this page
is written for everyone watching the era from inside it.

## How it shows up

[Pluto](/learn/planets/pluto/) keeps each sign for 12 to 31 years
(the orbit’s eccentricity sets the pace), so a Pluto sign marks a
generation’s shared relationship to power and transformation, with the
house and aspects left to do the individual work. This era opened with
two previews, March to June 2023 and January to September 2024, before
the continuous run that lasts until March 2043; a final pass ends in
January 2044.

[Aquarius](/aquarius/) is the sign of the group: networks, systems,
the crowd, the future treated as a shared project. Pluto here moves
the power question from the top of the tower to the shape of the
network.

## Power in the plural

Pluto eras hand off. The Capricorn years, 2008 to 2024, put
institutions under strain; the Aquarius question is what
replaces the tower: who counts as us, what a crowd can decide, and
what happens when coordination itself becomes the thing worth
controlling. The era’s first years have already set that agenda:
crowds that move faster than governments, and arguments about machine
intelligence conducted by everyone at once.

Reading a Pluto generation in advance is outline work. The territory
is known, the outcomes aren’t. What the sign commits to is only this:
the cohort born now will treat the shape of the collective as the
thing that must be transformed, the way the Scorpio cohort treated
depth and the Sagittarius cohort treated belief.

## In your own chart

Unless you were born after March 2023, your Pluto is elsewhere, and
this era reaches you as a slow transit, worth checking which of your
houses Aquarius occupies. For the children of the era, the natal
reading is easy to set up: their birth times are still on the
paperwork, and the house (eleventh for the theme at full strength
through friendship and cause, fifth for the same current running
through play and creative nerve) will matter more than the sign they
share with two decades of classmates. Run a
[birth chart](/birth-chart/) either way: yours for the transit’s
target, theirs for the house.
