The email is four lines long, and the fourth line is the deadline.
[Mercury](/learn/glossary/#mercury) in Capricorn writes like postage still costs money, and
somehow it never reads as rude, only as finished.

## How it shows up

[Mercury](/learn/planets/mercury/) is the chart’s thinking and
talking; [Capricorn](/capricorn/) runs both like operations. Problems
get decomposed, sequenced, and scheduled. This mind doesn’t
brainstorm so much as triage. It learns inside structures: give it the
map, the syllabus, the standard, and it climbs them in order,
verifying credentials as it goes. The wit is dry and placed, one
deadpan line at the exact moment the meeting needed it, gone before
half the room caught it.

The other tells: texts like telegrams, a calendar that is actually
true, and directions with times attached (left at the second light,
about four minutes).

## In meetings and email

This placement speaks last and often ends the discussion: what
happened before, what it costs, who owns it, when it’s due. It writes
the agenda other people wander from and the minutes that quietly
become policy. Its word is load-bearing. “I’ll handle it” closes a
topic, and everyone present knows it.

The bill: brevity reads as coldness to people who narrate their
thinking, and praise arrives so compressed it gets missed entirely.
From this Mercury, “good” is a standing ovation, but not everyone owns
the decoder. Some colleagues spend years feeling merely tolerated by
someone who considers them excellent.

## The growth edge

Spending words on purpose. The compression is real efficiency, but
relationships run on a little redundancy: the extra sentence that
says how, the compliment delivered at full length once a quarter, the
reasoning offered before the verdict so people can join it rather than
receive it. The other work is entertaining an unproven idea for ten
full minutes before demanding its evidence; some of the best ones
arrive without references.

Even review season gets managed here: this placement treats the three
[Mercury retrograde](/mercury-retrograde/) windows a year as scheduled
maintenance (the calendar goes in the calendar), which is, in
fairness, exactly how to use them. Run your
[birth chart](/birth-chart/) to see which house receives the memos.
