Eleven signs host the [Moon](/learn/glossary/#moon) as a guest. Cancer is where it lives, the
one sign the Moon rules, which makes this the placement where lunar
life runs undiluted, for better and occasionally for wetter.

## How it shows up

Everything the [Moon](/learn/planets/moon/) governs (memory, moods,
care, the reflex to protect) operates at full signal in
[Cancer](/cancer/). Feelings arrive whole and with their history
attached: this placement doesn’t just feel today’s slight, it feels the
pattern the slight belongs to, back to the original. The memory is
famous for a reason. Kindnesses from a decade ago are kept as carefully
as injuries, both filed, both retrievable, neither expiring.

The moods move like tides (on their own schedule, indifferent to the
day’s plans), and the skill of the placement, sometimes learned over
years, is recognizing a tide as a tide: not a verdict, just water doing
what water does. What soothes is care with a direction. Feeding
someone. A door that locks. The small sovereign territory of a kitchen
at night. A Cancer Moon can make anywhere feel like home in an
afternoon (the right lamp, a smell that means safety, one person to
look after) and can feel homeless in a crowded house where nobody
needs it.

## What it needs from its people

To be needed, mostly. The care has to go somewhere; with no one to
tend, it turns inward and curdles into worry. This Moon reads its
people constantly and accurately (the changed voice, the shorter text,
the off week nobody mentioned), and it quietly hopes to be read back
with the same attention. That hope is the placement’s riskiest
assumption. Most people can’t do it, and their failure to guess is not
proof they don’t care.

The shell matters too. When a Cancer Moon withdraws, it is processing,
not punishing. It retreats to feel things at its own depth and comes
back when it’s done. Saying that out loud, in advance, saves everyone
involved a bad week.

## The growth edge

Protection is the gift and the trap. Cared-for people flourish; managed
people eventually leave, and under strain this placement can slide from
the first to the second without noticing, anticipating needs nobody
voiced, then aching when the unasked-for care goes unthanked. The score
is kept silently, which is exactly what makes it corrosive.

The mature version asks. It says *I need* in words, before resentment
writes the script, and discovers the thing that felt least safe was the
safest move available: nobody has to guess, and nobody has to leave.
Care that names itself is this Moon at full strength.
