Capricorn builds the ladder; [Uranus](/learn/glossary/#uranus) asks why it leans on that
particular wall. The generation born with both (first crossing in
February 1988, settled from that December, done by January 1996) grew
up holding the two questions at once: how do things get built, and why
this way?

## How it shows up

[Uranus](/learn/planets/uranus/) spends about seven years in a sign, so
the placement describes a birth cohort, its shared posture toward
convention, while house and aspects handle the personal part.
[Capricorn](/capricorn/)’s conventions are the big ones: government,
corporation, career track, the accumulated architecture of authority.
Uranus here marks a generation that walked into those buildings,
checked the beams, and started taking measurements.

The tell is a double fluency. This cohort can speak institution (it
understands credentials, process, the long game) while privately
keeping a list of which parts are structure and which parts are
ritual.

## What the cohort restructures

The generational project is authority audited for function: leadership
made to demonstrate its legitimacy rather than inherit it, careers
assembled from parts instead of climbed in one piece, institutions
slimmed to whatever actually holds weight. It’s the most patient of the
Uranus signs. Capricorn teaches even its rebels to pour foundations.

The risk is the split life: outward compliance, inward secession, the
employee of the year quietly drafting the exit. Ambition electrified
can also shear, the urge to summit arguing with the urge to condemn
the mountain. The mature version builds the replacement before it
closes the original.

## In your own chart

Born between 1988 and 1996, you carry the sign with your whole cohort;
the house says which building you personally renovate. A tenth-house
Uranus in Capricorn makes a career of the remodel; a sixth-house one
reorganizes the workday from inside. Run your
[birth chart](/birth-chart/) to place the house and the aspects.
That’s where the generation’s blueprint becomes your own job site.
