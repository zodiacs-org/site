Some [Moons](/learn/glossary/#moon) keep their feelings in drawers. A Leo Moon keeps them in the
window, well lit, not for everyone walking past, but for the small
audience that matters, whose attention is half the point.

## How it shows up

The [Moon](/learn/planets/moon/) marks what refuels a person, and in
[Leo](/leo/) the fuel is being enjoyed, visibly, specifically, by the
people this Moon has chosen. It doesn’t require a stadium. It requires
mattering to a handful of people in a way it can actually feel, and it
can tell to the ounce when it doesn’t.

Feelings here are expressed at their honest size: joy arrives loudly,
affection in full production, hurt with the dignity of a closing
soliloquy. What soothes is warmth in both directions: celebrating
someone, being celebrated, making something and having it received.
Home is where the performance is allowed to stop, though anyone who
visits will still get the full hospitality, because hosting is how this
placement says *you matter* with the lights on.

## In relationships

A Leo Moon notices effort the way other placements notice moods, and it
applauds out loud: the promotion, the haircut, the sentence you were
proud of. It needs the same current flowing back, and not as flattery:
flattery is water, and this Moon runs on something more like accuracy.
Specific praise, delivered on time, is the whole vocabulary.

The sulk deserves decoding rather than mockery. When a Leo Moon goes
quiet and stately, it has usually concluded it is invisible to someone
it loves, and pride will not let it ask for the attention it needs.
It can perform *fine* for a long time (chin up, lights on) while
waiting for someone to notice the act. The kindest thing a partner can
learn is to notice early.

## The growth edge

The work is separating applause from love. Applause responds to the
shine; love stays for the unlit hours, and a Leo Moon that has only
ever offered its polished self may quietly believe the unpolished one
is unlovable. It isn’t, but the placement has to test that the hard
way: by being seen tired, unfunny, and unimpressive, and finding that
the room stays warm.

The mature version also learns to ask plainly. Radiating and hoping is
a strategy; a sentence is a tool. *I need some of your attention
tonight* costs one moment of pride and saves whole seasons of silent
arithmetic. When that lands, this Moon becomes what it always suspected
it was: not the person who needs the light, but the person who is one.
