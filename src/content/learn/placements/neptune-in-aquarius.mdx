[Neptune](/learn/glossary/#neptune) crossed into Aquarius in January 1998 and left for good in
February 2012, which means an entire generation grew up while the
network stopped being a place you visited and became the place you
were. At about 14 years per sign, Neptune’s sign is an era’s
signature (what a whole cohort idealizes, dissolves, and dreams in),
with the house and aspects deciding the personal dose.

## How it shows up

[Neptune](/learn/planets/neptune/) idealizes the territory of
[Aquarius](/aquarius/): the collective, the future, the invention that
frees everyone at once. This cohort’s shared dream is connection at
scale: friendship without geography, causes without borders, humanity
as one addressable thing. Technology is not a tool in this imagination;
it is weather.

The idealism is real and unembarrassed. This is a generation inclined
to believe the future can be designed, and that the group (the right
group, fully connected) sees further than any of its members.

## The crowd and the fog

What it romanticizes: the crowd at its best (the swarm that funds the
surgery, finds the missing person, tips the regime). What the fog blurs:
crowd and community, audience and friend, consensus and truth.
Aquarian Neptune can mistake the feed’s agreement for reality and the
network’s reach for intimacy, then feel oddly alone at maximum
connection. The durable gift is cut from the same cloth: a reflexive,
genuine egalitarianism. Strangers are not abstract to this cohort, and
never were.

## In your own chart

The house localizes the dream. An eleventh-house Neptune in Aquarius
lives inside its communities, for better and for blurrier; a
seventh-house one wants a partner who is also a cause. Aspects to the
Moon or Mercury show how deep the era soaked in.
[Run a birth chart](/birth-chart/) to find the house. The sign belongs
to everyone born from 1998 to early 2012; the house is where it becomes
yours.
