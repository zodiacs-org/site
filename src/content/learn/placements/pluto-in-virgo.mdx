[Pluto](/learn/glossary/#pluto) crossed from Leo into Virgo in October 1956, trading the
spotlight for the wiring behind it, and the generation born over the
next sixteen years inherited the least glamorous and most necessary of
Pluto’s assignments: find out how everything actually works.

## How it shows up

The sign is generational, as [Pluto’s](/learn/planets/pluto/) signs
always are: 12 to 31 years per crossing on a lopsided orbit, marking
a cohort’s shared relationship to power and transformation while the
house and aspects carry the individual story. This cohort spans
October 1956 to October 1971, with early returns to Leo before June
1958 and one final pass from April to July 1972.

[Virgo](/virgo/) hands the generation its own territory: work, health,
craft, service, the parts of life that are graded on function rather
than style.

## The repair work

The contested power here was competence: who actually knows how a
thing works, as opposed to who holds the title. The cohort grew up
inside mid-century institutions its predecessors had built and spent
adulthood taking them apart: the workplace, the food supply, medicine,
and the environment all got inspected, criticized, and partly rebuilt
on its watch. Recalls, ingredient lists, and second opinions are its
fingerprints. Where the Leo generation asked to be seen, this one
asked to see the manual.

The failure mode is diagnosis without end, criticism as a permanent
address, perfectionism that never ships, the health regimen as a
second job. The mature version is repair: the quiet, specific,
uncredited work of making a system honest, which this generation does
so habitually it rarely registers it as a skill.

## In your own chart

Sixteen years of births share the sign; the house says which system
became yours. A sixth-house Pluto in Virgo works the theme where it’s
most at home, through the daily job and the body’s upkeep, while a
third-house one turns the fine-grained attention onto language and
the near world. A close aspect to an inner planet sharpens the
generational habit into a personal signature. Run your
[birth chart](/birth-chart/) to find the house. The sign explains the
generation’s toolkit, and the house says where you were handed it.
