Nobody meets your [Sun](/learn/glossary/#sun) first. A new person gets the rising sign at the
door, the Moon somewhere around the third honest conversation, and
only with time does the Taurus underneath become unmistakable: you
were slow to arrive, and now you are not leaving.

## How it shows up

The [Sun](/learn/planets/sun/) is the chart’s core identity, the
person you are steadily becoming, and in Taurus the operative word is
*steadily*. This placement is built for consistency: slow starts, long
follow-through, and an unteachable instinct for what feels good and
lasts. Where other Suns audition new selves every few years, this one
improves the existing model, year over year, until it is unmistakably
and irreversibly yours.

The tells are physical as much as psychological. Taurus Suns trust
what can be touched, tasted, saved, and sat on; quality registers in
the hand before the head signs off. Decisions take a while because
they are being made once. The full [Taurus guide](/taurus/) walks the
sign end to end; what the Sun contributes specifically is durability,
an identity that doesn’t wobble because it was never assembled
quickly.

## Identity on its own schedule

This Sun feels alive around visible accumulation: the garden that
looked like dirt in March, the craft on its ten-thousandth hour, the
home improved one deliberate object at a time. Comfort isn’t laziness
here. It’s the reward system working. A good chair, a slow dinner, an
unhurried evening: that’s the battery recharging in its native format.

The drain is enforced pivoting. Open-plan chaos, quarterly
reinventions, other people’s urgency. A Taurus Sun kept in constant
flux doesn’t adapt so much as erode. Change is genuinely fine, as long
as it was your idea and arrived on schedule; what corrodes this
identity is change administered by surprise.

## The growth edge

Steadiness has a shadow, and it isn’t stubbornness. It’s the moment
holding on stops being strength and becomes furniture. The chair kept
past comfort, the job outgrown years ago, the opinion defended purely
from habit. The work of this placement is reviewing its own
permanence now and then: keeping what still holds you up, releasing
what you are only holding up. Done honestly, that review leaves the
real Taurus gift intact, a self people can lean on because it is
actually there.

Which part of life gets this permanence (money, craft, family,
reputation) depends on the house. Run your
[birth chart](/birth-chart/) to see where your Sun sits and what the
steadiness is currently building.
