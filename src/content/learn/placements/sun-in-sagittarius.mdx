Of the twelve things a [Sun](/learn/glossary/#sun) can be, Sagittarius is the one that behaves
most like an arrow: identity with a heading rather than an address.
Where you are matters less to this placement than where all of this is
going, and whether anyone plans to be honest about it along the way.

## How it shows up

The [Sun](/learn/planets/sun/) describes the project of a life, and in
Sagittarius the project is distance: meaning, motion, and the honest
version of every answer. This placement converts experience into
worldview at speed. The trip becomes a theory, the disaster becomes a
story with a moral, the stray fact becomes a thesis by Thursday. The
famous bluntness isn’t carelessness; it’s a working belief that people
deserve the real answer, delivered at the speed it was thought.

The equally famous restlessness is easier to respect once you see what
it’s for. The full [Sagittarius guide](/sagittarius/) covers the sign
end to end; the Sun’s own signature there is simpler, an identity
that stays alive by moving toward something larger than itself.

## What the restlessness is for

This Sun charges on horizon: literal travel, yes, but also study,
philosophy, foreign anything, the question behind the question. It
needs a bigger picture the way other placements need security, a
frame in which today’s absurdity means something. Teaching suits it
unusually well; so does any work that turns experience into
transmissible sense.

The drain is low ceilings: rules without reasons, jobs that map one
week onto every future week, conversations that circle the same drain.
Confinement doesn’t make this identity patient. It makes it sarcastic,
then gone, usually in that order.

## The growth edge

Commitment stops feeling like a cage once it comes with a window; that
sentence is the whole curriculum. The unexamined version treats every
promise as a future escape problem and calls the pattern freedom,
though on inspection it’s usually just motion. The mature version
learns the difference between a horizon and an exit. It picks a
person, a place, a discipline, and discovers that depth is also a
distance: you can travel a long way into one commitment, provided the
view keeps changing. Honesty gets the same upgrade. Still total, but
aimed, and timed for when it can actually land.

Where the horizon-chasing happens (career, belief, partnership,
actual geography) depends on the house your Sun holds. Run your
[birth chart](/birth-chart/) to see it, and what the arrow is
currently pointed at.
