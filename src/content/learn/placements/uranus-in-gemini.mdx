This is the placement being written into new charts right now: [Uranus](/learn/glossary/#uranus)
crossed into Gemini in July 2025, settled in April 2026, and keeps the
sign, final pass included, until 2033. The generation that will carry
it is presently in strollers.

## How it shows up

[Uranus](/learn/planets/uranus/) spends roughly seven years in a sign,
which makes the sign generational by definition: a description of how
an entire age group breaks rules, with the house and aspects left to
say what any individual does about it. In [Gemini](/gemini/), the
rule-breaking runs through the connective tissue: words, questions,
siblings, neighborhoods, the daily traffic of information between
minds.

Gemini’s conventional layer is sturdier than it looks: what counts as
literacy, what counts as a classroom, which languages are worth
learning, who is allowed to ask. Uranus in this territory produces a
cohort that treats every one of those as an open question.

## What this generation rewires

Forecasting a cohort still in diapers is honest guesswork, so the
tradition works from the sign. The expectation: children of this era
will be fluent in channels that don’t exist yet, will learn sideways
from each other faster than downward from institutions, and will treat
the sentence itself (who finishes one, in which language, through what
device) as negotiable.

Gemini’s native risks arrive electrified too: attention that scatters,
ideas abandoned mid-thought, novelty mistaken for progress. And since
the previous Gemini era closed in June 1949, the new one starts with
its nearest precedent more than seventy-five years back, a thoroughly
Uranian arrangement.

## In your own chart

For most people reading this, Uranus in Gemini is a transit rather
than a birthright: your own Uranus sits in an earlier sign, doing its
generational work there, while the current era crosses one house of
your chart for about seven years. For the children born through
2032-33, the sign is settled and the house will be the personal part,
which is to say the birth time matters. Run your
[birth chart](/birth-chart/) to see both: where your Uranus lives, and
which room the present weather is moving through.
