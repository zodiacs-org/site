Few generations have taken as much credit or blame as the one born
between 1937 and 1956, and its [Pluto](/learn/glossary/#pluto) sign rarely comes up in the
argument. It should. Leo is the sign of the individual, and this is
the cohort that turned individuality into infrastructure.

## How it shows up

[Pluto](/learn/planets/pluto/) holds each sign for 12 to 31 years
(the orbit is genuinely eccentric), so the sign belongs to a generation
and describes its shared relationship to power; the house and aspects
in one chart do the individual work. The shared territory here is
[Leo’s](/leo/): identity, visibility, creative will, the right to be
someone in particular rather than someone in general.

Pluto first touched Leo in October 1937, slid back into Cancer twice,
settled in June 1939, and stayed through October 1956, with final
passes ending in June 1958, two decades of births, bookend to
bookend, including the first ten years of the postwar baby boom.

## Identity as power

Before this cohort, importance mostly arrived through station: the
family, the rank, the role. The Pluto in Leo generation moved the
throne. Celebrity, youth culture, and the self as a lifelong project
all scaled up on its watch, and the question of who you are stopped
meaning whose you are.

Power renegotiated is never free. The cohort’s known failure mode is
the spotlight that won’t release, leadership aging into occupancy,
self-expression narrowing into self-reference. Its mature version is
the reason the failure gets forgiven: teachers, founders, and artists
who treated a self as something you build carefully and then spend on
purpose.

## In your own chart

Two decades of births share this sign, which is exactly why it can’t
tell one of them from another. The house does the sorting. A
fifth-house Pluto in Leo runs the theme through creative work and
children; a twelfth-house one plays the same drama offstage, in
private material a public generation rarely showed. Aspects to the Sun
or Moon make it personal enough that its owners rarely need telling.
Run your [birth chart](/birth-chart/), your own or a parent’s, and
find the house before you read the headlines back into the person.
