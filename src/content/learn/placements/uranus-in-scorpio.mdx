The era ran from 1975 to 1981: a first crossing in November 1974, a
settled run from September 1975 to February 1981, and a final
[retrograde](/learn/glossary/#retrograde) pass that ended in November 1981. What remains is a cohort
now in midlife, with a signature the tradition finds easy to spot.

## How it shows up

[Uranus](/learn/planets/uranus/) spends about seven years in each sign,
long enough that the sign describes a generation’s shared style of
rebellion rather than anyone’s personality; the house and aspects do
the individual work. [Scorpio](/scorpio/)’s province is what stays
hidden: intimacy, shared money, power, death, the subjects that make
dinner tables go quiet. Uranus there breeds a cohort whose instinct is
to say the quiet part at full volume.

The convention this generation disrupts isn’t a custom or an
institution so much as a silence. Where it finds a taboo, it files a
report.

## What gets unearthed

The generational project is disclosure: intimacy discussed in plain
terms, power named as power, the finances and failures other cohorts
kept off the record put onto it. There’s a surgical quality to the
style (Scorpio prefers the clean cut) and a standing distrust of any
comfort that was purchased with denial.

The cost is intensity as a reflex: the revelation delivered because
revelation works, the depth that can’t do shallow even when shallow
would heal faster. The mature version keeps the nerve and adds
discretion. It still goes down to the basement, but it stops charging
admission.

## In your own chart

If this is your placement, you were born in the years running up to
1981, and the sign belongs to your whole cohort; the house is where
your particular basement sits. An eighth-house Uranus doubles the
theme; a tenth-house one conducts the disclosures in public. Run your
[birth chart](/birth-chart/) to place it, and to see what the rest of
the chart makes of all that daylight.
