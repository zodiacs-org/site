The generation born between 1912 and 1939 drew [Pluto](/learn/glossary/#pluto) in the sign of
home, and then spent much of its life watching home become the thing
history fought over.

## How it shows up

[Pluto](/learn/planets/pluto/) spends 12 to 31 years in each sign,
depending on where its stretched orbit is, so the sign marks a
generation’s shared relationship to power rather than a personal
trait; the house and aspects handle the personal part. Here the shared
ground is [Cancer’s](/cancer/): home, family, lineage, belonging,
everything people mean when they say where they’re from.

Pluto first entered in September 1912, settled in May 1914 after two
slides back into Gemini, ran to October 1937, and made final passes
through June 1939. Twenty-seven years separate first entry from last
exit, the wide end of Pluto’s range.

## The meaning of home

The stakes were security itself. Born into a world of empires and
farm kitchens, this generation was asked (twice, in many countries)
to defend, leave, or rebuild every structure that defined belonging:
the family, the village, the nation, the house. Displacement on that scale
would have marked any generation; this one answered by treating walls
as a moral project. The housing programs, pensions, and family ideals
of the mid-century carry its signature.

The failure mode was fortification, home defended until it became a
bunker, belonging drawn so tight it excluded. The mature version,
visible in how many of them actually lived, kept the door open and the
soup warm regardless of who knocked.

## In your own chart

If this is your placement, you were born between 1912 and 1939 and
have likely known all of this longer than astrology has had words for
it. For everyone else it belongs to parents, grandparents, and the
family charts that explain the family. The house says where the theme
concentrated: a fourth-house Pluto doubled down on the household
itself, while a seventh-house one worked the same need for secure
belonging through marriage. Run a [birth chart](/birth-chart/) for
yourself or for the relative whose story you’re tracing. A birth time
narrows it to the house, and the aspects say how personally it ran.
