For once the sky moves at your speed. Mars is in Gemini from the first of
the month to the last, in your solar first house, and on July 4 it meets
Uranus, also in your sign. That is initiative with a live wire in it: the
announcement, the haircut, the thing you finally quit. The next day, July
5, Mars trines Pluto and sextiles Neptune, which can give a bold move both
traction and a reason. If you have been waiting for a starting signal,
this weekend may feel like one.

The money picture asks for more patience. Mercury, your ruler, is
retrograde in Cancer, your second house of income, until it stations
direct on July 23. The new moon there on July 14 is a good moment to
choose a financial goal, such as the rate you will ask for or a budget you
can keep. You might set it on the 14th and sign anything after the 23rd.
The Sun's square to Saturn on July 6 sketches the tension early, with a
cost on one side and a commitment to friends or a long-range plan on the
other. If only one gets funded this month, that is fine.

July 19 is quietly useful. Mars in your sign sextiles Saturn in your
eleventh house, and the people who share your long game may offer real
help. It is worth accepting.

Then the month hands you the microphone. The Sun enters Leo and your third
house of messages, pitches, and the near neighborhood on July 22, and meets
Jupiter there on July 29, a strong day for talking. It suits sending the
pitch, publishing the piece, or making the ask you have been rehearsing.
That same day's full moon in Aquarius falls in your ninth house of travel
and study. A faraway matter, such as a trip, an application, or an
audience in another time zone, may come to a head just as you find the
words.
