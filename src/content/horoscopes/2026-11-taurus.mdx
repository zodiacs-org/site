Your ruling planet spends the first half of November still moving backwards.
Venus stations direct on November 14 at 22.9° Libra, in your solar sixth
house of work, health and the ordinary shape of a week, so the first
fortnight is an approach, not a start. Venus sextiles Jupiter on November 4
at 24.7°, tying that work house to Jupiter in Leo and your fourth house of
home: an easing between the job and the household.

The new moon on November 9 at 16.9° Scorpio lands in your seventh house, the
one part of the chart that is not about you. Four days later Mercury
stations direct at 5.0° Scorpio on November 13, in the same house. Two
retrogrades ending within a day of each other, one in the house of the other
person and one in the house of your working week: that pairing is the month.
This is the fortnight the stalled conversation gets its words back.

November 16 is loud at home. Mars meets Jupiter at 25.9° Leo in your fourth
house, and on November 18 the Sun squares Jupiter from 26.1° Scorpio, so the
household and the partnership pull against each other in one week. The Sun
enters Sagittarius on November 22 and your eighth house of shared money and
debts.

Then both ends of the money question light up. The full moon on November 24
at 2.3° Gemini falls in your second house, your own earnings and what you
actually value, and on November 25 the Sun opposes Uranus across that same
axis at 3.7°. Mars enters Virgo the same day, which gives your hands
somewhere easier to go. On November 29 Uranus trines Pluto at 3.5°, a slow
agreement between what you earn and where you stand, and the Sun trines
Saturn on November 30. The month ends steadier than it behaved.
