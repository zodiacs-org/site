The first three weeks are the wind-up. The Sun sits in Cancer, your solar
twelfth house of rest and the backstage, until July 22, and Mercury is
retrograde there until July 23. The new moon on July 14 lands in that same
quiet territory, and it suits closing the old year on purpose. You might
cancel what you kept out of politeness, sleep a great deal, and let the
drafts stay drafts. Your season is coming, and arriving rested helps.

Your friends may not be resting. Mars and Uranus meet in Gemini, your
eleventh house, on July 4. Think of the invitation that appears an hour
before the event, or the plan that reroutes twice and improves both times.
The holiday weekend may be lively, since that corner of your chart is the
loudest one until your own season begins.

Jupiter has been in Leo since June 30, and on July 20 it opposes Pluto in
your seventh house of partnership. As your momentum becomes visible,
someone close may respond by holding on tighter. The Sun repeats the
opposition on July 27. Neither day needs a showdown. It can be enough to
say where you are going and to mean the invitation to come along.

Then the sky clears its throat. The Sun enters your sign on July 22 and
meets Jupiter at 6° Leo on July 29, a bright day in your season. It suits
a launch, a party, or the ask that felt too big in March. Because the same
day's full moon at 6° Aquarius falls in your partnership house, a
relationship may come into clearer focus just as you are most visible.
The 29th is a natural point to build toward in July.
