Jupiter has been crossing your own sign, and on December 13 it stations
retrograde at 27.0° Leo, in your solar first house. The expansive year you
have been having pauses to look back at itself. Nothing is taken away by a
station; what changes is that growth stops arriving from outside and starts
needing a decision from you.

Three aspects bracket that turn, which is unusual and worth watching.
Mercury squares Jupiter on December 4, and an early claim may be larger
than the facts support. The Sun trines Jupiter on December 18 from
Sagittarius and your fifth house of play and self-made things, the best day
of the month for asking plainly for what you want. Mercury trines Jupiter
again on December 23, when the same request can be put in writing.

Venus enters Scorpio on December 4 and your fourth house of home, where it
stays for the rest of the month: the house itself, who is in it, and what
it costs. Venus squares Pluto on December 9, so one domestic decision meets
a harder financial fact. Mars squares Uranus on December 4 from Virgo and
your second house of earned money against your eleventh house of friends
and groups, which is a short, sharp collision between your budget and
someone else's plan.

The new moon at 16.9° Sagittarius on December 9 opens your fifth house
properly: a creative start, a trip, a romance given room. Saturn stations
direct at 7.9° Aries on December 10 and Neptune at 1.6° Aries on
December 12, both in your ninth house, so study and travel plans that have
been on hold become possible to book.

The Sun enters Capricorn on December 21 and your sixth house of work and
health, and the full moon at 2.2° Cancer on December 24 completes something
quiet in your twelfth house. The year ends with rest, not display, and that
is the right order this time.
