reaches its visible end just as everyone else is distracted. Full moons in
your own sign tend to show you how you have actually been, rather than how
you planned to be. The Sun opposes it from Capricorn and your seventh
house, so another person is part of the picture.

at 7.9° Aries on December 10 and Neptune stations direct at 1.6° Aries on
December 12, both in your tenth house of work and public standing. A job
situation that has been stalled for months, or vague for months, starts to
move in one direction. Mercury trines Saturn on December 11, and a message
sent that day about work is likely to land well.

and squares Uranus on December 4, when the week's logistics may come apart
and need rebuilding rather than rescuing. Venus enters Scorpio on
December 4 and your fifth house of pleasure and self-made things, which is
the warmest placement of your month. Venus squares Pluto on December 9, so
one enjoyable plan runs into a friend's expectation.

sixth house of work and health: the routine, the treatment, the system you
have been meaning to set up. Jupiter stations retrograde at 27.0° Leo on
December 13 in your second house, turning a money question back for review
rather than closing it.

Sun squares Neptune on December 23 and Saturn on December 29, so December

than postponed.
