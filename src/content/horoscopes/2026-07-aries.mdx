July runs on your ruler. Mars spends the month in Gemini, your solar third
house of errands, messages, and neighborhood logistics, and it opens
loudly. Mars meets Uranus on July 4, which is the sky's version of a group
chat catching fire. There may be a surprise text, a sudden plan, or a
detour that turns out to matter. The quick ones can be fun to say yes to,
though it helps to reread before you hit send.

The month's real weight sits at home. The Sun squares Saturn in your sign
on July 6, and the friction is familiar: obligations to family or household
pressing against what you are trying to become. The new moon in Cancer on
July 14 offers a fresh start under your own roof, whether that is a lease,
a conversation, or a room finally cleared. Mercury has been retrograde in
the same territory all month. It stations direct on July 23, so domestic
negotiations and paperwork may settle more easily after that date.

Two stations land in Aries itself. Neptune turns retrograde on July 7, and
Saturn follows on July 26. Both are slow background weather rather than
daily news, but the Saturn station is worth noticing. The discipline
project you started this spring may come up for review through the end of
the year. You might keep what held and let go of what was posture.

Then the sky turns lighter. The Sun enters Leo and your fifth house of
pleasure, romance, and self-made things on July 22, and meets Jupiter there
on July 29. That is a bright day this month for a launch, a date, or an
opening night. The same day's full moon in Aquarius lights your house of
friends and future plans, so something you make for joy may find its
audience. Mars sextile Saturn on July 19 already showed that speed and
structure can, for once, sit on the same side.
