The month is blunt about money, value, and what you are willing to ask for.
Leo is your solar second house, and on August 7 the Sun trines Saturn from
there to your career house. A rate, role, invoice, or budget can get real
when you treat it as a grown-up matter instead of a hope.

The middle of the month does more. Mercury enters Leo on August 9, then
opposes Pluto, trines Neptune, and sextiles Uranus on Wednesday, August 12.
That same day brings the new moon, a total solar eclipse, at 20° Leo. It is
a natural point to start a new money plan, with room for the hidden detail:
taxes, shared resources, a friend's advice, or the private fear that has
been steering the numbers. Mercury meets Jupiter on August 15, and Venus
sextiles Jupiter on August 17. If you need to name a number, that window is
a friendly one.

Mars enters Cancer on August 11, giving you more heat than usual. It is
useful if aimed. On August 17 Mars squares Neptune in Aries, your tenth
house, and a vague professional pressure could start to feel like a
personal emergency. Asking what is actually due, by whom, and when may
help. The answer may be smaller than the feeling.

Home softens once Venus enters Libra on August 6. Its trine to Pluto and
opposition to Neptune on August 10 can make a family or household matter
feel tender and overlarge at once. By August 21, when Venus opposes Saturn,
a repair may need a boundary as well as goodwill. The Sun enters Virgo on
August 23 and Mercury follows on August 25, shifting you into messages and
local logistics. The August 28 full moon, a deep partial lunar eclipse at
4.9° Pisces, lights your ninth house of travel, study, and belief while
Mercury and the Sun square Uranus. A route may change, and the larger
direction does not have to.
