Mars is in your sign for the whole of October, and it does not sit still.
It trines Neptune on October 2, opposes Pluto at 3.1° on October 3,
sextiles Uranus at 5.4° on October 7, and trines Saturn at 10.4° on October
16. Read that as a sequence: imagination, then a collision with someone
else's will in your solar seventh house of partnership, then a fast
opening, then, best of the four, the day your drive and your patience
finally agree. October 16 is the day to push something heavy uphill.

Jupiter is also in Leo, and on October 15 the Sun sextiles it at 22.0° from
Libra and your third house of conversation and short trips. Say the thing.
It travels further than you expect that week, and Jupiter in your own sign
means the reception is warmer than the message strictly earns. The new moon
at 17.4° Libra on October 10 resets that third house: a new rhythm of
correspondence, a local arrangement, a sibling conversation restarted. The
Sun opposed Saturn at 11.3° on October 4 before it, so the first week has
the sharper tone: someone slows you down and you dislike it.

The second half moves indoors. The Sun enters Scorpio on October 23 and
your fourth house of home, Mercury stations retrograde at 21.0° Scorpio on
October 24 in the same place, and household logistics will need doing
twice. Venus has been retrograde there since October 3, at 8.5° Scorpio, so
the home question has been open all month; it moves to your third house on
October 25, still retrograde. Then the full moon at 2.8° Taurus on October
26 tops your tenth house of career, and something public completes. Pluto
turned direct in your partnership house on October 16, and that is the
quieter story underneath.
