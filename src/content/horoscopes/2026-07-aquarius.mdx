For once, the month's closing act is addressed to you. The July 29 full
moon lands at 6° Aquarius, your solar first house, and much of what comes
before it reads as buildup. First-house culminations are personal. You may
notice something about how you live, look, or introduce yourself coming
into fuller view, with other people watching.

The buildup runs through partnership. Jupiter is in Leo, your seventh
house, and on July 20 it opposes Pluto in your own sign. That can feel like
a partner's growth pressing on who you are becoming: an offer to merge
homes, calendars, or plans that is generous and a little engulfing at once.
The Sun repeats the opposition on July 27, then meets Jupiter at 6° Leo on
July 29, a warm day that can belong to the two of you. It is a good day to
make the offer or consider accepting it. Under your own full moon that same
evening, it may be worth checking that the arrangement still fits the
person you actually are.

Romance and side projects get the early sparks. Mars in Gemini, your fifth
house, meets Uranus, your planet, on July 4. Think of the spontaneous date,
the idea that arrives sideways, or the collaboration that starts as a
joke. Mars trines Pluto the next day, July 5, which lends staying power.
Whatever sparks that weekend can become serious if you want it to.

The unglamorous middle matters too. The Sun squares Saturn on July 6, with
your workload leaning on the errands and paperwork of your third house. The
new moon on July 14, at 22° Cancer in your sixth house, suits a reset of
routine: the sleep schedule, the checkup, the calendar rebuilt around how
you actually work. Mercury is retrograde in that same house until July 23,
so appointment times may shift until then. Saturn stations retrograde on
July 26 in your third, and a writing project or sibling arrangement may
come up for review. That can read as a pause rather than a cancellation.
