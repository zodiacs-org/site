Your ruler stops and turns around on November 14. Venus stations direct at
22.9° Libra, in your own sign and your solar first house: the body, the
face, the version of you other people meet before you have said anything.
Weeks of that running in reverse end here. Ten days earlier, on November 4,
retrograde Venus sextiles Jupiter at 24.7°, Jupiter in Leo and your eleventh
house of friends and the long future, which is the easiest room on the chart
that week.

The money half runs alongside it. The new moon at 16.9° Scorpio on November
9 lands in your second house, where your earnings and what you count as
worth having are kept, and a day before Venus turns, on November 13, Mercury
stations direct at 5.0° Scorpio in that same house. Two retrogrades ending
within a day of each other is November's real story: the number and the
self-image come out of review together.

Then it gets loud. Mars meets Jupiter at 25.9° Leo on November 16, both in
the eleventh, about as much collective heat as a year offers. On November 18
the Sun squares Jupiter from 26.1° Scorpio and the second house and the
eleventh pull against each other. This is the week what the group wants and
what you can afford stop agreeing. The figure is worth saying out loud.

The Sun enters Sagittarius on November 22 and the month moves into your
third house: talk, short trips, the people within a mile of you. The full
moon at 2.3° Gemini on November 24 lights the ninth house opposite, and the
Sun opposes Uranus there on November 25, so the near and the far argue. Mars
enters Virgo and your twelfth the same day, where effort goes quiet. Venus
sextiles Jupiter again on November 28, at 26.7° and moving forward now, the
aspect the month opened with run the right way round. A good month to finish
a sentence you started in October.
