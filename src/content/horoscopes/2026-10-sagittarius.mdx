Your people are the month's best resource. The new moon at 17.4° Libra on
October 10 falls in your solar eleventh house of friends, groups and shared
projects. It is the cleanest reset of your year for who you spend time with and
what you are building together. Ask for the introduction in the fortnight
after it. On October 15 the Sun sextiles Jupiter at 22.0°, and your ruler
is in Leo and your ninth house of travel, study and conviction: the
connection that opens a door somewhere further away than your current map.

Mars is in that ninth house all month and it is the engine. It trines
Neptune on October 2, opposes Pluto on October 3 (a plan meets a flat
refusal from your third house of everyday communication), sextiles Uranus
at 5.4° on October 7, and trines Saturn at 10.4° on October 16. That last
day is when the far plan becomes a booked, paid, scheduled thing rather
than an intention. Pluto stations direct at 3.1° Aquarius the same day in
your third house, and a conversation frozen since spring restarts.

The end of the month asks for quiet, which you will resist. The Sun enters
Scorpio on October 23 and your twelfth house; Mercury stations retrograde
at 21.0° Scorpio on October 24 in the same place. Old thoughts come back
around, and most of them want reviewing rather than acting on. Venus has
been retrograde there since October 3 at 8.5° Scorpio, so something
unfinished and private has already been asking for attention. It moves to
your eleventh house on October 25, still retrograde. The full moon at 2.8°
Taurus on October 26 completes something in your sixth house of work and
health: a routine reaches the point where it either changes or stays for
good.
