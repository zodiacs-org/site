Neptune, your ruler, stations direct at 1.6° Aries on December 12, in your
solar second house of earned money and what you value. It has been
retrograde for months, and the effect of the turn is less mystical than it
sounds: the question of what you are worth, and what you charge, stops
going round in circles. Saturn stations direct at 7.9° Aries on December 10
in the same house, two days earlier, which adds structure to the same
subject.

and that is the day an idea about money arrives in a usable shape. The Sun
squares it on December 23, when someone else's version of the figure is
foggier than yours. Mercury squares it again on December 26, so check the
document rather than trusting the summary. Mercury trines Saturn on
December 11, between the two stations, and is the best day of the month to
name a price.
