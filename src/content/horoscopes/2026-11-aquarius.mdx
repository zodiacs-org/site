Start at the top of your chart, where the month does most of its work. The
new moon at 16.9° Scorpio on November 9 falls in your solar tenth house:
work, standing, whatever your name is publicly attached to. Scorpio makes
that a question of what you want to be known for, not what you drifted into.
Mercury stations direct in the same house at 5.0° Scorpio on November 13.
The circling stops, which is a different thing from an answer.

Venus stations direct the next day, November 14, at 22.9° Libra, in your
ninth house of study and the long view. Two stations on consecutive days is
the month's real architecture: one planet in your career house, one in the
house of why the work matters. Venus sextiles Jupiter, which sits in your
partnership house, at 24.7° on November 4 and again at 26.7° on November 28,
the second time with Venus direct. On November 16 Mars meets Jupiter at
25.9° Leo, directly opposite your sign. The Sun squares Jupiter at 26.1° on
November 18: your standing on one side, one person on the other, and
splitting the difference is not the same as answering it.

The Sun enters Sagittarius on November 22 and your eleventh house of
friends, groups and the long future takes over. The full moon at 2.3° Gemini
on November 24 brings the opposite house to a head: what you make for
pleasure, not for the record. November 25 stacks three: the Sun sextile
Pluto at 3.5° Aquarius in your own sign, the Sun opposite Uranus at 3.7°
Gemini, and Mars into Virgo and your eighth house of shared money.

November 29 is the quiet one, and the slowest moving: Uranus trines Pluto at
3.5°, the fifth house in long conversation with the rebuild running through
your sign. On November 30 the Sun trines Saturn at 8.0° Aries, in your third
house of plain speech. After a month this loud at the top of the chart, one
ordinary sentence is the right size.
