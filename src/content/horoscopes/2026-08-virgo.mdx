rest, privacy, and unfinished business, and the Sun trines Saturn on August
7. That favors useful solitude: the appointment kept, the old obligation
closed, the quiet discipline that supports the year ahead.

Mercury, your planet, enters Leo on August 9 and makes August 12 unusually
busy behind the curtain: opposition Pluto, trine Neptune, sextile Uranus.
The same day brings the new moon, a total solar eclipse, at 20° Leo.
Something private may be ready for a reset, such as a sleep pattern, a
secret worry, a draft, or a goodbye. There is no need to rush it into
public. Mercury meets Jupiter on August 15, still in that hidden house, and
any good news may feel more like relief than applause.

your second house. Venus trines Pluto and opposes Neptune on August 10. An

you get paid for work done quietly, while Venus opposite Saturn on August
21 asks for a cleaner boundary around debt, obligation, or someone else's
expectations.

plans, then squares Neptune on August 17. A group promise may be badly
defined, and names next to tasks help. Your season begins when the Sun
enters Virgo on August 23, and Mercury follows on August 25. The August 28
full moon, a deep partial lunar eclipse at 4.9° Pisces, lands in your
partnership house while Mercury and the Sun square Uranus in your career
house. A relationship may reach clarity just as work changes shape. You can
answer both, one at a time.
