The Virgo weeks put the other person in the light. The Sun crosses your
solar seventh house of partnership through September 22, and the new
moon at 18.4° Virgo on September 11 is a fresh start for that house: the
talk that resets terms, the first date that feels like a plan, the
working alliance made official.

Money is the month's serious thread. Saturn sits retrograde in your
second house, and on September 1 Mars in Cancer, your fifth house of
romance, children and self-made things, squares it. The first week may
weigh the fun against the budget. Then your ruler complicates the
arithmetic. Neptune, also in your second house, takes the Sun's
opposition on September 26, the month's fog bank, and it sits directly
on the numbers. Between those two dates Mercury enters Libra and your
eighth house on September 10 and works on the shared side. It opposes
Neptune on September 12 (a joint figure that needs a second source),
opposes Saturn on September 18 (the repayment schedule, stated), and
sextiles Jupiter in your sixth on September 21, a good day for work,
when a steady thing may quietly grow.

September 10 also sends Venus into Scorpio and your ninth house, and
affection may lean toward distance: a trip, or a conviction you share with
someone. Its square to Pluto on September 15 then asks what you are still
carrying in private. Uranus stations retrograde at 5.7° Gemini in your
fourth house on September 10 as well, and the home changes of the past year
may come up for review. Living in them a while before redrawing them is an
option.

The Sun enters Libra on September 23 and the season of shared accounts
begins. The full moon at 3.6° Aries on September 26 culminates in your
money house on the same day as that Neptune opposition. An income matter
may reach a visible peak just as the story around it goes soft. Reading
the statement rather than the mood helps, and the Sun's trine to Pluto
suggests the sound part holds. Mars enters Leo and your sixth house on
September 28, and the last days may trade drama for traction. Mercury
enters Scorpio on September 30, and the horizon calls early in October.
