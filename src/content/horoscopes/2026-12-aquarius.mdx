Pluto is in your sign for years, in your solar first house, and this month
it gets touched twice in two days. Mercury sextiles Pluto on December 8,
and Venus squares Pluto on December 9. The sextile is an opening: a
conversation where you say the true thing and it is received. The square is
friction between how you are changing and what your career expects of you,
since Venus is in Scorpio and your tenth house from December 4 onward.
December is a month when the slow rewrite of who you are meets the job
description.

Your third house fills up and turns forward. Saturn stations direct at 7.9°
Aries on December 10 and Neptune stations direct at 1.6° Aries on
December 12, both there, so writing, study, local arrangements and dealings
with siblings or neighbours stop being stuck. Mercury trines Saturn on
December 11 and is the practical day of the month: the message that moves a
thing along after weeks of nothing.

Mars squares Uranus on December 4 from Virgo and your eighth house against
your fifth, which can read as a sharp disagreement about money attached to
something you enjoy. Mercury squares Jupiter the same day, and a claim may
be bigger than the evidence.

eleventh house of friends, groups and hopes, which is home territory for
you: a group joined, a project proposed, a circle widened. Jupiter stations
retrograde at 27.0° Leo on December 13 in your seventh house, so a
partnership that has been growing pauses to be examined. The Sun trines
Jupiter on December 18, the easiest day between you and someone close.

The Sun enters Capricorn on December 21 and your twelfth house, and the
year ends quietly. The full moon at 2.2° Cancer on December 24 completes
something in your sixth house of work and health.
