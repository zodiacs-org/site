This is your month, but not the loud kind. The Sun is in Leo until August
23, your solar first house, and it trines Saturn on August 7. That supports
serious self-definition: the promise you can keep, the plan you can repeat,
the version of confidence that does not need applause to stand up.

The main reset arrives on Wednesday, August 12. The new moon, a total solar
eclipse, lands at 20° Leo. Mercury, newly in your sign since August 9,
spends the same day opposite Pluto, trine Neptune, and sextile Uranus. You
may hear yourself say something that changes the room. Pluto is in your
partnership house, though, and the other person is not a prop in your
reveal. Mercury meets Jupiter in Leo on August 15, a good day to consider
the announcement, the ask, the introduction, or a first step into a larger
role.

Venus enters Libra on August 6, sweetening your third house of messages and
local plans. August 10 is more complicated: Venus trines Pluto and opposes
Neptune, so a conversation can feel intimate and slightly unreal at once.
It can stay beautiful while you check the facts. Venus sextile Jupiter on
August 17 is easier, and Venus opposition Saturn on August 21 asks whether
the words match the commitment.

Mars enters Cancer on August 11, your twelfth house, and squares Neptune on
August 17. Restlessness may have no obvious target, and there is no need to
invent one. The Sun enters Virgo on August 23, Mercury follows on August 25,
and attention turns to money and self-worth. The August 28 full moon, a
deep partial lunar eclipse at 4.9° Pisces, lights shared resources while
Mercury and the Sun square Uranus in your friendship house. Someone's plan
could change the math. Recalculating before you react is the calmer path.
