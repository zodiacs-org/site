Your season begins on September 23, when the Sun enters Libra at the
equinox. Before that, the Virgo weeks run through your solar twelfth
house of rest and loose ends. The new moon at 18.4° Virgo on September 11
can be read as permission to close tabs, cancel gracefully and sleep.

Your voice arrives ahead of your season. Mercury enters Libra on
September 10 and spends the month making your case. September 12 is a
double day for it. Mercury trines Pluto in your fifth house, when a
creative or romantic conversation may go satisfyingly deep, and opposes
Neptune in your partnership house, when another may dissolve into mist.
Signing nothing during the mist is a fair rule. Mercury trine Uranus on
September 14 may open an unexpected door at a distance, through travel,
study or publishing. Mercury opposite Saturn on September 18 can bring a
partner's limit into words. Saturn has been retrograde in your seventh
house all season, and the 18th simply gives it a voice. Mercury sextile
Jupiter in your house of friends on September 21 may bring a warmer
answer from your wider circle.

Money moves early too. Venus, your ruler, enters Scorpio and your second
house on September 10 and squares Pluto in your fifth on September 15.
Income and indulgence may negotiate, and deciding the number before the
checkout screen does is sensible. The Mars-Saturn square of September 1,
from your career angle into your seventh, sets the month's opening
question: how much of the push at work belongs to you, and how much to
the partnership behind it.

The sky spells it out later in the month. The full moon at 3.6° Aries on
September 26 culminates in your partnership house, and a relationship may
show its true shape. The Sun opposes Neptune at the same time, which asks
you to see the person rather than the ideal. The Sun's trines to Pluto on
September 26 and Uranus on September 28 suggest that what remains is
sturdier than what fades. Mars enters Leo and your eleventh house on
September 28, and your season can open with allies nearby. Mercury enters
Scorpio on September 30, and money may get the last word.
