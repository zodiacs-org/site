Two retrogrades end a day apart this month, Mercury on November 13 and Venus
on November 14, and for you they end in adjacent rooms. The month opens
quietly: on November 4 Venus at 24.7° Libra sextiles Jupiter at 24.7° Leo,
an easy line between your solar eighth house of shared money and the sixth
house of work and the body. The new moon on November 9 at 16.9° Scorpio
falls in the ninth, the house of the long view: study, distance, belief, the
subject you keep meaning to take seriously. It asks about scope, not
logistics.

The stations are the real event. Mercury turns direct at 5.0° Scorpio and
the ninth-house question stops circling. Venus turns direct at 22.9° Libra,
so the shared-money conversation resumes a day behind it. Both have been in
review together; they come out of it together. On November 16 Mars meets
Jupiter at 25.9° Leo, both of them in that sixth house of work, exercise and
daily order. The Sun at 26.1° Scorpio squares Jupiter at 26.1° Leo on
November 18: the wide frame and the day's workload pulling against each
other.

The Sun enters Sagittarius on November 22 and the top of your chart opens
for a month. On November 24 it trines Neptune at 1.7° Aries, your ruler
sitting in your house of earnings, and the full moon at 2.3° Gemini lights
your fourth house of home the same day. Public life and private life both
ask at once. November 25 crowds in behind that: the Sun sextile Pluto at
3.5° Aquarius, the Sun opposite Uranus at 3.7° Gemini, and Mars into Virgo
and your seventh house.

Three easy aspects close the month. Venus at 26.7° Libra sextiles Jupiter at
26.7° Leo again on November 28, Uranus trines Pluto at 3.5° Aquarius on
November 29, and on November 30 the Sun trines Saturn at 8.0° Aries. Saturn
in your second house, the Sun at the top of the chart: a plain look at what
the work is actually worth.
