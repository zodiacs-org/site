Mars is in your sign for the month, in your solar first house, and it
starts by hitting something. Mars squares Uranus at 3.3° on December 4,
with Uranus in your tenth house of career and public standing. You will
have energy and a short fuse on the same day, and the friction is likely to
come from a decision made above you. Acting quickly is tempting and
probably premature; let the moment pass before locking in a reply.

Mercury, your ruler, is busy and mostly helpful. It squares Jupiter on
December 4, enters Sagittarius and your fourth house of home on December 6,
trines Neptune on December 7, then opposes Uranus and sextiles Pluto on
December 8. That cluster reads as a week of talking about where you live
and what it costs, with one piece of real information arriving in the
middle of it.

Venus enters Scorpio on December 4 and your third house of errands,
messages and local life, and stays all month, which makes ordinary
correspondence noticeably more pleasant. Venus squares Pluto on December 9,
when one message is heavier than its length suggests.

The new moon at 16.9° Sagittarius on December 9 is a home beginning:
moving, hosting, repairing, or simply deciding to stay. Saturn stations
direct at 7.9° Aries on December 10 and Neptune at 1.6° Aries on
December 12, both in your eighth house of shared money and what is held
jointly, so a long, slow financial entanglement starts to clear. Mercury
trines Saturn on December 11 and is the day to ask for the number.

Jupiter stations retrograde at 27.0° Leo on December 13 in your twelfth
house, and rest becomes the project. The Sun enters Capricorn on
December 21 and your fifth house, the full moon at 2.2° Cancer on
December 24 gathers friends in your eleventh, and Mercury squares Saturn on
December 30 with one last practical question.
