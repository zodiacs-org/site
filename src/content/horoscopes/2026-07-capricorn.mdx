On July 6 the Sun squares Saturn, your planet, and the month states its
theme early. The Sun sits in Cancer, your solar seventh house of
partnership. Saturn sits in Aries, your fourth house of home and family.
Someone close may want more from you than the household has to spare, or
the household may want more than the relationship does. Neither side is
wrong, and the week may need setting aside for it.

Some repair is available mid-month. The new moon on July 14, at 22°
Cancer, is a clean start in that same partnership house. It suits an
honest conversation, a second chance, or a contract renewed on better
terms. Mercury has been retrograde there all month and stations direct on
July 23, so anything negotiated before that date may deserve one more read
after it. Old agreements that resurface in early July are material for the
new moon, not verdicts.

Work may run hot and then settle. Mars conjunct Uranus on July 4, in your
sixth house of routines, can look like a schedule flipped without notice:
an outage, a coverage gap, a reorganized week. Mars sextile Saturn on July
19 favors a fix that holds, and it is a sensible day to set a new routine.

Money is the late-month story, on both sides of the ledger. Jupiter
opposes Pluto on July 20 across your eighth and second houses, the line
between what you share and what is yours. The Sun repeats the question on
July 27. On July 29 the Sun meets Jupiter at 6° Leo in your eighth house,
a supportive day for a loan, a settlement, or a partner's good news. The
full moon at 6° Aquarius lands in your second house the same day, which
makes that morning a natural time to send an invoice.

Saturn itself stations retrograde on July 26 in your fourth house. The home
project or family arrangement you committed to this spring may come up for
review through the end of the year. You might keep the load-bearing parts
and renegotiate the rest.
