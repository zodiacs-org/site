This is your season and it is not a simple one. The Sun is in your sign
until October 23, and the new moon at 17.4° Libra on October 10 is your
solar first house reset, the annual beginning that sets the tone for how
you present yourself and what you start. Make it concrete. Before it, on
October 4, the Sun opposes Saturn at 11.3° from your seventh house: a
partner or close counterpart names a constraint, and October's work is
deciding how much of it you accept.

Your ruler turns retrograde early. Venus stations at 8.5° Scorpio on
October 3, in your second house of money and self-worth, and re-enters your
own sign on October 25 still moving backwards. Read the whole month as a
re-pricing: what you charge, what you accept, what you thought you were
worth in August. The answer arrives slowly and should not be forced. On
October 15 the Sun sextiles Jupiter at 22.0°, linking your first house to
Jupiter in Leo and your eleventh: friends and networks are the easy route
this month, and one introduction is worth more than a fortnight of effort.

October 16 unsticks things. Mars trines Saturn at 10.4° and Pluto stations
direct at 3.1° Aquarius in your fifth house, where a creative project or a
romance frozen since spring starts to move again. Then the tone changes:
the Sun enters Scorpio on October 23 and your money house, Mercury stations
retrograde there at 21.0° on October 24, and financial admin gets slower
and more careful. The full moon at 2.8° Taurus on October 26 culminates in
your eighth house of shared resources, and the Sun squares Pluto the same
day. Something jointly held reaches its decision point. You will have the
information by then.
