The loudest aspect of November, Mars meeting Jupiter on November 16, lands
before the Sun leaves Scorpio on November 22. Until then the Sun sits in
Scorpio, your solar twelfth house, the quiet room before your own season,
and the new moon at 16.9° Scorpio on November 9 starts something there with
no audience attached. Venus sextiles Jupiter on November 4 at 24.7°, from
your eleventh house of friends across to your ruler in the ninth, so the
first week runs sociable and wide while the Sun is withdrawing. Company and
the pull of somewhere further off get loud together, and the question is
which one you are answering.

The real story is November 13 and 14. Mercury stations direct at 5.0°
Scorpio, then Venus stations direct at 22.9° Libra: two retrogrades ending a
day apart, one in the private house, one out among your people. Nothing
resolves at a station. They stop going backwards, which is a smaller thing
than it sounds and still enough to feel.

November 16 is the blaze. Mars meets Jupiter at 25.9° Leo in your ninth
house of distance, study and conviction, which is your own weather. The Sun
squares Jupiter at 26.1° on November 18, the honest footnote: appetite
running ahead of the battery, with the Sun still in the quiet house. The
plan is not wrong. The month is not finished warming up.

Then the Sun enters Sagittarius on November 22 and your year restarts. It
trines Neptune at 1.7° on November 24, the day the full moon fills 2.3°
Gemini in your seventh house, and opposes Uranus there on November 25, so
the charge in that full moon comes from the other chair rather than from
you. Mars leaves for Virgo and your career house the same day. By the Sun's
trine to Saturn at 8.0° on November 30 the question has narrowed: of
everything the month opened, what do you intend to keep?
