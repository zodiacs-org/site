July 14 is worth circling. The new moon that day, at 22° Cancer, lands in
your solar fifth house of romance, play, and the creative work that is
yours alone. It is a clean fresh start in your summer, suited to asking
someone out, starting the draft, or booking studio time. The footnote is
Mercury, retrograde in that same house until July 23. An old flame who
resurfaces or a stalled project that stirs in early July may deserve a
look, though committing to either can wait until after the 23rd.

Partnership warms up. Venus enters Virgo, your seventh house, on July 9,
and much of the month may feel easier for couples and close collaborators.
The one dated bump is July 13, when Venus squares Uranus and a household
surprise could test everyone's patience. It may be an aftershock of July
4, when Mars met Uranus in your fourth house and something at home changed
without asking first: the appliance, the roommate's announcement, the
sudden idea about moving. Estimates can be gathered early. Mars sextile

house of routines and health, and the Sun joins it on July 22, then meets

worth considering then. The caution is the axis it sits on. Jupiter opposed
Pluto on July 20, the Sun repeats it on July 27, and both point at your
twelfth house of rest. More work tends to hold up only when recovery is
scheduled alongside it. Fittingly, the July 29 full moon at 6° Aquarius
lands in that twelfth house the same day, so a quiet evening may suit.

About money: the Sun's square to Saturn on July 6 weighs the month's fun
against the budget. Saturn stations retrograde in your second house on July
26, with Neptune retrograde there since July 7, and the fall can be a time
to review the spending plan. Nothing dramatic needs deciding. You can keep
paying the same bills and let the numbers sit still for a while.
