October asks you to sort out money that is not entirely yours. Venus
stations retrograde at 8.5° Scorpio on October 3, in your solar eighth
house of shared accounts, debts and what you owe each other: six weeks of
rechecking terms you agreed to quickly. The following day the Sun opposes
Saturn at 11.3°: the Sun in Libra and your seventh house, Saturn retrograde
in your own sign. Someone states a limit and it lands on you personally. It
is a boundary, not a verdict.

The new moon at 17.4° Libra on October 10 is the cleanest partnership reset
of your year: one relationship, business or personal, restated from the
beginning. Take the fortnight after it seriously. On October 15 the Sun
sextiles Jupiter at 22.0°, connecting that partnership house to Jupiter in
Leo and your fifth house of pleasure and things you make: an easy yes,
offered rather than negotiated. October 16 is the month's most usable day.
Mars in Leo trines Saturn at 10.4°, so ambition and patience point the same
direction, and Pluto stations direct at 3.1° Aquarius in your eleventh
house, unsticking something in a group you belong to.

The last week turns slower. Mercury stations retrograde at 21.0° Scorpio on
October 24, back in that shared-money territory. Expect a contract to be
reread, and read it. Venus re-enters Libra on October 25, still retrograde,
which returns the partnership question to the table with a softer face.
Then October 26 does two things at once: the full moon at 2.8° Taurus
lights your second house, where your own money and what you actually value
come to a head, and the Sun squares Pluto. Decide what is yours before you
decide what to share.
