August begins at home. Leo is your solar fourth house, and on August 7 the
Sun trines Saturn from that private territory to your twelfth house of
rest. The theme is plain: a quieter house, a real boundary, and less
calling exhaustion a personality trait.

The domestic reset arrives on Wednesday, August 12. Mercury, newly in Leo
since August 9, opposes Pluto, trines Neptune, and sextiles Uranus that
day, and the new moon, a total solar eclipse, lands at 20° Leo. A family
conversation, lease matter, renovation, or room-by-room decision may move
fast. A flexible plan helps, since Uranus is in Gemini, your second house,
and any surprise could carry a cost. Mercury meets Jupiter in Leo on August
15, a warmer day for the generous version: the family table, good news, a
big yes about where you live.

Venus, your planet, enters Libra on August 6 and your sixth house of work,
health, and the small systems that keep a life running. Its trine to Pluto
and opposition to Neptune on August 10 asks whether the routine serves your
future or just looks graceful from the outside. The Venus-Jupiter sextile on
August 17 helps, and the Venus-Saturn opposition on August 21 suggests the
help needs structure.

Mars enters Cancer on August 11, your third house of messages and local
motion. When Mars squares Neptune on August 17, a confusing text may be
better answered once the anger passes. By August 23 the Sun enters Virgo,
your fifth house, and Mercury follows on August 25. Pleasure returns, and
the August 28 full moon, a deep partial lunar eclipse at 4.9° Pisces,
lights your eleventh house of friends just as Mercury and the Sun square
Uranus. If the group plan changes, the second version may be the better
one.
