October's money is other people's. The new moon at 17.4° Libra on October
10 falls in your solar eighth house of shared accounts, debts and what is
inherited: a genuine restart for a joint arrangement, and the best
fortnight of the year to renegotiate one. It follows the Sun's opposition
to Saturn at 11.3° on October 4, that eighth house against Saturn
retrograde in your second: someone else's terms against your own resources,
said out loud. Neither side is unreasonable. Write the numbers down.

Venus stations retrograde at 8.5° Scorpio on October 3 in your ninth house
of travel, study and belief, and Mercury stations retrograde there too, at
21.0° Scorpio on October 24. A plan to go somewhere, learn something or
commit to a view gets reviewed twice over. That is not an obstruction; it
is a chance to find the flaw while it is still cheap. The Sun enters
Scorpio and that house on October 23, so the last week has the appetite
even while the rest counsels patience.

Mars spends the month in Leo and your sixth house of work and health:
opposite Pluto on October 3, sextile Uranus at 5.4° on October 7, trine
Saturn at 10.4° on October 16. That trine is the month's most productive
day for anything needing sustained effort. Pluto stations direct at 3.1°
Aquarius the same day in your twelfth house, and something carried
privately since spring starts to lift. On October 15 the Sun sextiles
Jupiter at 22.0° in your sixth: a practical offer of help at work. The full
moon at 2.8° Taurus on October 26 completes something in your third house:
a conversation, a piece of writing, a local matter finishes.
