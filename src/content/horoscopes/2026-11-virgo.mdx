Two planets turn around a day apart this month, and the first is yours.
Mercury stations direct at 5.0° Scorpio on November 13, in your solar third
house of talk, messages and the ordinary traffic of the mind. The new moon
at 16.9° Scorpio on November 9 sits in the same house four days earlier,
which puts a beginning and a reversal in one small room. Whatever you start
writing or asking that first week is worth a second pass once your ruling
planet turns.

Venus stations direct at 22.9° Libra on November 14, in the house of your
earnings and what you actually value. Two retrogrades ending that close
together is the month's real shape: the sentence you have been trying to
word and the number you have been avoiding come up in the same two days.
Venus makes an easy angle to Jupiter on November 4 at 24.7° and repeats it
on November 28 at 26.7°, Jupiter sitting in Leo and the private twelfth
house behind your chart. Neither day asks loudly.

The middle of the month is loud where you cannot watch it. Mars meets
Jupiter at 25.9° Leo on November 16, both in that twelfth house: a lot of
fuel burning in rest, old business, and the part of your life with no
audience. The Sun squares Jupiter at 26.1° on November 18, your third house
against that hidden one, and what you want to say rubs against what you are
not ready to say.

The Sun enters Sagittarius and your home ground on November 22, and the full
moon at 2.3° Gemini on November 24 lights your tenth house of work and
public standing while the Sun sits at the base of the chart: house and
career at opposite ends of one night. The Sun opposes Uranus at 3.7° on
November 25, a degree and a half past where that moon stood. Mars enters
Virgo the same day, out of the hidden house and into your own first. The Sun
trines Saturn at 8.0° on November 30: a quiet, load-bearing end to a loud
month.
