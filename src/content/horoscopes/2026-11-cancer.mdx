Two retrogrades end within a day of each other this month, and for you they
end in the two rooms that matter most. Mercury stations direct at 5.0°
Scorpio on November 13, in your solar fifth house of romance, play and the
things you make. Venus stations direct at 22.9° Libra on November 14, in
your fourth house of home and family. Everything before those two dates is
the run-up.

The run-up is warm. Venus sextiles Jupiter at 24.7° on November 4, linking
that home ground to Jupiter in Leo and your second house of earnings and
what you value: the household and the money for it in easy contact. The new
moon at 16.9° Scorpio on November 9 falls in the fifth house four days ahead
of Mercury's turn in the same sign, which gives that creative start a second
pass built into the timing. Begin anyway.

November 16 is the loud one. Mars meets Jupiter at 25.9° Leo in your second
house, appetite and opportunity in the same degree, and this is the week the
money question gets loud. Two days later the Sun squares Jupiter at 26.1° on
November 18, the fifth house against the second: what you are making, costed
against what you are willing to spend on it. Say the number before the
enthusiasm does.

The Sun enters Sagittarius and your sixth house of work, health and routine
on November 22, and two days later the full moon at 2.3° Gemini lands in
your twelfth, the unaccounted one. November 24 is a rest reckoning arriving
exactly as the schedule tightens. Mars enters Virgo on November 25 and your
third house of errands and plain speech sharpens. Venus sextiles Jupiter
again on November 28, closing the loop it opened on November 4, and the Sun
trines Saturn at 8.0° on November 30, the sixth house meeting the tenth.
Nothing in November asks you to settle the whole thing. It asks what you are
willing to keep making once the retrogrades stop handing you a reason to
wait.
