Your season starts on December 21, when the Sun enters Capricorn and your
solar first house, and it starts into a headwind. The Sun squares Neptune
on December 23 and squares Saturn on December 29, both from your first
house against your fourth house of home and family. Two birthday-week
squares sounds worse than it is: the first blurs a picture you wanted
clear, the second states a limit you already suspected. Neither requires an
answer the same week.

Most of December happens behind you, which suits the end of a year.
Mercury enters Sagittarius on December 6 and your twelfth house, and the
new moon at 16.9° Sagittarius on December 9 falls there too. A quiet
beginning, out of sight: the rest you finally take, the thing you stop
telling people about, the help you ask for privately. Mercury trines
Neptune on December 7 and sextiles Pluto on December 8, so the private
version of a plan comes together before the public one.

Saturn, your traditional ruler, stations direct at 7.9° Aries on
December 10 in your fourth house, and Neptune stations direct at 1.6° Aries
on December 12 in the same house. The household question that has been
unanswerable since the spring becomes answerable. Mercury trines Saturn on
December 11, which is the day to raise it.

Venus enters Scorpio on December 4 and your eleventh house of friends and
groups, and stays: the month's warmth arrives through other people rather
than one person. Venus squares Pluto on December 9, when one friendship
meets a boundary. Mars squares Uranus on December 4 from Virgo and your
ninth house, so a travel or study plan may need rebooking.

Jupiter stations retrograde at 27.0° Leo on December 13 in your eighth
house. Mercury enters Capricorn on December 25, and the full moon at 2.2°
Cancer on December 24 closes something in your seventh house first.
