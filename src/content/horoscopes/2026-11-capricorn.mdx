Two retrogrades end within a day of each other this month, and both have
been sitting on your working life. Mercury stations direct at 5.0° Scorpio
on November 13, in your solar eleventh house of friends, colleagues and the
people who say your name in rooms you are not in. Venus stations direct at
22.9° Libra on November 14, at the top of your chart, where your reputation
lives. A station direct ends a review; it does not start something new. They
end in the two houses your working life runs through: who you know, and what
you are known for.

Before that, the early month is quieter than it looks. Venus sextiles
Jupiter on November 4, at 24.7° Libra and 24.7° Leo, linking that career
angle to your eighth house of shared money and what other people control. It
is the first of two passes, and Venus is still retrograde. The new moon at
16.9° Scorpio on November 9 resets the network house with Mercury still
backing up underneath it. That is a beginning dated inside an unfinished
review.

Mid month, the eighth house takes over. Mars meets Jupiter at 25.9° Leo on
November 16, and the Sun squares Jupiter from 26.1° Scorpio on November 18.
These are the two days the money that is not yours gets loud, and the square
comes in from your eleventh house.

The Sun enters Sagittarius on November 22 and your twelfth house, the quiet
lap before your own season. The full moon at 2.3° Gemini on November 24
fills your sixth house of work and health, and on November 25 the Sun at
3.7° Sagittarius opposes Uranus at 3.7° Gemini from your twelfth. Venus
sextiles Jupiter again on November 28, forward this time. November 30 closes
it: the Sun at 8.0° Sagittarius trines Saturn at 8.0° Aries, your ruler in
your fourth house of home, briefly in agreement with you.
