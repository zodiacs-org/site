Uranus sits in your sign all month, in your solar first house, and December
keeps knocking on it. Mars squares Uranus on December 4 from Virgo and your
fourth house of home: a household demand arrives against your own plans,
and it will not wait politely. Mercury, your ruler, opposes Uranus on
December 8, which is the month's most talkative day. Something you have been
circling gets said, possibly faster than you intended.

Mercury enters Sagittarius on December 6 and your seventh house of
partnership, where the new moon at 16.9° Sagittarius on December 9 also
lands. That is a genuine beginning in one close relationship: a
conversation restarted, terms reset, or a new person taken seriously. On
December 8 Mercury also sextiles Pluto, so the useful version of the
exchange is the one where you ask rather than announce.

Venus enters Scorpio on December 4 and your sixth house of work and health,
and stays. The month's practical improvements are small and physical: the
appointment, the better chair, the shift pattern that suits you. Venus
squares Pluto on December 9, when one of those changes meets someone else's
authority.

Saturn stations direct at 7.9° Aries on December 10 and Neptune at 1.6°
Aries on December 12, both in your eleventh house of friends and groups. A
stalled plan with other people starts moving. Mercury trines Saturn on
December 11, a good day for the practical message that makes it real.
Jupiter stations retrograde at 27.0° Leo on December 13 in your third house,
so writing, study and local business turn back for a second look.

The Sun enters Capricorn on December 21 and your eighth house, and the full
moon at 2.2° Cancer on December 24 fills your second house of earned money:
a figure becomes visible. Mercury enters Capricorn on December 25, then
squares Saturn on December 30, and the year ends on an unglamorous but
useful piece of arithmetic.
