Your ruler stations retrograde late in the month, so front-load anything
that needs saying cleanly. October opens with Mercury square Pluto on
October 2 (a conversation carrying more pressure than its subject deserves)
and with Mars in Leo, your solar third house of talk, errands and the
near neighbourhood, running three contacts in a week: trine Neptune on
October 2, opposite Pluto on October 3, sextile Uranus at 5.4° on October
7. That last one is the good one. Uranus is retrograde in your own sign,
and the sextile is the day an idea about yourself arrives usable rather
than disruptive.

The new moon at 17.4° Libra on October 10 opens your fifth house of
pleasure, romance and the things you make for their own sake. It is the
month's genuine beginning, and it wants play rather than output. The Sun
sextiles Jupiter at 22.0° on October 15, linking that fifth house to
Jupiter in Leo and your third: the pitch, the post, the sentence that
carries further than expected. On October 16 Pluto stations direct at 3.1°
Aquarius in your ninth house, and a long question about study, belief or
somewhere you want to live stops circling.

Then the month narrows. The Sun enters Scorpio on October 23 and your sixth
house of work and health; Mercury stations retrograde at 21.0° Scorpio on
October 24 in the same house. Expect the schedule to argue with itself for
a few weeks. That is not the week to add a commitment. Reread the last one
instead. Venus re-enters Libra on October 25 while retrograde, warming the
creative house again, and the full moon at 2.8° Taurus on October 26
completes something private in your twelfth. Rest is the correct answer to
that one.
