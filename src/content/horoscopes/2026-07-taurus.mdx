Money moves first this month. Mars and Uranus share Gemini, your solar
second house of income and what you own, and they meet on July 4. That can
look like a surprise expense, a sudden side offer, or a subscription you
cancel on principle. Quick money decisions that weekend are easier to live
with if they are reversible, and the irreversible ones can wait.

Venus, your ruler, enters Virgo and your fifth house of romance and
self-made pleasure on July 9, which sweetens the middle of the month: a
date, a studio afternoon, something you make because you want to. July 13
is worth watching, when Venus squares Uranus back in that money house and
an impulse purchase may look more convincing than it is. Something
inexpensive and enjoyable could be a better plan.

The new moon on July 14 lands in Cancer and your third house of errands,
siblings, and the neighborhood. It is a fresh start for a message, a
course, or a local commitment. Mercury has been retrograde in that same
house all month, though, and it stations direct on July 23. You might draft
the email and the agreement now, then send and sign after the 23rd.

The finish is domestic and bright. The Sun enters Leo and your fourth
house of home and family on July 22, then meets Jupiter there on July 29.
It is a good day for a housewarming, a decision about a place, or a family
table with everyone at it. The same day's full moon at 6° Aquarius lights
your tenth house of career, so something public may peak just as home news
arrives. If July 27's opposition between the Sun and Pluto puts a
demanding voice from work on the line, it is fine to let it ring. This
round can go to the house.
