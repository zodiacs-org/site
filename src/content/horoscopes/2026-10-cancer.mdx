Home is the month's centre of gravity. The new moon at 17.4° Libra on
October 10 falls in your solar fourth house of home, family and where you
are from. It is the cleanest reset of your year for the living situation, the
room, the family arrangement. Before it, on October 4, the Sun opposes
Saturn at 11.3°, that home angle against Saturn retrograde in your tenth
house of work and public standing. It is the old argument between what the
household needs and what the job takes. This year it is worth answering
rather than postponing.

Mars spends October in Leo and your second house of money and what you own,
which is unusually useful: trine Neptune on October 2, sextile Uranus on
October 7, and trine Saturn at 10.4° on October 16. That last day is when
earning and patience line up and a practical money move actually works.
Mars opposes Pluto on October 3, across to your eighth house of shared and
borrowed money, so early in the month one figure will feel like a power
question. It is arithmetic. Treat it that way. Pluto stations direct at
3.1° Aquarius on October 16 in that same eighth house, and something
contractual frozen since spring begins to move.

Venus is retrograde from October 3 at 8.5° Scorpio, in your fifth house:
an old affection, an old creative project, or a child's situation coming
back for another look. It returns to Libra and your home angle on October
25, still retrograde, which draws the two threads together. The Sun enters
Scorpio on October 23 and the fifth house warms; Mercury stations
retrograde there at 21.0° on October 24. The full moon at 2.8° Taurus on
October 26 culminates in your eleventh house of friends and groups: one
circle reaches its natural end or its clear commitment.
