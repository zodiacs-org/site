Venus enters your sign on December 4 and stays all month, in your solar
first house. People find you easier to approach, and you may find yourself
willing to be approached. It is the most straightforwardly pleasant transit
of your December, and it is worth using early rather than saving.

The same day is less smooth elsewhere. Mercury squares Jupiter on
December 4 from the last degrees of your sign against your tenth house of
career, and something you say about work may overreach slightly. Venus
squares Pluto on December 9 from your first house against your fourth house
of home, so the new warmth meets an old family pattern. Naming the pattern
is usually enough; fixing it this month is not required.

Money is the month's practical subject. The new moon at 16.9° Sagittarius
on December 9 falls in your second house of earned money, which is a clean
place to begin: the rate raised, the account opened, the work quoted
properly. Mercury entered that house on December 6, trines Neptune on
December 7, then opposes Uranus and sextiles Pluto on December 8, so the
numbers get talked through in the days before the new moon rather than
after it.

Saturn stations direct at 7.9° Aries on December 10 and Neptune at 1.6°
Aries on December 12, both in your sixth house of daily work and routines. A
workflow, a routine or a job process that has been stalled since the
spring starts to move. Mercury trines Saturn on December 11, a clean day for
the email or calendar block that arranges it.

Jupiter stations retrograde at 27.0° Leo on December 13 in your tenth
house, so a career expansion pauses for review. The Sun enters Capricorn on
December 21 and your third house, and the full moon at 2.2° Cancer on
December 24 finishes something in your ninth house of travel and belief.
