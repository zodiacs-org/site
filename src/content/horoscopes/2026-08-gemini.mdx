August hands you the microphone and asks you to use it carefully. Leo is
your solar third house of messages, siblings, errands, and the near
neighborhood. Mercury, your planet, enters Leo on August 9, and by
Wednesday, August 12 it is opposite Pluto, trine Neptune, and sextile Uranus
in Gemini. A sentence can have reach, and it can also have consequences.
The true thing can be said without making it harder than truth requires.

That same day, the new moon, a total solar eclipse, lands at 20° Leo. It is
a natural start for a writing project, local commitment, conversation,
class, or pitch. Mercury meets Jupiter on August 15 in the same house, a
good day for words that need to travel: the proposal, the announcement, the
application, the message you want forwarded.

Money and workload ask for cleaner edges. Venus enters Libra on August 6,
your fifth house, and August 10 can feel both magnetic and confusing as
Venus trines Pluto and opposes Neptune. You can enjoy the invitation without
turning it into a life plan by midnight. Mars enters Cancer on August 11,
your second house, then squares Neptune on August 17. A cost may be blurry,
or a desire may be dressed up as necessity. Getting the number in writing
helps.

August 17 also brings Mercury trine Saturn and Venus sextile Jupiter, so the
day that muddles one corner may steady another. The version with a receipt
and a calendar tends to hold. The Sun enters Virgo on August 23 and Mercury
follows on August 25, moving attention to home and family. The August 28
full moon, a deep partial lunar eclipse at 4.9° Pisces, lights your career
house while Mercury and the Sun both square Uranus in your sign. A public
matter may come to a head, and your private life may not stay quiet while
it does.
