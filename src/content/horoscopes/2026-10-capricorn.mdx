Your career angle carries the Sun for three weeks and the year's clearest
professional reset. The new moon at 17.4° Libra on October 10 opens your
solar tenth house: a new role, a new title, or the same job restated on
different terms. Start it deliberately. On October 4 the Sun opposed Saturn
at 11.3°, your ruler retrograde in your fourth house of home, so the
month's underlying tension is the honest one: what the work demands against
what the household is owed. Saturn retrograde means the answer is a
renegotiation, not a resignation.

Mars spends October in Leo and your eighth house of shared money, debt and
other people's resources. It opposes Pluto on October 3 (one financial
arrangement shows its teeth) and trines Saturn at 10.4° on October 16,
which is the day to restructure rather than argue. Pluto stations direct at
3.1° Aquarius the same day in your second house of income, and money stuck
since spring begins to move. Mid-month is where your leverage is: on
October 15 the Sun sextiles Jupiter at 22.0° in that eighth house, and
someone with resources says yes.

Venus stationed retrograde at 8.5° Scorpio on October 3 in your eleventh
house of friends and networks, so a professional friendship is under quiet
review all month; it returns to your career house on October 25, still
retrograde, which brings the review to the surface. The Sun enters Scorpio
on October 23, Mercury stations retrograde at 21.0° Scorpio on October 24,
and group logistics get slower. The full moon at 2.8° Taurus on October 26
culminates in your fifth house of pleasure and the things you make: a
personal project reaches its finish, with the Sun square Pluto the same
day. Take the finish.
