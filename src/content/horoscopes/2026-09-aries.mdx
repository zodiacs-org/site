September starts with a wall worth pushing against. Mars squares Saturn on
September 1: your ruler in Cancer, your solar fourth house of home and
family, grinds against Saturn retrograde in your own sign. The household
task is real, and so may be your shortage of patience for it. Doing the
thing slowly and once tends to work better here, since the square favors
method over force.

The Sun spends the first three weeks in Virgo, your sixth house of work,
health and routines, and the new moon at 18.4° Virgo on September 11 is a
clean reset for that territory: the schedule, the checkup, the habit you
keep meaning to start. The day before, on September 10, three things move
at once. Mercury enters Libra and your partnership house, Venus enters
Scorpio and your house of shared money, and Uranus stations retrograde at
5.7° Gemini, sending your calendar of errands and plans into review. One
commitment may need to be reshuffled.

The partnership conversation may matter most. Mercury opposes Saturn on
September 18, and it is a day when someone's limit can be said out loud.
It is worth hearing as information rather than a verdict. Mercury then
sextiles Jupiter in your fifth house on September 21, when the same
dialogue can turn more generous. Venus square Pluto on September 15
touches money: a joint expense and a friend's expectation may pull in
different directions, and naming the number can ease it.

The Sun enters Libra on September 23 and relationship season begins in
earnest. The full moon at 3.6° Aries on September 26 is in your sign, and
a personal decision may reach a visible end. The Sun opposes Neptune that
same day, so anything blurry can stay unsigned for a little while. The
Sun's trine to Pluto that day suggests the deeper change is sturdy. On
September 28 Mars enters Leo and your fifth house of pleasure and
self-made things, and the Sun trines Uranus. The month that began with a
wall may end with a door open.
