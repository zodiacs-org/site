Almost everything happens in your sign this month, and most of it moves
backwards. Venus stations retrograde at 8.5° Scorpio on October 3, in your
solar first house: six weeks of reconsidering how you look, what you want
and who you want it from. Mercury squared Pluto from your sign on October 2
and stations retrograde at 21.0° Scorpio on October 24. The Sun arrives on
October 23. So the last ten days put the Sun, a retrograde Mercury and your
birthday season in the same place: loud, personal, and not the week for a
final decision.

The first three weeks are quieter than that sounds. The Sun spends them in
Libra and your twelfth house, which is the year's rest window whether or
not you take it. The new moon at 17.4° Libra on October 10 is a private
beginning: something started alone, unannounced, and better for it. On
October 15 the Sun sextiles Jupiter at 22.0° in your tenth house of career:
a good word said about you in a room you are not in. On October 16 Mars
in Leo trines Saturn at 10.4° and Pluto stations direct at 3.1° Aquarius in
your fourth house of home and family, where a long-stuck domestic matter
finally gives.

Two things then land together on October 26. The full moon at 2.8° Taurus
culminates in your seventh house (a partnership reaches its visible peak
or its honest end) and the Sun squares Pluto from your sign to that home
angle. Between Venus retrograde in your first house and Mercury retrograde
behind it, the instruction for the last week is narrow: gather the facts,
say less than you want to, and let November decide. Venus leaves for Libra
on October 25, which takes some of the heat out of it.
