The public part of your month comes first. The Sun crosses Cancer, your
solar tenth house of career and reputation, until July 22, and the new
moon there on July 14 suits a professional reset: an application, a
conversation about your title, a portfolio finally reorganized. One timing
note: Mercury is retrograde in that same house until July 23, so you might
write things on the 14th and submit them after the 23rd.

July 6 names the month's central tension. The Sun squares Saturn in Aries,
your seventh house, with the job pulling one way and the partnership the
other, and both have a point. Saturn has been asking your closest
relationship to mature all year. It stations retrograde on July 26, and the
agreements you made in spring may come up for review through the fall.
Reading them over together may work better than doing it separately.

Two smaller currents are worth using. Mars and Uranus meet in Gemini, your
ninth house, on July 4. If a trip suggests itself out of nowhere, it may be
worth a look. Venus, your ruler, slips into Virgo and your twelfth house
on July 9, which favors scheduling some real solitude, because the social
battery is not infinite.

You may want it charged. The Sun enters Leo and your eleventh house of
friends on July 22, then meets Jupiter there on July 29, a good social day
and a likely setting for a useful introduction. The same day's full moon at
6° Aquarius lights your fifth house of romance and creative work, so
something you love may come into full view in front of people who
appreciate it. The 29th could be a good night to go out and stay late.
