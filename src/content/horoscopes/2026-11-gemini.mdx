The hinge of this month is two days wide and sits right in the middle.
Before it, the month is mostly setup. Venus sextiles Jupiter at 24.7° on
November 4, linking Venus in Libra, your solar fifth house of pleasure and
the things you make, to Jupiter in Leo and your third, where you talk, write
and run errands. The new moon at 16.9° Scorpio on November 9 opens the
sixth: work, health, the shape of an ordinary day. It is a clean reset,
arriving while the traffic is still stopped.

Then the hinge arrives. Mercury, your ruler, stations direct at 5.0° Scorpio
on November 13, in that same sixth house, and the rereading ends. Venus
stations direct at 22.9° Libra the next day, November 14. Two retrogrades
finishing on consecutive days is the month's real story: both planets stand
still before they turn, and the handbrake comes off the work question and
the creative one at once. The question they make loud is whether the
revising is finished or has become the work.

Mars meets Jupiter at 25.9° Leo on November 16, both in the third house,
Mercury's own territory and so familiar ground. That conjunction is loud
wherever it falls, and here it falls on talk. On November 18 the Sun squares
Jupiter at 26.1°, sixth house against third: the friction between how much
you are saying and how much of the day is already spoken for.

The Sun enters Sagittarius and your seventh house of partnership on November
22, but the last week is mostly about you. The full moon at 2.3° Gemini on
November 24 is in your own sign, with the Sun trine Neptune the same day:
something about how you come across reaching its visible end, gently. On
November 25 the Sun in Sagittarius opposes Uranus at 3.7° Gemini and asks it
again, less gently, while Mars enters Virgo and your fourth house of home.
Uranus trines Pluto on November 29. Whatever surfaced, let it stay up.
