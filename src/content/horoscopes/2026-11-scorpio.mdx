Two retrogrades end within a day of each other this month, Mercury on
November 13 and Venus on November 14, and the first of them ends in your
sign. Venus sextiles Jupiter at 24.7° on November 4 ahead of that, still
retrograde in Libra and your solar twelfth house of rest and private
matters. The new moon at 16.9° Scorpio on November 9 then lands in your
first house, the one new moon all year that is simply about you.

Mercury stations direct at 5.0° Scorpio on November 13, after three weeks of
walking backwards through it. Venus follows at 22.9° Libra on November 14,
so that retrograde ends out of sight, in the twelfth, after beginning in
your own sign in early October. Most of the reconsidering happened in plain
view. November 16 is the loud one: Mars meets Jupiter at 25.9° Leo in your
tenth house of career. The Sun squares that Jupiter from 26.1° Scorpio on
November 18, the week the gap between what you want and what the work wants
is easiest to measure.

The Sun enters Sagittarius on November 22 and your second house of income
and worth takes over. On November 24 the full moon at 2.3° Gemini fills the
eighth, the far end of that axis: joint accounts, debts, what is owed both
ways. The Sun trines Neptune at 1.7° the same day, tying the money question
to work and health in your sixth.

November 25 stacks three: the Sun sextiles Pluto at 3.5°, reaching your
fourth house of home, opposes Uranus at 3.7° back across that money axis,
and Mars leaves for Virgo and your eleventh house of friends. Venus repeats
that sextile to Jupiter on November 28, forward this time. The Sun trines
Saturn at 8.0° on November 30, income and daily work pointed the same way.
Mercury's rereading ended on November 13. What the last week asks for is
arithmetic.
