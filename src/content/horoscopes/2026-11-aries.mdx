Two reversals end a day apart this month, and most of November arranges
itself around them. Mercury stations direct at 5.0° Scorpio on November 13,
in your solar eighth house of shared money and terms agreed to in a hurry.
Venus stations direct at 22.9° Libra on November 14, in the partnership
house next door. Two adjacent rooms, both under review, both let go.

The approach is gentler than the arrival. Venus sextiles Jupiter at 24.7° on
November 4, Libra to Leo, your seventh house in easy contact with your
fifth, where pleasure and the things you make live. Then the new moon at
16.9° Scorpio on November 9 restarts the shared account four days before
Mercury turns. The reset lands before the planet of terms moves, so this is
the fortnight the joint money question gets loud rather than settled.

November 16 is the loud one. Mars, your ruler, meets Jupiter at 25.9° Leo,
both in your fifth house: appetite and scale at the same degree, pointed at
whatever you do for the pleasure of doing it. On November 18 the Sun squares
Jupiter at 26.1°, Scorpio against Leo, the eighth house asking the fifth
what all this costs. Hear the question out before you answer it.

Then the month opens outward. The Sun enters Sagittarius on November 22 and
your ninth house of distance and study, and trines Neptune in your own sign
at 1.7° on November 24, the same day the full moon at 2.3° Gemini fills your
third house with every local, half written thing. November 25 is crowded:
Sun sextile Pluto at 3.5°, Sun opposite Uranus at 3.7°, and Mars into Virgo
and your sixth house, where the Leo blaze becomes a list. On November 30 the
Sun trines Saturn at 8.0°, Saturn still in Aries. The question it has put to
you all year, what you can actually sustain, gets asked gently for once.
