December moves two long-stuck things in your own sign. Saturn stations
direct at 7.9° Aries on December 10, and Neptune stations direct at 1.6°
Aries on December 12, both in your solar first house of self and how you
start. Whatever has felt like your own slow year, the thing you could
neither finish nor drop, begins to face forward again. It will not resolve
in a week, but the direction changes.

Mercury trines Saturn on December 11 from Sagittarius, your ninth house of
travel, study and the long view, which makes the days around that station
unusually good for saying the plan out loud. Mercury trined Neptune on
December 7 from the same house, so the softer version of that conversation
may have come first.

December 4 is busy and a little abrasive. Mars, your ruler, squares Uranus
from Virgo, your sixth house of work and routines, against your third
house of errands and messages, and the schedule may simply break. Mercury
squares Jupiter the same day. Venus enters Scorpio and your eighth house of
shared money, where it stays all month, and the question of who pays for
what grows both warmer and more pointed. Venus squares Pluto on December 9,
when a joint number may need naming rather than managing.

The new moon at 16.9° Sagittarius on December 9 is a clean start for study,
travel and belief. Jupiter stations retrograde at 27.0° Leo on December 13
in your fifth house, so a creative or romantic thread turns inward for
review. The Sun trines Jupiter on December 18, the most generous day of the
month.

The Sun enters Capricorn on December 21 and your tenth house of career and
public standing takes over. The full moon at 2.2° Cancer on December 24
sits opposite it in your fourth house, and the pull between work and home
is the shape of your holiday. The Sun squares Saturn on December 29: the
year ends with a limit stated plainly, in your own sign.
