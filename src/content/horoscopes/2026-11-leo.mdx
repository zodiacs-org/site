The large fact in your sky is Jupiter in Leo, which is your solar first
house: your face, your appetite, the room you take up. Venus sextiles it
from Libra on November 4 at 24.7°, out of the third house of talking and
writing: a day when phrasing matters more than usual. The new moon at 16.9°
Scorpio on November 9 is another register, fourth house, home and
foundations, a beginning you make from underneath.

Then two planets turn around on consecutive days. Mercury stations direct at
5.0° Scorpio on November 13, in that same domestic ground, and Venus does
the same at 22.9° Libra on November 14, in the house of how you say things.
Both had been retrograde: the rereading and the restating that goes with
that stops, which is the month's largest event and its quietest. On November
16 Mars meets Jupiter at 25.9° Leo, both in the first house, a concentrated
shot of personal fuel about as subtle as it sounds. On November 18 the Sun
squares Jupiter from 26.1° Scorpio, home pulling against the version of you
that just caught fire.

The Sun enters Sagittarius on November 22 and the fifth house, familiar
ground for a Leo: play, making things, performing because you like to. It
trines Neptune at 1.7° on November 24 and the edges soften; the full moon
that same day at 2.3° Gemini fills the eleventh house of friends. On
November 25 the Sun opposes Uranus at 3.7°, setting what you make alone
opposite the crowd it belongs to, and sextiles Pluto at 3.5° Aquarius, which
puts a partner in the frame. Mars leaves your sign for Virgo the same day
and the heat relocates to earning.

Venus sextiles Jupiter again on November 28 at 26.7°, the November 4
conversation without the retrograde, and Uranus trines Pluto at 3.5° on
November 29. The Sun trines Saturn at 8.0° on November 30, fifth house to
ninth: what you made for pleasure, set against what you actually believe.
Worth asking what the fuel was for.
