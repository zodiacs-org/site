August starts by asking what you are willing to take seriously because you
enjoy it. Leo is your solar fifth house of pleasure, romance, and self-made
work, and the Sun trines Saturn in Aries on August 7. That is less glamour
than practice: the date that deserves planning, the creative thing that
needs a schedule, the child or project that wants your steadier attention.

The middle of the month is louder there. Mercury enters Leo on August 9 and
meets a stack of aspects on Wednesday, August 12: opposition Pluto, trine
Neptune, sextile Uranus. That same day brings the new moon, a total solar
eclipse, at 20° Leo. A simple announcement may serve you best, since the
audience, the dream, and the surprise variable are all in the room. Mercury
meets Jupiter on August 15, and Venus sextiles Jupiter on August 17. If you
have a pitch, an invitation, or an opening night in mind, the 15th to the
17th is a friendly stretch to consider.

Partnership gets its own chapter once Venus enters Libra on August 6. The
Venus trine to Pluto and opposition to Neptune on August 10 can make a
relationship feel both fated and difficult to read. The plain question
usually helps. By August 21, when Venus opposes Saturn in your sign, any
answer has to live inside time, limits, and actual behavior.

Home asks for care rather than drama. Mars enters Cancer on August 11 and
squares Neptune on August 17, which can fog a family conversation or
household plan. The righteous speech can wait until you know what happened.
The Sun enters Virgo on August 23, Mercury follows on August 25, and the
August 28 full moon, a deep partial lunar eclipse at 4.9° Pisces, lands in
your twelfth house. Rest is not a moral failure. That same day, Mercury and
the Sun both square Uranus, and the calendar may shift. You can let it.
