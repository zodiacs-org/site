Venus, your ruler, enters Scorpio on December 4 and spends the month in
your solar second house of earned money and what you value. This is a
practical placement rather than a romantic one: rates, invoices, the price
of a thing you want. Venus squares Pluto on December 9, and that is the day
a figure gets contested. Holding your number tends to work better here than
splitting it early.

Your partnership house does something more interesting. Saturn stations
direct at 7.9° Aries on December 10 and Neptune stations direct at 1.6°
Aries on December 12, both in your seventh house. A relationship that has
been stuck, or deliberately unclear, begins to face forward. For a sign
that lives in this house, two slow planets turning at once is the month's
real news. Mercury trines Saturn on December 11, when the plain version can
be said.

Mars squares Uranus on December 4 from Virgo and your twelfth house against
your ninth: a tiredness you have not admitted meets a plan you have not
booked. Mercury enters Sagittarius on December 6 and your third house, then
opposes Uranus on December 8, so the week's messages are quick and a little
scattered. Mercury sextiles Pluto the same day, which is the useful half.

The new moon at 16.9° Sagittarius on December 9 begins something small and
local: a course, a short trip, a piece of writing. Jupiter stations
retrograde at 27.0° Leo on December 13 in your eleventh house, turning a
group plan back for review. The Sun trines Jupiter on December 18, a good
day among friends.

The Sun enters Capricorn on December 21 and your fourth house of home, and
the full moon at 2.2° Cancer on December 24 completes something in your
tenth house of career. The Sun squares Saturn on December 29, which puts
one partnership limit on the record before the year closes.
