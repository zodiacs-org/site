Money is the visible thread and health is the hidden one. The new moon at
17.4° Libra on October 10 opens your solar second house of income,
possessions and what you are worth. It is the clearest financial restart of your
year, and one worth making a plan around rather than a wish. It follows the
Sun's opposition to Saturn at 11.3° on October 4, that money house against
Saturn retrograde in your eighth: your resources against a shared
obligation, stated plainly by someone else. Attend to the number, not the
tone.

Your ruler has a complicated month. Mercury squares Pluto on October 2 and
stations retrograde at 21.0° Scorpio on October 24, both in your third
house of talk, errands and the local round. Between those dates, say the
important things. After October 24, repeat them. Venus is retrograde from
October 3 at 8.5° Scorpio in that same third house, which doubles the
theme: old messages, old routes, an old neighbour or sibling thread
reopening. Venus returns to Libra and your money house on October 25, still
retrograde, so a price you agreed earlier comes back for review.

Two days carry the month's better weather. On October 15 the Sun sextiles
Jupiter at 22.0°, connecting your income to Jupiter in Leo and your twelfth
house: help arrives from somewhere unadvertised, and you should accept it.
On October 16 Mars in Leo trines Saturn at 10.4° while Pluto stations
direct at 3.1° Aquarius in your sixth house of work and health: a body or
work-shaped problem immovable since spring starts to shift, and you have
the stamina that day to act on it. The full moon at 2.8° Taurus on October
26 completes something in your ninth house: a course, a trip, a conviction
reaching its end.
