The new moon at 16.9° Sagittarius on December 9 is yours, in your solar
first house, and it is the cleanest start you get all year. Birthday-season
new moons are useful precisely because nothing has happened yet: the
decision you make in the first week of December is the one the next twelve
months are built on. Make it small enough to keep.

Mercury enters your sign on December 6 and the days around it are loud.
Mercury trines Neptune on December 7, opposes Uranus on December 8, and
sextiles Pluto on December 8 as well. You will have more to say than usual
and more people answering back. The trine and the sextile are the halves
worth acting on; the opposition is the half worth sleeping on.

Mars squares Uranus on December 4 from Virgo and your tenth house of career
against your seventh house of partnership, which is a brisk collision
between what work wants and what one person close to you wants. Venus
enters Scorpio on December 4 and your twelfth house, where it stays, so
some of this month's affection is private and stays that way.

Saturn stations direct at 7.9° Aries on December 10 and Neptune at 1.6°
Aries on December 12, both in your fifth house of play and self-made
things. A creative project that has been going nowhere since the spring
starts to move again. Mercury trines Saturn on December 11 and is the day
to write the plan down.

Jupiter, your ruler, stations retrograde at 27.0° Leo on December 13 in
your ninth house, and the Sun trines it on December 18: a strong day for
travel, study or a long argument made well. The Sun enters Capricorn on
December 21 and your second house, the full moon at 2.2° Cancer on
December 24 completes something in your eighth, and Mercury squares Saturn
on December 30 with one correction.
