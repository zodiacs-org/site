Venus, your ruler, enters Scorpio on December 4 and spends the rest of the
month in your solar seventh house of partnership. For four weeks the person
across from you is the subject, by their choice or yours. Venus squares
Pluto on December 9, and the first real test arrives: someone wants a
decision made rather than deferred, and the honest answer is likely to be
better received than the diplomatic one.

Money held jointly is the other thread. The new moon at 16.9° Sagittarius
on December 9 falls in your eighth house of shared accounts, debts and what
belongs to both of you, which is a sensible place to start something: a
payment plan, a conversation about a balance, a document finally read. It
is a quiet new moon rather than a dramatic one.

Mercury enters Sagittarius on December 6 and joins that house, then trines
Neptune on December 7 and opposes Uranus on December 8. The opposition can
unsettle a plan made with someone else; it rarely cancels it. Mercury
sextiles Pluto on December 8 as well, and the same day can produce a useful
piece of information if you ask a direct question.

Saturn stations direct at 7.9° Aries on December 10 and Neptune stations
direct at 1.6° Aries on December 12, both in your twelfth house of quiet
time and what goes on out of sight. A private project, or a quiet habit
held back since spring, finds its footing again. Jupiter stations retrograde at 27.0° Leo on December 13 in
your fourth house, turning attention back toward home and family.

The Sun enters Capricorn on December 21, lighting your ninth house, and the
full moon at 2.2° Cancer on December 24 completes something in your third
house of errands, messages and local life: a small matter closing rather
than a large one. The Sun squares Saturn on December 29, which asks for one
honest estimate of what next year can hold.
