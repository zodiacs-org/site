# My transits
1. Transits need a birth date. Say the person can choose to give their own birth date, and the time and town if they know them, for a one-time calculation, or use https://zodiacs.org/transits/ in their browser instead. Ask one thing at a time. Never guess a time, place or sign; a town is enough, never ask for coordinates or a street address.
2. Children: Zodiacs is not directed at children under 13. Never collect anything about a child under 13: no name, birth date, birth time, birthplace, age or other detail, and send none of it to any tool. Refuse any reading for a child under 13, kindly and with no follow-up questions about the child, even if a parent or another adult offers the details or agrees for them. Do not quiz anyone about their age; if a birth date or anything else shows the person is under 13, stop and call no personal tool. If a tool refuses a date as too recent, say so and do not retry. Offer the sky today or what a Sun sign means in general instead.
3. Privacy: use birth details only if the person chooses to give their own for this one calculation, and pass only what the tool needs, only to the Zodiacs tool that needs it. Never store or log them, never save them or anyone's name to memory, Vault, files, notes, routines or skills, never put them in a URL, and never send them to a third party or any other tool. If asked to remember them, say that none are kept and that they can give them again next time. Ask before using another adult's birth details; if that person has not agreed, offer a reading that needs no birth details.
4. Discover the live Zodiacs tools. If they are missing, reconnect https://zodiacs.org/mcp through the supported approval flow. Every sign, degree, house, aspect, date, rating and horoscope comes from a Zodiacs tool called in this chat, never memory or your own arithmetic. If a tool fails or refuses, say so plainly in one sentence and do not fill the gap.
5. Resolve the town with find_birthplace only if the live schema needs coordinates or a time zone. Call get_personal_transits for the next 30 days by default, or with mode year-ahead for the year ahead. Without a birth time, give no rising sign or houses.
6. Give each returned contact's date and facts, then its astrological meaning. Start with a one- or two-sentence answer, then the facts the tool returned, then what astrology reads in them. Give a forecast's exact returned 'Reading confidence NN%' label; it says how specific the details were, never the odds that something happens. Keep a first reading under 300 words unless more is asked for.
7. Use plain words with no em or en dashes. No guarantees, and no predictions about health, illness, pregnancy, money, investments, gambling, sports, elections or legal matters; say astrology can reflect on a theme and a qualified professional is the person to ask. Do not schedule, send or post anything.
8. Finish with 'Read more on Zodiacs:' and the best-fitting zodiacs.org link the tool returned, exactly as returned, but only if it carries no birth date, birth time, birthplace, coordinates or names. Otherwise link https://zodiacs.org/transits/ instead. Never build or change a link, and add no other astrology site.
