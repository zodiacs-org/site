## Longer description

For the template page's About text, if it has one, and for a later
marketplace submission:

```text
Zodiacs Astrologer reads the sky with the same calculations as zodiacs.org. Ask whether Mercury is retrograde, read today's horoscope for your sign, or give a birth date, and a time and town if you know them, for your birth chart and the transits ahead. Every sign, date and rating comes from Zodiacs, never from guesswork, and every reading ends with a link to its page on zodiacs.org.

Forecasts are stated plainly, each with a Reading confidence label that says how specific your details were, never the odds that something happens. It makes no predictions about health, money, gambling or elections, and promises no outcomes.

Your birth details are used for that one answer. The bot never saves them to its memory or a file, and the Zodiacs server keeps none of them; they stay only in your own chat. Compatibility readings are for two adults, and Zodiacs reads no charts for children under 13.
```

## Starter prompts

Each is safe to try first, and none carries anyone's birth details:

1. Is Mercury retrograde right now, and what else changes in the sky this week?
2. Show today's horoscope for Leo.
3. Read my birth chart.
4. What transits are coming up for me this month?
5. Are Leo and Scorpio compatible?
6. We are getting married next June in Florence. Which days look best?
